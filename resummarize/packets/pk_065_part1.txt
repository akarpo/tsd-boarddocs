2. RECOGNITION 
A. TSD Mission Moment – Leonard Elementary School “Peer to 
Peer Mentoring” 
Last year, Lisa Carruthers, teacher at Leonard Elementary School,  worked 
tirelessly to implement the “Peer to Peer Mediation” program into her third 
grade classroom.  Peer to Peer Mediation is an evidence -based practice 
that positively impacts students with Autism Spectrum Disorders as well as 
other students with disabi lities. In essence, the Peer to Peer program 
develops peer relationships.  Students learn about autism and how it 
affects other students in their classroom.  Mrs. Carruthers attended 
seminars during the summer months and met with other teachers that 
had implemented the program.  Mrs. Carruthers is currently busy sharing 
her knowledge with other teachers at Leonard and around the Troy School 
District. 
Lisa Car ruthers will be recognized and asked to come  up to receive a 
certificate.

B.   NUMATS Outstanding Educator Award 
Each year,  Northwestern University’s Midwest Academic Talent Search 
recognizes educators from across the country for their e nthusiastic 
participation in a special program  which provides gifted students the 
opportunity to take assessments designed for older students. NUMATS, as it 
is called, is used to determine placement in high school courses, and the top 
scoring students are honored in a special ceremony . NUMATS also 
recognizes the important role that educators play in the support and 
implementation of the program. In 2014, only 18 educators from the United 
States were singled out for extraordinary recognition . We are very pleased 
tonight to acknowledge  Tracey Kinney, TSD  Assessment Coordinator, for 
receiving this prestigious recognition.  Congratulations, Tracey!
