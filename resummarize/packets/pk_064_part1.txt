C.  Superintendent’s Student Advisory Board 
 
The Superintendent’s Student Advisory Board is a new group  that was instituted 
this year for the purpose of providing action-oriented leadership opportunities to 
students at the high school level while also serving as a sounding -board to the 
Superintendent and Central Office administration.  Students will serve on this 
board throughout their high school caree r – graduating seniors will be replaced 
by a new group of freshmen at the beginning of each school year.  We received 
a total of 55 applications submitted by  outstanding students from Athens, 
International Academy-East, Niles and Troy High Schools. Out of these 
applications, 24 students were selected; 6 seniors, 7 juniors, 7 sophomores and 
4 freshmen.   Congratulations to the  following students who have been asked to

Mr.  Witt,  Assistant  Superintendent,  Human  Resources,  shared  a 
brief  update  on  teacher  staffing  for 
the  2014/2015  school  year.  
He reviewed the primary factors which influenced the number of 
teachers hired, which included retirements/resignations, full‐year 
leaves of absence, FTE status, and student enrollment.  He shared 
an overview of the interview selection process and indicated that 
44  new  teachers  were  hired  this  year.   He  added  that  eight  of 
them  are  TSD  graduates  and  that  27  have  an  educational 
background  beyond a Bachelor’s Degree.   He also indicated  that 
the hiring process was almost complete and that only a couple of 
positions needed to be filled.   
 
Dr. Philippart inquired about the hiring process and if it is possible 
for one or two individuals to make independent hiring decisions.  
Mr. Witt provided a background of how the current hiring process 
was  developed  and  indicated  that  a  group  of  3  or  4  principals
