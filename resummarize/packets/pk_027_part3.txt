B.   REPORT:  Summer Success Academy  
This past summer the Troy School District provided an extended 
learning opportunity for 49 students that would be entering 9th grade 
for the 2011-2012 school year.  The purpose of the Summer Success 
Academy was to provide support for students making the transition 
from Baker, Boulan Park, Larson and Smith Middle Schools to Athens 
and Troy High Schools.  This is the first time the Troy School District 
offered a program of this kind, which offered students an opportunity 
to strengthen math, English and study skills prior to the vital 
transition to high school.  The goal of the Summer Success Academy 
was to reduce the number of studen ts failing one or more courses 
during their 9 th grade year and to jump-s tart their success in high 
school through intensive grade level instruction.
