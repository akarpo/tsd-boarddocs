MEETINGS 
All meetings, except closed sessions, are open to the public.  The Board 
welcomes citizens’ comments.  The public participation portion of the 
meeting is limited to one-half hour, but exceptions may be made at the 
discretion of the Board president.   Each person will be allowed to 
speak for up to five minutes, except where the number of speakers 
exceeds the time limit.  In those instances, the President may reduce 
the five-minute limit to a three-minute limit for each speaker  or the 
president of the Board may waive the half –hour time limit.  In any 
event, no one’s right to address the Board will be denied.  
Each person wishing to address the Board of Education must identify 
himself or herself by name and address.  If the person is representing 
an organization or group, the person should indicate whether the 
comments or presentation represents the official view of the 
organization or group. 
Board of Education members may question speakers but are not 
obligated to answer questions or make statements or commitm ents in 
response to issues raised by the public.  In general, such issues will be 
referred to the Superintendent for investigation, study, and 
recommendation or designated as future agenda items for Board of 
Education consideration.  
Individuals addressing the Board should take into consideration the 
rules of common courtesy.  The public participation portion of the 
meeting cannot be used to make personal attacks against any 
individual school board member or school district employee that are 
totally unrelated to the manner in which the member or employee 
performs his or her duties. 
To ensure individual due process rights, the District maintains a 
formal process for handling complaints against individuals.  A 
problem involving an individual or specific incid ent is handled 
through administrative channels.  For assistance, contact the 
Superintendent’s office. 
The president or designee is responsible for the orderly conduct of the 
meeting and shall rule on such matters as to the appropriateness of the 
subject being presented.

C-1402 SMOKING 
The policy of the Troy School District is to follow Public Act 140 of 
1993 regarding smoking and use of tobacco products on school 
district property, with the exception that smoking is prohibited at 
athletic fields. 
It is the policy of the Troy School District that the use and/or sale of 
any tobacco products is prohibited in vehicles, buildings, facilities, 
structures, or other real estate owned, leased or otherwise 
controlled by the Troy School Distric t at all times (24 hours a day – 
7 days a week.) 
For the purpose of this policy, the use and/or sale of a tobacco 
product is defined as follows: 
“Tobacco product” means a preparation of tobacco to be inhaled, 
chewed, or placed in a person’s mouth.  
“Use of a tobacco product” means any of the following:  
 The carrying by a person of a lighted cigar, cigarette, pipe, or 
 other lighted smoking device.  
 The inhaling or chewing of a tobacco product.  
 The placing of a tobacco product within a person’s mouth.

ADVERTISING & DISTRIBUTION OF MATERIALS IN THE SCHOOLS  
K-1300  
K-1304 SERVICE ADVERTISING 
 Advertising of a service to employees or service to students may be allowed by 
obtaining approval of the superintendent or designee. 
 ADVERTISING ON SCHOOL DISTRICT PROPERTY AND IN SCHOOL 
DISTRICT ELECTRONIC MEDIA  
The school district property and electronic media, like school newspapers, 
yearbooks, and other District-sponsored publications and property, is a nonpublic 
forum subject to the District’s control and regulation.  The District will accept 
commercial advertisements, subject to the following rules and procedures. 
 Advertising is restricted to commercial purposes.  (The District reserves the 
right to give preference to local businesses and businesses that sell goods and 
services that students may be interested in purchasing.)  The District property 
and Web site are not forums for political, religious, or public advocacy 
discussion or expression. 
 The District reserves the right to deny advertisements that are ungrammatical, 
poorly written, inadequately researched, biased or prejudiced, vulgar or 
profane or otherwise unsuitable for student audiences. 
 Advertisements cannot be for goods or services which are illegal. 
 The sponsors, goods, services, advertisement or activities may not violate or 
condone (explicitly or implicitly) the violation of Board or School District’s 
policies, or be inconsistent with or in competition with the educational mission 
of the School District. 
 The advertisements cannot be libelous, offensive, contain explicit sexual 
content, or be obscene. 
 The District will not approve advertising in connection with the following 
products or uses: 
1. Products used to injure or kill. 
2. Alcohol-related products. 
3. Tobacco-related products. 
4. Illegal drug-related products. 
128
5. Prescription or over-the-counter medication. 
6. Sexually suggestive products or language. 
7. Gambling-related products. 
8. Products that present an unacceptable risk of liability. 
9. Products that are harmful to or inconsistent with the mission or image of 
the School District. 
10. Racist, sexist, hateful, demeaning or degrading language or statements. 
11. Profanity. 
12. Sexual acts. 
13. Statements impugning any school in the Troy School District or schools 
outside of the Troy School District. 
14. Art or a design incorporating trademarks or copyrights owned by third 
parties, unless written permission of such use is satisfactory in form and 
substance to the school, is obtained from the trademark holder or 
copyright owner, and approved in advance. 
15. Any use which suggests or implies the school district’s endorsement of 
other organizations, companies, products, services, political parties or 
views, or religious organizations or beliefs. 
16. Any use that discriminates or implies discrimination against any persons 
or groups based on age, ancestry, belief, color, creed, disability, national 
origin, race, ethnicity, religion, sex, sexual orientation or veteran status, or 
in any other way that would be a violation of the School District’s anti-
discrimination policies or practices. 
All advertisements are to be reviewed by the superintendent or designee who is 
responsible for applying and enforcing this policy.  Any entity or person who 
disagrees with the superintendent or designee’s decision can petition the 
superintendent’s office for a review within 30 calendar days from the initial 
written decision of the designee.  The superintendent, or the superintendent’s 
designee, will review the petition and upon request meet with the petitioner.  The 
superintendent or designee will issue a decision within 30 calendar days after the 
petition and/or meeting which decision shall be final.
