 B. MASB’S PRESIDENTS AWARD OF RECOGNITION – IDA EDMUNDS 
The Michigan Association of School Boards’ (MASB) Board Member Awards 
Recognition Program was developed in 1990 with input from both veteran 
Michigan school board members and representatives from higher 
education and state administrator organizations. Its goal is to enhance 
the ability of school boards to improve Michigan public schools and the 
achievement capabilities of 1.6 million Michigan students. 
Board Member, Ida Edmunds, has achieved the President’s Award of 
Recognition, the highest certificat ion for a school board member, by 
completing all seven levels of certifi cation and 1,383 certification credits.  
Ms. Kathy Hayes, Executive Director of the Michigan Association of School 
Boards, is here with us this evening and will be presenting the award.  
Congratulations Ida!

 on the Troy Board of Education.

Resolution of Respect 
to the Memory of Anthony Spagnola  
By the Troy Board of Education September 6, 2011 
WHEREAS, Anthony Spagnola, a 30 year resident of Troy, Michigan, was born in 
Easton, Pennsylvania on May 20, 1950, and departed on July 20, 2011, at the 
age of 61, and;  
WHEREAS, Anthony Spagnola was a man who valued education and served on 
the Troy Board of Education from July 1991 through June 2007; several of those 
years as its president, and;  
WHEREAS, the Troy Board of Education recognizes the 16 years of excellent 
service that Anthony Spagnola dedicated to Troy Public Schools and the Troy 
Community by serving on its Board, and;  
BE IT THEREFORE RESOLVED THAT the Troy Board of Education expresses its 
profound sadness upon the loss of our friend and colleague; that we remember 
with affection and appreciation, the devotion he had to our community and we 
express to the Spagnola family our deepest sympathy; 
NOW BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED THAT a copy of this resolution be given to Mrs. 
Sharon Spagnola and family and that a copy be placed in the official minutes of 
this Board.

WHEREAS, MCL 388.1622f(1)(c) requires that if a district did not enter 
into an agreement with the Michigan  Department of Education (MDE) to 
develop a service consolidation plan to  reduce school operating costs under 
former section 11d as it was in effect for 2010-2011, the district enters into 
an agreement with MDE to develop a service consolidation plan that is in 
compliance with department guidelines described in subsection (2); and; 
WHEREAS, the district has already impl emented cost savings through 
cooperative purchasing and service consolidation initiatives; and; 
WHEREAS, the district continues to pursue any and all consolidation of 
services that make sense for the district without such agreement; and; 
WHEREAS, the district intends to continue to realize savings from 
cooperative agreements an d service consolidation plans currently in place; 
and; 
WHEREAS, the intent is for Troy School District to fully qualify for the 
$100 per pupil incentive payment un der MCL 388.1622f Best Practices 
Incentive Grants; 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT the Troy School District 
will enter into an agreement with the Michigan Department of Education to 
develop a Service Consolidation Plan to reduce school operating costs that is 
in compliance with MDE Service Conso lidation Guidance distributed on June 
30, 2011.

Included in the 2011-12 School Aid Bill is Sect ion 388.1622f – Incentive to Districts 
Meeting Financial Best  Practices. This section requires districts to meet 4 out of the 5 
required criteria in order to qualify for a one-time $100 per pupil payment for the 2011-
2012 fiscal year (FY) only.  Funds will not be appropriated for the purposes of this 
section for subsequent fiscal years. This in centive was included to help offset the $470 
per pupil reduction all districts incurred for the 2011-12 year.  
Attached is the Michigan Department of  Education (MDE) Amended Guidance for 
Service Consolidation Plans dated June 30, 2011. It amends the or iginal guidance of 
Section 11d of the State School Aid Act (MC L 388.1611d). Under the original guidance, 
a district was required to adopt a resolution no later than February 1, 2010 entering into 
a Service Consolidation Plan with the MDE in order to have maximum flexibility when 
applying the $165 per pupil reduction against one of the unprotected State School Aid 
categoricals other than Section 22b for th e 2009-2010 school year. The Troy School 
District did not enter into an agreement under Section 11d for the 2009-10 year. 
Based on the criteria of Section 22f, in the new State School Ai d Act, the Board of 
Education must approve a resolution stating that the Troy School District must enter into 
a Service Consolidation Plan Ag reement with the Michigan De partment of Education in 
order to meet the requirement of MCL 388.1622f(1)(c) and meet the overall requirement 
of attaining 4 out of 5 criteria  of section 22f (1) (a-e) in or der to qualify for the additional 
$100 per pupil funding.  Attached is backup information regarding the above.
