Subject: Synchronized Clock System (RFP #9840) Evaluation and Recommendation  
We received, opened and publicly read bids and proposals for a complete, turn-key, district-wide 
Synchronized Clock System at 2:30 P.M. March 10, 2017. The following is a summary of the evaluation 
and our recommendation. 
Four proposals were received: Sound Engineering, Digital Age Technologies, I.COMM and Amcomm. No 
mandatory alternates were requested/required. 
The initial results were tabulated as follows: 
Proposal Responses 
Bidder System  Base Bid  
Sound Engineering Sapling  $ 391,473.00  
Digital Age Technologies (DAT) Carehawk  $ 403,787.00  
I.COMM Primex  $ 430,372.96  
Amcomm Primex  $ 492,000.00  
All proposals were evaluated for compliance with the RFP requirements, pricing, technical 
solution and acceptance of terms and conditions of the RFP. All Bidders provided compliant 
proposals and none of the bidders took any exceptions (See notes on transportation building below). 
We requested further information and clarification from bidders on March 16, 2017. As all systems met 
the RFP requirements including additional clarification requested, we proceeded with the low compliant 
bidder, Sound Engineering. 
The following clarifications were made: 
• The proposed digital clock is Sapling Model is SBL31S4041R, the unit is a 4-digit model 
• Countdown functionality is available and will be provided and programmed at no additional cost 
to the district for all secondary buildings as requested by the principals 
• Digital clocks will show hours and minutes in normal mode, switch to minutes and seconds 
during count-down mode, then revert to normal mode after the countdown is complete 
• The clock system will synchronize with the district time server on an hourly basis 
• The clock system will integrate with the existing PA system and correct the PA system time once 
a day at 2:00 A.M. PA system integration is at no cost to the district 
• Sound Engineering, the recommended bidder, confirmed a single point of contact (Project 
manager) and weekly online written status reports will be provided to ensure project success 
• Sound Engineering, the recommended bidder, confirmed that they will meet the project 
schedule outlined in the RFP (Completion August 4th) and that installation will be by building. 
126
Once complete, the building will be moved to the new system prior to commencing 
implementation in the next building 
Note: Drawings for the transportation building were not available during the RFP process and therefore 
pricing was not included in any proposal. We are requesting an allowance of $15,000.00 to cover this 
building and any other clocks not discovered during our walk through of the District buildings. 
We recommend the award of the Synchronized Clock System RFP #9840 to Sound Engineering in the 
amount of base bid amount of $391,473.00 with an additional allowance of $15,000.00 for a total of 
$406,473.00.
