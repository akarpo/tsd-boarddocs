Equity and Excellence Policy - DRAFT  
The Troy School District commits to achieving equity and excellence for each and every student, 
preparing them for citizenship, higher education and the workforce. The School Board will provide 
the leadership and resources such that every student, with support for his or her unique 
circumstances, has access to and is provided the opportunit ies and experiences that lead to 
academic, emotional, physical and social success.  
The Troy School District  believes each student will reach high levels of achievement when 
supported by a challenging curriculum, effective instruction, engaging relationshi ps and an 
environment that promotes their well-being academically, emotionally, physically and socially.  
The Troy School District commits to policies, regulations and practices that incorporate and are 
consistent with the following principles:  
 Developmentally responsive educational practices and inclusive learning environments 
empower students to perform at high levels. TSD  will maintain, and employees will use, 
differentiated teaching strategies, assessment and other tools that support students in their 
learning.   
 Educational outcomes are not presumed by income, race, disability, gender, first language 
or family background.   
 TSD will develop a systematic approach to assessing and monitoring institutional climate, 
ensuring that implicit bias and its potential consequences are understood, and that people of 
diverse backgrounds feel welcome and respected. 
 By working together, administrators, teachers and staff model collaborative behavior to  
improve the quality of learning for all students. Each employee [administrators, teachers 
and staff] is responsible and accountable for the learning and achievement of all students  
and will help build a culture that reinforces this responsibility.   
 Positive educational outcomes are the responsibility of the Troy School District employees 
and our students, in partnership with our families and community.   
TSD will Include teaching and research about racism, its causes and effects, as a central part 
of the teaching mission of our district. 
Our curriculum will strive to 
 Provide students with materials and learning that prepare them to engage respectfully with 
others, regardless of background and prior life experience. 
 Create classroom environments where hierarchy and privilege can be identified and 
challenged and ignorance of barriers to opportunities can be corrected while encouraging 
and supporting freedom of thought and expression. 
 Provide curricular opportunities, as appropriate, in all academic subjects that expose 
students to the role of inequity as it relates to the discipline under study.
