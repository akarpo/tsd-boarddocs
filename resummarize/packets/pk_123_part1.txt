2. RECOGNITION 
B. State School of Character—Wass Elementary School  
We also honor Wass Elementary School this evening for being named a 2019 
State School of Character.  
A select few schools across the country are chosen each year as a State School 
of Character through the implementation of 11 core principles, which include 
promoting good character, culture and ethics ; fostering student self -
motivation, giving students the opportunity for moral action and engaging 
families and community membe rs as partners in character -building efforts. 
Wass Elementary School was praised for the high quality of work in all 11 
principle areas and the school’s application has now been forwarded on for 
consideration as a National School of Character, which will be announced later 
this Spring. 
We would also like to commend Wattles Elementary School, which was named 
a 2019 Honorable Mention School of Character and Athens High School, which 
was recognized as Emerging.   
Ms. Lori Soifer , Character.org Coordinator for the State of Michigan , is here 
with us this evening to present the official certificate to Matt Jansen, Principal 
of Wass Elementary.   
Recommended Action:  Certificate is presented to Matt Jansen
