A. Advanced Placement Honors 
Troy School District 
 More than 350 Troy students have been honored for their outstanding performance 
on Advanced Placement exams during the past school year.  Troy students were 
honored by the College Board in four award categories: AP Scholar, AP Scholar with 
Honors, AP Scholar with Distinction and National AP Scholar. Tonight, we will be 
honoring students who have earned the National AP Scholar honors. This award is 
granted to students who receive an average grade of four on all of the AP exams 
that were taken last year. Students in this category must also take eight or more 
AP exams during the school year. A number of those students were seniors last 
year.  This evening, we invited students who achieved this honor during their junior 
year and those students are:  Raymond Chen, Mason Liang, and Xiao Zhu.
