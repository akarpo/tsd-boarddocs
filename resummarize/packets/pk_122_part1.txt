C.  Principal Appreciation Month 
Throughout the State of Michigan, February has traditionally been celebrated as 
Principal Appreciation Month, in recognition of the outstanding work of our 
building-level educational leaders. In Troy, we are fortunate to have an exemplary 
group of principals dedicated to the success of every student in the District. This 
evening, we will pass a resolution to commend our elementary and secondary 
principals for their exceptional commitment to excellence in the Troy School 
District.  
 
WHEREAS, energetic and inspiring school leadership is essential if teachers, 
schools and districts are to ensure that all Troy students are college  and career 
ready; and, 
  
WHEREAS, K-12 school principals play a vital role in the success of students and 
act as the liaison between the school and the community it serves, ensuring that 
parents and the community are aware of student and school achievements; and, 
  
WHEREAS, Troy School District principals are dedicated,  quality educational 
professionals committed to providing sound leadership and guidance to their staff 
and students; and,  
  
WHEREAS, during this month, we join with educators, parents and students 
throughout Michigan to raise awareness of the importance of educational 
leadership, and to recognize and thank all principals working in Troy for their 
exemplary service; 
  
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED , that the Troy Board of Education 
officially recognizes February as Principal Appreciation Month in Troy and thanks 
our dedicated building leaders for all they do for our students and staff.
