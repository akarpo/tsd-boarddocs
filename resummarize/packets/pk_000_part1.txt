B.  Recognition      
 
 
1.   Board of Education Recognition 
 
Each January the Troy School District recognizes and celebrates the 
work of a very important group of individuals: The Board of 
Education. Troy is truly fortunate to be governed by Board members 
who are tireless, professional, and passionate about improving the 
opportunities of our students. The successes that we receive in the 
Troy School District stem in large part from the support and guidance 
we receive from our Board members. The Troy Board of Education 
works very hard to ensure that the entire district is committed to 
promoting student achievement. Tonight, we would like to honor the 
Board of Education with a short slide show that features thanks and 
some advice from Boulan Park Middle School students.

Special Board of Education Meeting 2 January 11, 2010 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 WHEREAS,  as a condition to applying for and 
receiving Race to the Top funds, the State of Michigan is 
required to adopt a final statewide plan in conformity with 
ARRA requirements, a condition that has not been met as 
of the date the Board adopted this resolution. 
 
 WHEREAS,  as a condition to applying for and 
receiving Race to the Top Funds, the Troy School District is 
required to execute the Michigan Participating LEA/ISD 
Memorandum of Understanding, attached hereto, which 
incorporates the requirements of the yet-to-be 
promulgated final statewide plan. 
 
 WHEREAS, the State Superintendent, Michael 
Flanagan, has represented, in writing, he will permit 
school districts, upon request, to rescind their agreement 
to the attached Michigan Participating LEA/ISD 
Memorandum of Understanding; 
 
 WHEREAS,  regardless of the State of Michigan’s 
failure to timely promulgate a final statewide plan as 
required by the ARRA, the Troy School District is required 
to submit its application for Race to the Top Funds on or 
before the January 7, 2010 deadline; 
 
 NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS RESOLVED:   That the 
Board President and the District’s Superintendent are 
authorized to sign the Michigan Participating LEA/ISD 
Memorandum of Understanding attached hereto. 
 
 IT IS FURTHER RESOLVED:   That the District’s 
Superintendent, after receiving direction from the Board 
of Education, is authorized to rescind the District’s 
agreement to the attached Michigan Participating LEA/ISD 
Memorandum of Understanding if, as written or 
interpreted, it incorporates terms or conditions that are 
not in the best interest of the District or the District’s 
students. 
 
Discussion of the motion followed.  It was then moved by 
Mrs. Philippart and seconded by Mr. Miletti that the 
resolution be amended as follows:
