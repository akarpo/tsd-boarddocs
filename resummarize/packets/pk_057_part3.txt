 CONSIDERATION:  TEA CONTRACT RATIFICATION  
A tentative agreement between representatives of the Troy Board of Education 
and the Troy Education Association/MEA/NEA (TEA) was reached on March 22, 
2014.  The TEA membership voted to ratify the agreement on March 26, 2014.   
The tentative agreement is a contract for the period of April 1, 2014, through 
June 30, 2015.   The tentative agreement provides all TSD teachers and ancillary 
staff with an off -schedule payment for the 2013/2014 school year in the amount 
of $3,250.  As of July 1, 2014, TSD teachers and ancillary staff employed in such 
positions before the 2013/2014 school year who are eligible for step advancement 
will advance two steps from the 2013/2014 sal ary schedule for placement on a 
new 14-step salary schedule (unless additional step advancement is required to 
ensure that a TSD teacher’s or ancillary staff member’s base salary is not the 
same or lower than it was during the 2012/2013 school year.)   Longevity 
payments for TSD teachers and ancillary staff will remain unchanged and lane 
changes for eligible teachers and ancillary staff will only be made for the 
2014/2015 school year.   All TSD teachers and ancillary staff will also receive a 
Board-paid Tax-Sheltered Annuity contribution during the 2014/2015 school year.  
During the 2014/2015 school year, the number of student instructional days will 
be increased from 179 days to 181 days and the number of teacher work days 
will be increased from 184 days to 1 87 days.   Finally, the tentative agreement 
includes a fund balance provision which will help to maintain the District’s General 
Fund Equity Balance at a minimum of 12% after conclusion of the financial audit 
for the 2014/2015 budget year and which would sh are any additional General 
Fund Equity Balance in excess of 17% after conclusion of the financial audit for 
the 2014/2015 budget year with TSD teachers and ancillary staff.

2014 with two providers responding, our current provider, First Student, Inc. and the

responses.

prices their service on a per run fee, which is up to 5.5 hours. Therefore, Dominick 
Asaro created a spreadsheet to convert Durham’s pricing into a 5.5 hour run time, to 
compare the two firm’s responses and our current run configurations.

performance for the district has met our needs and expectations, Dominick 
recommends Troy continue its relationship with First Student, Inc. for transportation 
services.

C‐1402  SMOKINGTOBACCO‐FREE SCHOOLS 
It is the policy of theThe Troy School District that prohibits the use 
and/or  sale  of  any  any  type  of  tobacco  products  and  non‐tobacco 
nicotine  products  (including  smokeless  tobacco  and  any  new 
emerging  tobacco  products)  is  prohibited  in  vehicles,  buildings, 
facilities, structures, or other real estate owned, leased or otherwise 
controlled  by  the  Troy  School  District  and  at  all  school‐sponsored 
events and functions on or off campus, by any person, at any time, 
in  any  location,  and  at  any  event  in  vehicles,  buildings,  facilities, 
structures,  or  other  real  estate  owned,  leased  or  otherwise 
controlled by the Troy School District at all times (24 hours a day – 
7 days a week.) 
For  the  purpose  of  this  policy,  the  use  and/or  sale  of  a  tobacco  or 
non‐tobacco nicotine product is defined as follows: 
 “Tobacco  product”  means  a  preparation  of  tobacco  to  be 
inhaled,  chewed,  or  placed  in  a  person’s  mouth.Tobacco 
products  include,  but  are  not  limited  to,  cigarettes,  cigars,  spit 
tobacco,  shisha  or  hookah  use,  snus,  tobacco  strips,  sticks,  and 
dissolvable  tobacco  products.   Non‐tobacco  nicotine  products 
include,  but  are  not  limited  to,  e‐cigarettes.   This  does  not 
include  products  that  are  classified  as  “nicotine  replacement 
therapy”  and  prescribed  by  a  physician  to  help  tobacco  users 
quit. 
 “Any  person”  includes  students,  employees,  visitors, 
contractors,  delivery  drivers,  etc.,  all  groups  using  school 
property, and any other persons.   
128
 “Any time” means 24 hours per day, seven days per week, and 
365 days per year. 
 “Any  location”  includes  the  school’s  property,  grounds, 
buildings,  and  vehicles,  even  when  school  is  out  of  session  or 
the event is sponsored by another organization. 
 “Any  event”  includes  all  school‐sponsored  events,  whether  on 
or off school property. 
“Use  of  a  tobacco  or  non‐tobacco  nicotine  product”  means  any  of 
the following: 
 The  carrying  by  a  person  of  a  lighted  cigar,  cigarette,  pipe,  or 
  other lighted smoking device. 
 The inhaling or chewing of a tobacco product. 
 The placing of a tobacco product within a person’s mouth.

RIGHTS AND RESPONSIBILITIES, CODE OF STUDENT CONDUCT    J‐2000 
J‐2012  ANTI‐BULLYING POLICY 
It is the policy of the District to provide a safe educational environment for all 
students and employees.  Bullying of a student or employee at school, in any 
building within the Troy School District boundaries or any TSD event is strictly 
prohibited. This policy shall be interpreted and enforced to protect all students 
and employees to equally prohibit bullying without regard to its subject matter 
or motivating animus.   
A. Prohibited Conduct.  
1. Bullying.  Bullying of a student or employee at school is strictly prohibited.  
For the purposes of this policy, ʺbullyingʺ shall be defined as: 
Any written, verbal, or physical act, or any electronic communication, that is 
intended or that a reasonable person would know is likely to harm one or more 
students or employees either directly or indirectly by doing any of the 
following:   
a.  Substantially interfering with educational opportunities, benefits, or 
programs of one or more students or employees;  
b.  Adversely affecting a studentʹs or employee’s ability to participate in or 
benefit from  
the Districtʹs educational programs or activities by placing the student or 
employee in reasonable fear of physical harm or by causing substantial 
emotional distress;  
c.  Having an actual and substantial detrimental effect on a student or 
employeeʹs   
  physical or mental health; or 
d.  Causing substantial disruption in, or substantial interference with, the  
  orderly operation of the school.   
2.  Retaliation/False Accusation.  Retaliation or false accusation against a target of 
bullying, anyone reporting bullying, a witness, or another person with reliable 
information about an act of bullying is strictly prohibited.   
131
B.  Reporting an Incident.  If a student, staff member, or other individual believes 
there has been an incident of bullying in violation of this policy, s/he shall promptly 
report such incident to the appropriate principal or designee,  
 C.  Investigation.   All reported allegations of a policy violation or related 
complaint about bullying shall be promptly and thoroughly investigated by the 
building principal or designee.  Each verified incident of bullying and resulting 
consequences including discipline and referrals will be reported annually to the 
school board. 
D.  Notice to Parent/Guardian.  If the principal or designee determines that an 
incident of bullying has occurred, s/he shall promptly provide notification of same to 
the parent/guardian of the victim of the bullying and the parent/guardian of the 
perpetrator of the bullying.   
E.  Annual Reports.  At least annually, the building principal or designee, or the 
Responsible School Official shall report all verified incidents of bullying and the 
resulting consequences, including any disciplinary action or referrals, to the Board of 
Education.   
F.  Responsible School Official.   The superintendent shall be responsible for 
ensuring the proper implementation of this policy throughout the District.  The 
foregoing appointment shall not reduce or eliminate the duties and responsibilities of 
a principal or designee as described in this policy. 
G.  Posting/Publication of Policy.  Notice of this policy will be: (a) annually 
circulated to all students and staff, (b) posted in conspicuous locations in all school 
buildings and departments within the District, (c) annually discussed with students 
and employees, and (d) incorporated into the teacher, student, and parent/guardian 
handbooks. 
H.  Definitions 
1. ʺ At schoolʺ means in a classroom, elsewhere on school premises, on a school 
bus or other school‐related vehicle, or at a school‐sponsored activity or event 
whether or not it is held on school premises.  ʺAt schoolʺ also includes any 
conduct using a telecommunications access device or telecommunications service 
provider that occurs off school premises if the device or provider is owned by or 
under the Districtʹs control. 
2. ʺ Telecommunications Access Deviceʺ means that term as defined in Section 
219a of the Michigan Penal Code, 1931 PA 328, MCL 750.219a, as may be 
amended from time to time.   
132
3. ʺ Telecommunications Service Providerʺ means that term as defined in Section 
219a of the Michigan Penal Code, supra, as may be amended from time to time.
