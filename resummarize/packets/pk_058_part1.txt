 D.  National Teacher Appreciation Week 
Teacher Appreciation Week is observed nationally each year in May. This 
year, Teacher Appreciation Week is being celebrated May  5th – May 9th.  At 
tonight’s meeting , the Troy School District and Board of Education would 
like to recognize all of our outstanding teachers by adopting the following 
resolution: 
WHEREAS, throughout Troy and across the country, teachers inspire, 
nurture and open children’s minds to new ideas,  
WHEREAS, teachers promote and strengthen the principles of democratic 
equality, develop leaders and encourage critical thinking; and 
WHEREAS, Troy teachers set the bar for excellence in education and 
continually exceed the highest standards in the profession; and 
WHEREAS, the goal of Teacher Appreciation Week is to raise national and 
local awareness of teaching excellence in building a better society; now,  
THEREFORE BE IT  RESOLVED, that the Troy School District supports 
National Teacher Appreciation Week, May 5-9, 201 4, and encourages our 
schools and community to appropriately recognize the vital and changing 
role of teachers in imagining and creating a better future for society.

‘Classroom and Kids’ Budget 
Proposal Resolution  
The  Board  approved  the  following  resolution  in  support  of 
the  ‘Classrooms  and  Kids’  budget  proposal,  which  will  be 
sent  to  legislators  regarding  legislative  priorities  and  a 
proposal to: 
Base state funding for education on an accurate assessment 
of  the  cost  to  provide  a  satisfactory  education  to  each 
student,  accounting  for  different  ages,  different  locations, 
and different learning styles and needs. 
Restore Michigan's education system to its past success by 
developing  a  long‐term  plan  to  reverse  the  last  decade's 
cuts in the money available for classroom teaching. 
Maintain  the  integrity  of  K‐12  funding  by  ensuring  that  it 
faces  no  new  revenue  cuts  or  spending  demands  without 
providing matching revenue.
