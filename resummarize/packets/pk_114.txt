             Boulan Park Middle School   
The American Scholastic Achievement League was formed by a committee 
of middle school teachers for the purpose of  challenging students to 
demonstrate knowledge and thinking skills, and to recognize and reward 
excellence.

Mathematics. The questions are not related to a specific curriculum but are 
selected to reflect the general knowledge and educational levels expected of 
sixth or eighth grade students.

K.  FIRST Robotics World Competition 
             International Academy East and Troy High School 
Under strict rules, limited resources, and an intense six-week time limit, 
teams of students are challenged to raise funds, design a team "brand", 
hone teamwork skills, and build and program industrial-size robots to play a 
difficult field game against like-minded competitors. It’s as close to real-
world engineering as a student can get. 
The IA-East team took 30th place in the Curie Division at the World 
Championships and also won the State Entrepreneur Award at the state 
competition, a District Engineering Inspiration Award, and a District 
Imagery Award.  An IA-East student also received the First Deans List 
Award at the World Championships (he is 1 of 10 students to receive this in 
the world!)  
A Troy High student was also named as a Dean’s List Finalist at the FIRST 
Robotics Competition in Detroit last month.  This is a very distinguished 
honor! 
            Congratulations!
