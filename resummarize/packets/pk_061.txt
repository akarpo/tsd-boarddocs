Re: Bid Award Recommendation for iPad Air 
The original distribution plan called for utilizing the district stock of iPad2’s for use with our current 
Third Grade students.  Depending on the actual longevity of the iPad2’s, they were scheduled to be 
replaced in the Summer of 2015 with iPad Airs.  Recent information has shown that the current 
iPad2’s trade in value may be as much as $175/unit.  The district may have as many as 750 units that 
will be traded-in for additional value.   
Because of the higher current trade-in value and because these units were scheduled for replacement 
in Bond Series One-Summer of 2015 and are fund are within budget.  I am recommending that we 
sell the iPad2’s originally planned for the 3rd Grade students and replace them with iPad Airs.  This 
will allow us to be consistent with all of the iPads being distributed for all 3rd-5th grade students 
across the elementary schools.  iPad Airs will also have a higher residual value than the iPad2’s two 
or three years from now.  The iPad Airs are better performers than the iPad2s as they have a faster 
processor and a better touch screen.  
I am recommending the purchase of a quantity of 1040 iPad Air from Apple Computers of 
Cupertino, California @ 479/each for a total of $498,160.  These items will be purchased from the 
MDE Technology Readiness Infrastructure Grant Bid tool providing the district with an additional 
rebate for each iPad Air. 
All items recommended for purchase are being expensed from the 2014 Bond Fund.  
Should you have any questions, please feel free to contact me.

2. Purpose
The purpose of the 90-Day Entry Plan is to provide a road map to guide the 
transition from the Assistant Superintendent position to the Superintendent of 
the Troy School District in a seamless and unified manner. A practice will be 
established of “seek first to understand” through collaborative listening, honoring 
of the past persons and practices and clarifying our collective needs going 
forward. This practice will be mandatory in order to maintain the current district’s 
exemplary student achievement and the continuation to forge a coherent plan 
to assure the success and future accomplishments of all TSD students.

Superintendent has an immediate responsibility to listen, observe and learn 
so that the efforts of the entire school community are validated and all 
decision-making is “future” focused. In order to accomplish this focus the 
Superintendent must:
• Develop a collaborative working relationship with the Board of Education   
   based on the mutual agreement that all decisions made will be in the best

4. Statement of Commitment
The Troy School District, is committed to the learning of all children. 
Simply said, “All Means All!” Every day in every classroom in every school, we 
expect that all students will be met by the highest quality teachers who utilize 
the best instructional practice and who will hold all students to the highest 
standard. This is made possible when the leaders in our organization agree 
that we exist to provide equitable access to the best possible educational 
experience for each TSD student. We must embrace the 21st Century learning 
experiences and acknowledge that the learning of today will be transformed 
by the technology of tomorrow. We must accept that learning extends 
beyond the classroom and that we have an obligation to our students to 
give them access to world class Fine and Performing Arts, and academic, 
as well as athletic competition. Our work must be collaborative, respectful, 
and transparent in manner. Most importantly, we must always remember 
that we are the sum of many parts, including our students, staff, families, and 
community. As your Superintendent I am committed to the success of all TSD 
students and I will fight each and every day to guarantee the best educational 
experience, because in the Troy School District, “All Means All!”

7. Conclusion
This draft of the 90 Day Entry Plan for the Superintendent is meant to serve as 
a formative process to provide a smooth transition from the role of Assistant 
Superintendent to Superintendent of the Troy School District. During this process 
qualitative and quantitative data will be gathered, collaboratively developed 
and assessed for the purpose of informed decision-making. Information will be 
shared regularly with the Board of Education and Central Office leadership.
This plan is designed to, “seek first to understand” the culture and practices that 
have made the Troy School District among the most elite in Michigan and the 
nation. I am committed to listening, learning and honoring the past as we seek 
information that will provide a clear roadmap for our future. 
Finally, we will be fully committed to maintain our status as a “lighthouse” district 
and continue to embrace the learning of all students. My first focus is firming my 
commitment to doing the right work, a laser-like focus on exemplary classroom 
practice. Secondly, exemplifying the right organizational focus; that the entire 
district organization is committed and in agreement that we exist to serve our 
students. With that agreement we have a moral obligation to provide the highest 
principles, most exemplary curricular opportunities and the continued support 
and maintaining of our fiscal responsibility in order to provide the right resources 
for all students every day. Thirdly, that we continually exhibit the “relentless 
press” to succeed regardless of our challenges, always asking the question, “If 
not you, then who?” And fourth, to strive to build a true learning organization, 
where we sustain and nurture relationships while building trust and integrity as 
we work together to build the district’s future. It is my vision that students, staff, 
administration, and community will proudly stand together and support the Troy 
School District as the standard by which all others are measured.
