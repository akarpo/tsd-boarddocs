WHAT IS A BOARD 
MEMBER?
Governance 
◦ School Board Members are elected officials
◦ We must uphold the Michigan Constitution’s mandate to provide free public elementary 
and secondary education.
◦ According to the School Code, the general powers a Board of Education may execute 
include:
◦ Incidental or appropriate to the performance of a function related to the operation of the district
◦ In the interests of public elementary and secondary education
◦ Educating pupils- K-12, preschool, adult ed
◦ Provide for the safety and welfare of students
◦ Acquire and dispose of school property
◦ Matters involving the receipt and expenditure of school funds
◦ Joint agreement and cooperative arrangements
BUT… Actions CANNOT conflict or circumvent state prohibitions
How School Boards Work
National School Boards Association Code of Ethics for School Board Members
”As a member of my local Board of Education I will strive to improve public education, and to that end I 
will take no private action that will compromise the board or administration.”
Michigan Association of School Boards Board of Education Governance Standards 
(endorsed by the Michigan State Board of Education)
“The individual school board member understands and respects both the authority and responsibilities 
of the Board of Education.
◦ 3.a.  The board member acts in accordance with Michigan law, which does not vest individual 
board members with any type of independent legal authority.
◦ 3.c. The board member follows established policies and procedures.
◦ 3.d. The board member supports decisions of the Board of Education after a vote is taken regardless 
of his/her individual position prior to the vote.
◦ 3.g. The board member demonstrates understanding of and respect for school district structure and 
protocols.”

How we do this in the Troy School District
Code of Ethics as outlined in the TSD Policy Manual Section 1000.01 Organization 
and Functioning of the Board:
Each Board member has acknowledged and signed the following excerpt of our Code of Ethics:
◦ As a member of the Board, I will promote the best interest of the School District as a whole and 
shall adhere to the following ethical standards and principles:
◦ I will recognize that a Board member has no legal authority as an individual and that decisions 
can be made only by a majority vote at a public meeting of the Board.
◦ I will take no private action that might compromise the Board or administration and will respect 
the confidentiality of privileged information.
◦ I will abide by majority decisions of the Board, while retaining the right to seek changes in 
such decisions through ethical and constructive channels.
◦ I will strive for a positive working relationship with the Superintendent, respecting the 
Superintendent's authority to advise the Board, implement Board policy, and administer the 
School District.
WHAT ABOUT 
VIOLATIONS?
Censure
◦ According to Oxford Languages, a censure is the expressing of “formal disapproval”
◦ “By a majority vote, the Board may censure an individual Board member or members 
for violating federal or Michigan law, these Bylaws or Board policy, or otherwise acting 
in a manner inconsistent with the office of a Board member.
Disclaiming Acts of Individual Board 
Members
◦ Michigan law requires that a school board act only as a board, not as private individuals 
◦ Only the vote of a majority of a board’s members creates action by the board. 
◦ Under Section 1201 of the Revised School Code, trustees may only exercise power and perform their 
duties as trustees when doing so collectively and only at a regular or a properly called special meeting. 
◦ When a trustee violates this law and fellow board members are concerned that the community 
may misconstrue the actions of said trustee acting as an individual to be representative of the 
board, they may disclaim the actions of that trustee.
◦ A disclaiming motion passed by the majority of a board alerts the community that the actions of 
an individual trustee do not represent the will of the board.
