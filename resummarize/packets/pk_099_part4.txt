Section 5000.03 - Purchasing 
 
The Board authorizes the Superintendent to purchase and/or supervise the purchase of all materials, 
equipment, supplies and services necessary for the operation of the School District.  It is the intent of the 
Board that the School District purchase competitively, without prejudice or favoritism, and seek maximum 
value for all expenditures.  Competitive pricing is to be pursued by following legally required processes, as 
well as through such methods as cooperative purchasing, formal or informal bidding, requests for quotations 
from multiple vendors, and negotiation. 
 
Competitive Bidding and Micro-Purchases   The Board seeks to ensure the Dis trict complies with all 
competitive bidding and micro -purchase guidelines contained in the Education Department General 
Administrative Regulations (EDGAR).  Specifically, that “all procurement transactions must be conducted 
in a manner providing full and o pen competition consistent with the standards of this section.”  2 CFR § 
200.319.  The Board further acknowledges that these requirements apply specifically to micro -purchases, 
which are defined by EDGAR as: “a purchase of supplies or services using simpli fied acquisition 
procedures, the aggregate amount of which does not exceed the micro -purchase threshold.”  2 CFR § 
200.67.  The Superintendent will delegate responsibility for the development of procedures that comply with 
this policy to the Assistant Superintendent for Business Services. 
 
Automated Clearing House (ACH) Arrangements and Electronic Transaction of Funds   This Board 
has determined that it is in the best interest of the District to make certain District financial transactions by
