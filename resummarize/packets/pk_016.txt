2.   RECOGNITION      
A.   Wendy Underwood, Board Service 
Wendy Underwood has served as president of the Troy Board of 
Education from July of 2009 to January 2011. Tonight we honor 
Ms. Underwood for her outstanding leadership and commitment to 
the students of our District.  
Under Wendy’s leadership, the Troy School District continues to be 
recognized for setting the standard for excellence in education 
throughout the country. Our stud ent achievement levels remain 
among the highest at national and state levels, and in the past year 
we were honored to have 42 Nation al Merit Scholar Semi-finalists 
and more than 600 students and staff members recognized for top 
performances in tournaments and competitions. 
This school Board joins the enti re Troy community in showing 
great appreciation for Ms. Unde rwood’s outstanding leadership. 
During 18 months of difficult an d changing economic conditions, 
Ms. Underwood led our District with professionalism and a 
steadfast commitment to our students. This allowed our schools to 
thrive and continue to provide exemplary opportunities to every 
Troy student. Under her direct ion, the East Campus of the 
International Academy added another freshman class and 
continued to recruit and retain outstanding students. We formed a 
district-wide Diversity and In clusion Council to advance our 
mission of tolerance and inclus ion for every member of our 
community. Each of our school s embraced new goals under our 
continuing North Central Accreditation process.  
Our Board also continued to grow professionally under Ms. 
Underwood’s leadership. We were honored to once again be among 
a handful of school districts to earn a Standard of Excellence Award 
from the Michigan Association of School Boards for continued 
professional development as well as the Visionary Leadership 
Award. The Board was also honored for its commitment to the Fine 
Arts for the third year in a row. The Troy School District owes a 
great debt to Ms. Underwood for her exemplary leadership 
throughout the past year and a ha lf. We were very fortunate to 
have you as our president and lead er. Thank you, Wendy, for your 
dedication to our students, our schools and our community. 
Recommendation:  Distribute token of recognition.

8. CURRICULUM 
B. CONSIDERATION:   Schools Closed to Open Enrollment for  
       2011/2012 
Administration has reviewed the enrollment for elementary, middle 
school, and high school attendance areas.  Our goal has been to 
maximize the enrollments at both  Athens and Troy High School 
resulting in both schools being closed to open enrollment for the past 
three years. For the 2010/2011 school year it was recommended that 
25 spots be available for students scheduled to go to Troy High School 
but wishing to attend Athens High Sc hool.  It is projected Troy High 
School will continue to maintain optimal enrollment and, due to some 
continued demographic shifts, it is appropriate to consider a limited 
number of open enrollments be made available for the 2011/2012 
school year at Athens High School.  It is therefore recommended that 
Athens High School and Troy Hi gh School be closed to open 
enrollment, with the exception that 30 openings be made available for 
incoming ninth graders who live in the Troy High School attendance 
area but wish to attend Athens Hi gh School for the 2011/2012 school 
year.  Due to limited space, the district will be accepting applications 
from February 2 – 18, 2011. 
RECOMMENDED ACTION: Adopt the following resolution. 
SUGGESTED RESOLUTION 
 Moved by: __________________________ 
 Supported by: __________________________ 
Be it therefore resolved that the Troy Board of Education adopts a resolution 
to close Athens High School and Troy High School to open enrollment for the 
2011/2012 school year, with the except ion of not more than 30 ninth grade 
students who live in the Troy High School  attendance area but wish to attend 
Athens High School.  Students with siblings attending Athens High School and 
currently enrolled in grades 9 – 11 will be allowed to open enroll to Athens for 
the 2011/2012 school year.
