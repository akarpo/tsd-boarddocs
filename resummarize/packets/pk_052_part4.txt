 A list of schools ranked on their student performance in 
mathematics, reading, writing, science, and social studies.

of their ranking.

assessment data for 30 or more full academic year students in two 
or more tested subjects.

based on the bottom 5% of this list.   
 It is used to determine:  
 Focus Schools, based on the achievement gap component of this list.   
 Reward Schools, based on the top 5% of schools in the ranking as well 
as the schools with the highest improvement values from this list.   
 Beating the Odds schools, which are those schools either 
outperforming their expected ranking or outperforming other similarly 
situated schools, are also reward schools.
