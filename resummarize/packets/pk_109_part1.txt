1. ELECTION OF ACTING CHAIRPERSON 
The first order of business is to e lect an acting chairperson who will 
conduct the meeting until such time as a president has been elected.  
Past practice has been that the previous year’s president conducts this 
part of the meeting. 
2. APPOINTMENT OF TEMPORARY SECRETARY 
 The second order of business is for the acting chairperson  to 
 appoint Judy Holmes to act as temporary secretary until such time as the 
 secretary to the Board has been elected. 
3. ELECTION OF PRESIDENT 
The acting chairperson declares nominations in order for the office o f 
president of the board of education (please note, nominations need not 
be seconded; and a motion and support are made to close the 
nomination.)  Please understand that four votes are required for election. 
4. ELECTION OF VICE PRESIDENT 
The newly elected  president declares nominations in order for the 
office of vice president, following the same procedures as outlined for the 
election of president. 
5. ELECTION OF A SECRETARY 
The president declares nominations in order for the office of secretary, 
following the same procedures as outlined for the election of president. 
6. APPOINTMENT OF TREASURER 
According to Board Policy, the treasurer is appointed annually by the 
Board as a nonvoting officer.  The assistant superintendent of business 
services has served  as treasurer of the Board.  It is my recommendation 
that this procedure continue.

A.   TSD Mission Moment — Superintendent Student Advisory Board 
  MLK, Jr. Day of Service  
Tonight, as part of our Troy School District Mission Moment, we recognize the 
2017-18 Superintendent Student Advisory Board. The SSAB is made up of 
outstanding student leaders from all four high schools in the District, who come 
together with the expressed mission of leaving the Troy School District better 
than it was when they entered. They are nominated by teachers, counselors and 
administrators and are selected from a group of applicants to serve in the Board. 
Yesterday, these students executed another successful Martin Luther King, 
Junior, Day of Service at Athens High School, where members of the community 
came toget her to learn about the legacy of Dr. King and perform four service 
projects that have a direct positive impact in his name. 
These students helped to plan and carry out the  Day of Service  as well as a 
smaller service project each Spring. They also give inp ut to Dr. Machesky and 
Central Office Staff from a high school perspective and work to encourage 
students at their own schools to participate in these district -wide activities. Our 
SSAB students are leaders on campus and we are proud of what they have 
accomplished. 
Recommended Action:  Invite students up to receive a certificate and 
congratulations from the Board.

B.    Board of Education Recognition 
Each January, the Troy School District recognizes and celebrates the 
work of a very important group of individuals: The Board of Education.  
A little over three years ago, we embarked on a journey to become a 
World Class School District by the year 2020. That ambitious goal could 
never become a reality without the hard work and dedication of the 
outstanding trustees who make up our Board. Troy is truly fortunate 
to be g overned by Board members who  put students first in all 
decisions and give tirelessly of their time, energy and talents to our 
school community. This community is grateful for all that you and all 
that you give.  
Tonight, we would like to recognize our Board of Education members 
with a gift that represents our Journey to World Class. Each of you is 
receiving a framed print of our four characteristics of World Class. 
These characteristics were developed after studying some of the best 
schools in the country and internationally; and after considerable input 
from our staff, and of course, our Board of Education. 
We hope that this gift serves as a reminder of the impact of your 
leadership in this exciting and important journey. What this Board 
does, truly makes a difference for our s tudents, our staff and our 
community.  
Thank you for all you do.   
Recommended Action:  Present gift to Board members.
