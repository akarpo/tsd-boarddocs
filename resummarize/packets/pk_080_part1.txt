1. ELECTION OF ACTING CHAIRPERSON 
The first order of business is to e lect an acting chairperson who will 
conduct the meeting until such time as a president has been elected.  Past 
practice has been that the previous year’s president conducts this part 
of the meeting. 
2. APPOINTMENT OF TEMPORARY SECRETARY 
 The second order of business is for the acting chairperson  to 
 appoint Judy Holmes to act as temporary secretary until such time as the 
 secretary to the Board has been elected. 
3. ELECTION OF PRESIDENT 
The acting chairperson declares nominations in order for the office o f 
president of the board of education (please note, nominations need not be 
seconded; and a motion and support are made to close the nomination.)  
Please understand that four votes are required for election. 
4. ELECTION OF VICE PRESIDENT 
The newly elected  president declares nominations in order for the 
office of vice president, following the same procedures as outlined for the 
election of president. 
5. ELECTION OF A SECRETARY 
The president declares nominations in order for the office of secretary, 
following the same procedures as outlined for the election of president. 
6. APPOINTMENT OF TREASURER 
According to Board Policy, the treasurer is appointed annually by the 
Board as a nonvoting officer.  The assistant superintendent of business 
services has served  as treasurer of the Board.  It is my recommendation 
that this procedure continue.

A.    Board of Education Recognition 
Each January, the Troy School District recognizes and celebrates the 
work of a very important group of individuals: The Board of 
Education.  
Troy is truly fortunate to be governed by Board members who are 
tireless, professional, and passionate about improving the  
opportunities for our students and our families . Tonight, we would 
like to recognize our Board of Education  members with a gift  that 
represents our students and staff, the very people you work so hard 
to serve.  
This gift is a token of appreciation that will allow Board members to 
enjoy some of the talents that our students bring to the Troy School 
District, under the direction of  some very dedicated fine arts 
teachers. Each of you has a professional photograph of the mosaic 
art that was created for the new DMC -Children’s Hospital in Troy. 
The photos were printed, matted and prepared by Athens art teacher 
Luba Sordyl and represent student work from across the district. 
We hope that this will serve as a reminder of the sincere appreciation 
of your support of our Fine Arts programs. You are truly a group that 
has given so much to our students and we are grateful to have each 
and every one of you. 
Thank you for all you do.

TSD Mission Moment – Troy 
the Wattles and Troy Union “Watch D.O.G.S.”  “Watch 
D.O.G.S.” stands for Dads of Great Students.  The group 
is comprised of fathers, grandfathers, uncles, and other 
father-figures who volunteer at school for at least one 
full day during the school year. “WatchDOGS” work 
alongside teachers by reading and working on flash 
cards with students, eating lunch and playing at recess, 
with students, monitoring the school entrances and 
hallways, assisting with traffic flow and any other 
assigned activities where they actively engage with all 
students. Wattles Principal Joyce Brasington and Troy 
Union Interim Principal Mike Cottone introduced the 
teachers who lead the program.  Patrick Werner, 5th 
Grade Teacher at Troy Union, Brad Sassack, 2nd Grade 
Teacher at Wattles, and Physical Education Teacher, 
Jamie VanHouzen, shared how the program has been 
working in their schools. Several dads who have 
participated in the program were also invited to speak 
about their experiences.  The students of the dads who 
participated gave “Watch D.O.G.S.” tee-shirts to Board 
members.   
The Board thanked everyone for sharing their 
experiences and agreed that it was a great program and 
appreciated their involvement with our students.
