viability of Apple iPads as part of next year’s laptop replacement project.

component of next year’s equipment refresh.

The District sought to investigate market pricing for selected technology staff, compared to the hourly 
equivalent of salary + benefits of these staff as provided by the district.  A bid was released for Technology 
Support Services on April 5, 2011.  Bids were opened April 19, 2011.  There were a total of six bid 
responses. 
The three apparent low bid responses  are all from companies owned and operated by current District staff.  
SRC Pro-Video Service (proposing AV Specialist service) states that it has been in operation for more than 
20 years, and Specialized Electronics LLC (proposing Telecom and Help Desk Spe cialists) has been in 
operation since 1998.  Edwards and Company (proposing Computer Systems Specialist) is a proposed 
organization that will be formed should its bid be selected.  
In every instance except one response for Help Desk, bid responses from oth er traditional organizations are 
either equal to or higher than District compensation for those positions, suggesting that the market generally 
cannot provide services at the level the District currently receives.  Therefore, the fiscally viable options fo r 
the District to consider are responses  from employee run companies or maintaining the District staff in place.  
When reviewing the first option, bids provided by the employee -run organizations do offer savings.  Please 
see my email to you dated April 19,  2011 for a summary of responses vs bid qualifications as stated in the 
document.  I have found statements in the bid responses indicating that each bidder agrees to comply with 
the RFP requirements.  The only concern raised by any bidder is the 30 day win dow for retirees returning to 
work, which would be part of negotiations for contracts.  
Therefore, should the District intend to proceed with privatizing a portion of technology support resources, I 
recommend negotiations to begin with:  
Edwards and Company 
Specialized Electronics LLC 
SRC Pro-Video Service
