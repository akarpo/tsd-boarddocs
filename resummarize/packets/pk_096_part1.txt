C.   TSD Mission Moment—Katarina Tasich, Troy Union Elementary  
     Writing Assignment  
Tonight, as part of our Troy School District Mission Moment, we recognize 3rd 
grader, Katarina Tasich, for her efforts to make the world a better place and 
taking a stand against bullying. During the month of December , Troy Union 
students wrote opinion pieces on a variety of topic s.  The students were excit ed 
about the project and wanted their voices to be heard.  M any of them reached 
out to the principal.  One topic that peeked Mr. Cottone’s interest was Katarina’s 
essay – she took a risk and shared that our community should do more to stop 
bullying.  A few days later Katarina formally shared her writing piece with him 
and outlined the steps that could be done.  The ideas were great but one really 
stood out.  She suggested that the entire school wear black around a unified 
message of “Blacking out Bullying.”  The message caught on at Tro y Union and 
throughout the entire school district as many community members wore black to 
support her message.  Katrina is proof that we can all make a difference. 
At this time, we would like to invite  Mr. Michael Cottone  and teacher Jess 
Bronsteen to come tell us a little more about Katarina’s efforts.   
Recommended Action:  Invite Kata up  to receive a certificate and 
congratulations from the Board.

D.   National Blue Ribbon Award – Schroeder Elementary School 
We are pleased to recognize Schroeder Elementary School this evening for their 
designation as a National Blue Ribbon School . This award honors Schroeder as 
one of the very best schools in the United States.  
U.S. Secretary of Education John B. King, Jr. recognized Schroeder in a ceremony in 
Washington, D. C. this past November . Just 3 29 schools from around the country 
were chosen for this honor in 2016. This award affirms the hard work of students, 
staff, parents, and t he Schroeder community.  As Secretary King stated, "National 
Blue Ribbon Schools are proof that we can prepare every child for college and 
meaningful careers.  Your schools are on the cutting edge, pioneering innovative 
educational practices —professional learning communities, project -based learning, 
social and emotional learning, positive behavior systems —making you shining 
examples for your communities, your state and the nation." 
Congratulations for this impressive designation!

E.  Principal Appreciation Month 
Throughout the State of Michigan, February has traditionally been celebrated as 
Principal Appreciation Month, in recognition of the outstanding work of our 
building-level educational leaders. In Troy, we are fortunate to have an 
exemplary group of principals dedicated to the success of every student in the 
District. This evening, we will pass a resolution to commend our elementary and 
secondary principals for their ex ceptional commitment to excellence in the Troy 
School District.  
WHEREAS, energetic and inspiring school leadership is essential if teachers, 
schools and districts are to ensure that all Troy students are college  and career 
ready; and, 
WHEREAS, K-12 school principals play a vital role in the success of students 
and act as the liaison between the school and the community it serves, ensuring 
that parents and the community are aware of student and school achievements; 
and, 
WHEREAS, Troy School District pr incipals are dedicated,  quality educational 
professionals committed to providing sound leadership and guidance to their 
staff and students; and,  
WHEREAS, during this month, we join with educators, parents and students 
throughout Michigan to raise awaren ess of the importance of educational 
leadership, and to recognize and thank all principals working in Troy for their 
exemplary service; 
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED , that the Troy Board of Education 
officially recognizes February as Principal Appreciation Month in Troy and thanks 
our dedicated building leaders for all they do for our students and staff.
