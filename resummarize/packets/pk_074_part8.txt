This proposal, if approved by the electors, will allow the Troy 
School District to continue to levy the number of operating 
mills required for the School District to receive revenues at the

Michigan.

all property, except principal residence and other property exempted by law, 
situated within the Troy School District, County of Oakland, State of Michigan, be 
increased as pro vided in the Michigan Constitution, in the amount of 18.3795 
mills with 18 mills being the maximum allowable levy ($18.00 on each $1,000 of

exempted by law, by 5.99 mills ($5.9 9 on each $1,000 of taxable valuation),

authorized millages which would otherwise expire on December  31, 2015 and

result of the reduction required by the Michigan Constitution? These operating 
millages if approved and levied, would provide estimated revenues to the School 
District of Thirty Three Million Five Hundred Eighty Three Thousand 
($33,583,000) Dollars during the 2016 calendar year, to be used for general
