I-1701 OPTING OUT OF VIDEO PRESENTATIONS 
 The Troy School District may occasionally show video of speeches, 
presentations, and enrichment activities outside the District 
curriculum. These videos are not produced by the District. 
Parents/guardians wishing to opt out of such video presentations 
must notify their child’s building principal /classroom teacher. 
  Parents/guardians will be informed of their right to opt out of any 
video presentations as part of the District’s annual notices to 
parents/guardians. 
  Reasonable and appropriate provisions will be made for students 
whose parents/guardians wish to have them removed from 
classrooms during video presentations.
