A. TSD Mission Moment – Smith Student Fundraising Initiative 
Calen Knight is a sixth grade student at Smith Middle School. She is being 
honored tonight for her efforts to create a charity to  help make her 
community a better place. When Calen was seven, she decided to donate 
some of her stuffed animals and toys to children at Lighthouse of Oakland 
County. She wanted to help kids her age who didn’t have the things she 
did. Then she learned that chil dren needed more—so she started to gather 
clothing, winter coats, anything she discovered there was a need for. 
Eventually, Calen started her own charity, Calen4kids.org and began 
recruiting her classmates and friends to help. 
Tonight, we recognize Calen in our Mission Moment as a leader in 
community outreach and charity for the entire region. Calen, would you 
please come up and tell us a little more about your efforts to help children 
and families through Calen4kids.org? 
RECOMMENDED ACTION:  Calen Knight will give a short presentation on 
her charity and receive a certificate from the Board.

C. Troy Foundation for Educational Excellence Mini-Grant Recipients 
This evening we are pleased to recognize the Troy Foundation for 
Educational Excellence and the work they do to support our schools, our 
students and our staff. The Foundation awards grants to help fund projects 
that support the curriculum in every school in the Troy S chool District. This 
year, the Foundation was able to fund  81 grants for a diverse group of 
projects to help enhance learning for students in every school in the 
District. That kind of support is invaluable. 
The Foundation is made up of volunteers who give of their time and talents 
to raise money and support innovative projects that enrich student 
learning. Again, these projects take place in every school in our District and 
many would not be possible without the resources the Foundation 
provides. 
At this time, I would like to introduce  Al Robinson, President of the Troy 
Foundation for Educational Excellence, who will briefly  recognize the most 
recent group of grant recipients and the many diverse ways they are 
enhancing the education of our Troy School District students.
