 2. RECOGNITION 
  A. Leonard Principal, Dr. Erin Detmer 
We would like to welcome Dr. Erin Detmer as the new principal of Leonard 
Elementary School.  Erin was appointed to her new position at the July 18th 
Board of Education Meeting .  The Board wanted to take this opportunity to 
publicly recognize Erin since she and the Leonard staff were not present at 
the July meeting due to summer vacation.   
Erin has a Bachelor of Science in Elementary Education from Central 
Michigan, a Masters in Reading & Language Arts and a Ph.D. in Reading from 
Oakland University. She served as a Literacy Specialist and Head Teacher at 
Hill Elementary for the past two years and was a Second Grade Teacher at 
both Hill and Leonard prior to that. 
We are very excited about Erin's leadership at Leonard and look forward to a 
great school year!

and welcome any staff members who are present to support Erin.  (Erin will

SCHOOL DISTRICT WINS THE 
CERTIFICATE OF EXCELLENCE AWARD 
Troy School District 
Troy, MI 
RESTON, VA – 2017 
The Association of School Business Officials (ASBO) International has awarded Troy School District  its 
Certificate of Excellence in Financial Reporting for having met or exceeded the program’s high standards for 
financial reporting and accountability. 
The district was recognized for its Comprehensive Annual Financial Report for the fiscal year ending 2016. 
Sponsored by VALIC, the Certificate of Excellence (COE) award confirms the school business office’s 
commitment to financial accountability and transparency.  Recognition through the COE program can help 
strengthen a district’s presentation for bond is suance statements and promotes  high quality financial 
reporting. 
For more than half a century, VALIC, a division of AIG, has served as a leading retirement plan provider for  
K-12   schools and school districts, as well as for higher education, healthcare, government and other  
not-for-profit institutions. As of December 31, 2015, VALIC has more than $83 billion in total assets  
under management and manages plans for nearly 24,000 groups serving more than 2 million  
participants. VALIC represents The Variable Annuity Life Insurance Company and its subsidiaries,  
VALIC Financial Advisors, Inc. and VALIC Retirement Services Company. 
American International Group, Inc. (AIG) is a leading global insurance organization serving customers  
in more than 100 countries and jurisdictions. AIG companies serve commercial, institutional, and  
individual customers through one of the most extensive worldwide property-casualty networks of any  
insurer. In addition, AIG companies are leading providers of life insurance and retirement services in  
the United States. AIG common stock is listed on the New York Stock Exchange and the Tokyo Stock  
Exchange.   
ASBO International , founded in 1910, is a professional organization of 5,000 members that provides 
programs and services to promote the highest standards of school business management pract ices, 
professional growth, and the effective use of education resources. 
For more information about the COE award, visit asbointl.org/COE.

E. Michigan Association of School Boards’ Awards: Honor Board & 
Standard of Excellence Award, President Nancy Philippart -
Master Platinum Award 
We are pleased tonight to present the Troy School District Board of 
Education with several honors that were received this past year as a result 
of their commitment to excellence in governance. T his evening, we are 
recognizing the Troy Board of Education for its Honor Board achievement 
and Standard of Excellence Award, both of which were presented by the 
Michigan Association of School Boards  last June . We are also honoring 
Board President Nancy Philippart for achieving the MASB Master Platinum 
Award, which requires a minimum of 813 education credits . All of these 
honors place the Troy Board of Education as being among a handful of 
other Michigan school district governing bodies that have demonstrated a 
true commitment to student achieveme nt and exemplary leadership. The 
Troy community is fortunate to have seven Board members who are so 
committed to the concept of life-long learning and positive policy making.
