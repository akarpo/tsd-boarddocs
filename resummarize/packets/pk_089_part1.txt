It was moved by Mr. Schmidt and supported by Mrs. Edmunds 
that; 
 
WHEREAS,  the Michigan Legislature has proposed a School 
Aid Budget which allocates $2.5 million for nonpublic schools. 
 
WHEREAS  these funds have been specifically allocated to 
nonpublic schools as “reimbursements” for state-mandated

conduct. 
WHEREAS  these funds could be utilized to “reimburse” 
nonpublic schools for such things as the costs associated with 
employing staff, including noncertified teachers, as well as the 
provision of mentor teachers, and the expenses associated 
with certain specific areas of instruction, such as government 
and history, among other things. 
 
WHEREAS,  Article VIII Sec. 2 of the Michigan Constitution 
specifically prohibits: 
 
- the use of public money to aid or maintain a nonpublic

- the use of public money to support the attendance of any

nonpublic school. 
 
WHEREAS  this appropriation is likely a violation of the 
Michigan Constitution. 
 
 
WHEREAS public schools do not receive similar additional 
funds specifically allocated to reimburse those schools for the 
costs of these same basic purposes.  
 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED  that the Troy Board of 
Education urges the Governor of the State of Michigan to 
VETO Section 152b of the Legislature’s proposed School Aid 
Budget and thereby uphold the Michigan Constitution as well 
as the will of the people, as demonstrated by multiple prior 
ballot initiatives to fund private schools with public funds, all 
of which were rejected by the citizens of this state.
