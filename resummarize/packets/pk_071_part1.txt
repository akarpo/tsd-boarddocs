A. TSD Mission Moment – Schroeder Elementary Students 
 
Over the past several years, Schroeder Elementary has teamed up with 
IA-West and an organization called buildOn to help build schools in 
underprivileged countries. This year, Schroeder is helping to build a school 
in Haiti. Schroeder students have also helped build schools in Nicaragua 
and Nepal. To make this project possible, Schroeder organizes an annual 
Kids 4 K ids Sale to help raise money for building a new school. Two 
Schroeder parents, Mrs. Weallans and Mrs. Pingree , organize and chair 
the event. Schroeder students participate by making numerous donations 
such as toys, athletic equipment, DVD’s, games and many  other gently 
used items. Parents, students and community members are invited to 
shop at the sale, which is one of Schroeder’s most popular events! All of 
the money raised at the Kids 4 Kids Sale is donated to buildOn. Students 
from IA -West travel to the d esignated country to join the construction 
crew and actually participate in building the new school. Every year, IA 
students return to Schroeder and visit classrooms to show pictures of the 
new school. This wonderful event helps show how our elementary 
students can change the world and help those in need.

Teacher Appreciation Week is observed nationally each year in May. This 
year, Teacher Appreciation Week is being celebrated May  4th – May 8th.  At 
tonight’s meeting , the Troy School District and Board of Education would 
like to recognize all of our outstanding teachers by adopting the following 
resolution: 
 
 
WHEREAS, throughout Troy and across the country, teachers inspire, 
nurture and open children’s minds to new ideas,  
 
WHEREAS, teachers promote and strengthen the principles of democratic 
equality, develop leaders and encourage critical thinking; and 
 
WHEREAS, Troy teachers set the bar for excellence in education and 
continually exceed the highest standards in the profession; and 
 
WHEREAS, the goal of Teacher Appreciation Week is to raise national and 
local awareness of teaching excellence in building a better society; now,  
 
THEREFORE BE IT  RESOLVED, that the Troy School District supports 
National Teacher Appreciation Week, May 4-8, 201 5, and encourages our 
schools and community to appropriately recognize the vital and changing 
role of teachers in imagining and creating a better future for society.
