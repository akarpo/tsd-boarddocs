Program Memo of Understanding 
The Women of Tomorrow Mentor &  Scholarship Program is an organization 
whose mission is to inspire, motivate and empower at -risk young women to 
live up to their full potential through a unique mentoring program with 
highly accomplished  professional  women  and scholarship  opportunitie s.  
In order for students in our District to participate in this program, the Board 
is required to enter into an agreement by signing the following Memo of 
Understanding.  At this time it would be appropriate for the Board to pass a 
resolution to sign the attached agreement.

MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING 
The Troy School Board (the "School Board"), whose address is 4400 Livernois, 
Troy, Ml 48098, and the Women of Tomorrow Mentor & Scholarship Program, Detroit 
Metropolitan Area, Inc. (WOT-Metro Detroit) whose address is  17251 West 12 Mile 
Road, Suite 204,  Southfield, MI 48076, or any affiliated entity thereof, have entered 
into this agreement (the "Agreement") on t his 15th day of October, 2013 to implement 
the WOT program described below commencing in October of 2013. 
WITNESSETH 
WHEREAS, The Women of Tomorrow Mentor & Scholarship Program’s mission 
is to inspire, motivate and empower at -risk young women to live up to their full 
potential through a unique mentoring program with highly accomplished professional 
women and scholarship opportunities.  WOT is specifically designed to meet the need s 
of at-risk teenage girls in our nation's public high schools and help them turn obstacles 
1 
4
into opportunities by providing the exposure, motivation, confidence and opportunities 
required to create a better future for themselves, their families, and society. 
WHEREAS,   the WOT and the School   Board   believe that the Women  of 
Tomorrow Mentor & Scholarship Program is of benefit to the students of the Troy 
School District; 
WHEREAS, The Women of Tomorrow Mentor &  Scholarship  Program , Inc.  
provides a program founded and headed by Jennifer Valoppi, author and television 
journalist, in which a group of highly accomplished professional women serve as 
mentors to improve the self-esteem,  academic  performance,  high  school  graduation 
rate and future adult lives of at -risk teenage girls in public high schools. The mentors 
are themselves successful in their respective careers; 
WHEREAS, WOT and School Board wants to provide the WOT program as an 
official School Board program. 
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the promises and benefits 
flowing to each other, and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and 
sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the parties hereto do mutually agree as 
follows: 
AGREEMENT 
A. The parties agree that the foregoing recitals are true and are hereby incorporated 
herein by reference. 
B. Terms and Duties. 
1. This Agreement shall commence on October  15, 2013 and shall 
automatically renew annually until otherwise amended or terminated by the 
parties hereto. This Agreement may be terminated by either party upon 
sixty (60) days prior written notice.  
2. The School Board hereby designates WOT as an "other sch ool official" or 
other applicable state equivalent to satisfy all applicable state requirements 
to be treated as an official School Board program including the ability to 
receive personally-identifiable student information (such as name, grade -
level, date of birth, social security number, school attending, academic 
performance, attendance, academic work completed;  level of  achievement 
records, including grades and standardized achievement test scores; 
attendance data; scores on standardized intelligence, aptitude, and 
psychological tests; health data; family background information; teacher or 
counselor ratings and observations; verified reports of serious or recurrent 
behavior patterns; and any other evidence, knowledge, or information) (the 
"Student Information") and agrees to provide WOT  by October 15, 2013,

with such Student Information because the School Board recognizes WOT 
has a legitimate educational interest in receiving this information in  order  
to operate its programs and to carry out research on the effectiveness of its 
program. The School Board further acknowledges that WOT can provide 
the Student Information to its third party providers. To the extent permitted 
by applicable law, WOT may utilize Student Data (on a de -identified basis) 
provided for additional research alone or in combination with other data 
acquired by WOT.   To the extent WOT creates aggregated data, WOT shall 
own such aggregated data and any inventions, patents, or other intellectual

C. Roles and Responsibilities of WOT. WOT will: 
1. Recruit WOT Mentors into the program and secure their commitment to 
meet with a small group (10 - 15) of at-risk young women ("Mentees") once 
a month for at least one (1) hour per month, throughout the school year on 
the school grounds, during the school day.  
2. WOT will be responsible for securing its Mentors ’ commitment to the 
program and ensuring that all WOT Mentors are qualified to work with 
children. The individuals identified by WOT and provided to the School 
Board, as Mentors of WOT shall be volunteers and shall not be employees of  
WOT.  The Mentors of WOT are required to be under the supervision of 
School District personnel at all times that WOT Mentors are with the 
students.  
3. Orient and train WOT  Mentors with assistance from school personnel where 
necessary. 
4. Orient and support WOT's designated WOT Program Coordinator (a WOT 
employee) who will act as a liaison with the applicable high school(s). 
5. Coordinate scheduling of mentoring sessions. 
6. Coordinate career -focused field trips and specialized college campus visits 
for its Mentees. 
7. WOT will provide the School Board with outcome based evaluations of the 
WOT program using quantifiable and qualitative goals and objectives. 
8. Retain full responsibility for  the management and fiscal control of the WOT 
Program. 
9. Require all volunteer WOT Mentors to complete any required school 
volunteer application and undergo a background check conducted by the 
School Board. 
3 
6
10. Specify program criteria and activities to be performed by the WOT Mentor.  
11. WOT will provide the School Board with a set of criteria for defining at -risk 
young women to assist the School Board in selecting participants. 
D. Roles and Responsibilities of School Board.  School Board will: 
1. Secure participation by senior high schools of its choosing and handle all 
administrative functions related thereto, including securing consents for 
Mentees to attend WOT program sessions during school hours. 
2. Select and provide the students to be involved in the WOT program using 
criteria provided by WOT. 
3. Identify at least one staff member at the high school who  will collect 
paperwork from Mentees, coordinate and provide administrative support for 
mentoring sessions within the school setting ("WOT School Coordinator"). 
4. Provide appropriate space for WOT Mentor(s) to meet with students at the 
high school during the school day. 
5. Conduct any security background check required for volunteers in schools. 
6. Assure that the service location shall be in conformity with any State Uniform 
Building Codes for Public Educational Facilities. 
7. Cooperate in the assignments and orientation, where necessary, of the WOT 
Mentors. 
8. Assure compliance with the purpose of the WOT Program to render 
supportive group mentoring services to students having special needs and 
further, assure that WOT Mentors will not be utilized for any duty o ther than 
working with students. 
9. Assure that only the WOT Program Coordinator may reassign or transfer a 
WOT Mentor from one school/center to another. 
10. Handle all required paperwork for field trips and college visits, and will be 
responsible for all costs related thereto. 
11. The School Board will be required to adhere to all WOT program guidelines 
unless WOT's President provides an express written waiver. 
12. The School Board will cooperate with and facilitate the surveying of students 
in the WOT program to allow WOT to evaluate the effectiveness of the WOT 
4 
7
Mentors and the WOT program. 
13. Each school at which a WOT program is conducted will assign a School 
Board official to supervise the WOT program. As part of such supervision, 
School Board will insure that a sch ool official (or School Board official) will 
attend all WOT program meetings. Such official shall be responsible for all 
legal reporting obligations arising out of such meetings and/or the WOT 
program (i.e., reporting suspicions of incest, Mentee abuse or evidence of a 
crime). The parties hereto expressly acknowledge that WOT shall, in no 
event, be required to make such reports.

2. Scholarships for qualified students will be paid directly to students by the 
WOT Program for those who have been awarded a WOT Scholarship. All 
scholarship decisions are made at WOT's sole discretion.
