B.  CONSIDERATION:   Schools Closed to Open Enrollment for  
                                        2017-2018 
Administration has reviewed the enrollment for elementary, middle, and high 
school attendance areas.  Due to the elementary and middle school  buildings’ 
total student enrollment and physical space constraints, we recommend that 
Bemis, Schroeder and Boulan Park Schools be closed to open enrollment.  Our 
goal for Athens and Troy High has been to maximize each building’s enrollment 
which has resulted in both schools being closed to open enrollment for the past 
several years. Based on current enrollment projections for the 2017-2018 school 
year, it is recommended that Athens and Troy High be closed to open enrollment 
with the exception of not more than (25) twenty -five incoming ninth grade 
students who reside in the Troy High School attendance area, but wish to attend 
Athens High School.  Applications for the (25) twenty-five spots at Athens High 
School will be accepted during a 2-week window at the end of March and early 
April. 
RECOMMENDED ACTION: Adopt the following resolution.

Be it therefore resolved that the Troy Board of Education adopts a resolution to close 
Bemis, Schroeder and Boulan Park Schools as well as Athens and Troy High Schools to 
open enrollment for the 2017-2018 school year, with the exception of not more than 
(25) twenty-five incoming ninth grade students who reside in the Troy High School 
attendance area , but wish to attend Athens High School.  Students with siblings 
attending Athens and Troy High and currently enrolled in grades 9 – 11 will be allowed

only).  Our current estimates for enrollment next year approximate to 728.  Accepting 15 applications during the 
initial application process will bring us extremely close to an ideal total student population at Baker, while allowing
