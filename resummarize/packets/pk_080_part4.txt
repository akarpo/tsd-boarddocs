B. CONSIDERATION:   Weiming International Student Pilot 
Mark Dziatczak , Deputy Superintendent of Teaching and Learning,  has 
negotiated a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU ) between the Troy 
School District and Weiming Education Group.  This MOU outlines a 
Chinese and American student exchange program between Beijing 
Weiming Education Group and the Troy School District. The MOU outlines 
the opportunity for eligible Chinese stu dents from Weiming Education 
Group (Chinese Foreign Students)  to participate in a three (3 ) + one (1) 
year program wherein the eligible Chinese Foreign Students will study for 
three (3) years at Weiming schools in China and subsequently transfer to 
the Troy School District for one (1) year. Students from the Troy School 
District would also have the opportunity to study at one of Weiming’s high 
schools for summer/winter camps and/or as exchange students.  The goal 
of this program is to encourage international education for Chinese and 
American students, teachers , and administrators in order to create a 
greater understanding between the two cultures, thus preparing students 
for a global economy .  Furthermore, this program is aligned to recent 
Oakland County initiatives designed to establish economic partnerships in 
China and to promote opportunities for Oakland County businesses and 
ventures in China.

as students in the Troy School District.
