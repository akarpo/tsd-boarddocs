A.   TSD Mission Moment — Larson 8th Grade Stream Leaders Program 
Sandra Bammel, 8th grade science teacher at Larson Middle School is the advisor for 
the Stream Leaders Program at Larson.  Stream Leaders is a program sponsored by 
the Clinton River Watershed Council that provides students the opportunity to 
participate in water quality monitoring, data interpretation, and citizen action within 
their community. The program began at Larson in the fall of 2013 and continues to 
evolve and grow over time. All 8th grade science students at Larson Middle School 
participate in this program.  Four of our 8th grade science students are with us tonight 
to share some information about their involvement in this program.

Post-Secondary Attainment 
Learning along with Kris Griffor, Assistant Superin tendent, 
Elementary Instruction, provided a brief presentati on on 
the students earning postsecondary credentials prio r to 
graduation from high school and how they have signi ficant 
advantages over their peers who do not have those 
credentials.  They are more likely to be successful  in 
academic studies beyond high school and more likely  to 
persist and obtain a postsecondary degree.  Because  of 
this, the Post-Secondary credit attainment level fo r school 
districts is a key performance indicator of the suc cess of 
the students in a district.  The number and percent age of 
students participating in the traditional high scho ol 
advanced placement programs are reviewed annually. 
Advanced placement classes can qualify students for  
college waivers and credit upon their matriculation  to the 
college or university of their choice.  Additionall y, the 
annual Equity and Access scores are tracked as the 
statistics clarify the number of graduates successf ully 
taking advantage of advanced placement classes. 
Post-secondary opportunities also exist at the Dist rict’s 
alternative high schools.  At Niles, students are e ncouraged 
to take college classes at Oakland Community Colleg e prior 
to graduation through their dual enrollment program .  At 
the International Academy, students enroll in chall enging 
courses intended to make them eligible for an IB Di ploma, 
which qualifies them for college waivers and credit  at 
colleges and universities similar to advanced place ment 
classes.  The report provided information regarding  the 
District’s progress toward the goal of expanding ea rly 
postsecondary opportunities for as many students as  
possible. 
There was Board discussion on dual enrollment and i f it is 
offered at all high schools.  Mr. Dziatczak indicat ed that 
dual enrollment is offered at all high schools.  Th ere was 
also discussion on how the District tracks AP stude nts and 
how many students take the exam.
