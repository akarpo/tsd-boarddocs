accepted into the prestigious Teachers’ College at Columbia University 
August Writing Institute.

 F.  National Teacher Appreciation Week 
 
Teacher Appreciation Week is observed nationally each year in May. This year, 
Teacher Appreciation Week was celebrated May 8th – May 12th.  At tonight’s 
meeting, the Troy School District and Board of Education would like to 
recognize all of our outstanding teachers by adopting the following resolution: 
 
 
WHEREAS, throughout Troy and across the country, teachers inspire, 
nurture and open children’s minds to new ideas,  
 
WHEREAS, teachers promote and strengthen the principles of democratic 
equality, develop leaders and encourage critical thinking; and 
 
WHEREAS, Troy teachers set the bar for excellence in education and 
continually exceed the highest standards in the profession; and 
 
WHEREAS, the goal of Teacher Appreciation Week is to raise national and 
local awareness of teaching excellence in building a better society; now,  
 
THEREFORE BE IT  RESOLVED, that the Troy School District supports 
National Teacher Appreciation Week, May 8-12, 2017, and encourages our 
schools and community to appropriately recognize the vital and changing role 
of teachers in imagining and creating a better future for society.

This past summer, a group of interested teache rs from 
Smith Middle School met to develop a “homeroom” for 
all Smith students.  As a building, Smith recognized the 
need to develop a stronger bond between staff and 
students and students between grade levels.  A plan was 
made to implement a 7 -period day 
once a month and 
have “U -Knighted” time during the 7 th hour.  Students 
were arranged by grade level and then divided amongst 
41 teachers resulting in approximately 17 students in 
each group. The committee meets before each U -
Knighted Hour and develops a le sson plan for the groups.  
The focus has been on character development, diversity, 
motivation, community, goal setting, and FUN!!!   This 
program has been well -received by both students and 
staff alike.  Assistant Principal  Amy Buchanan, Smith 
Middle Schoo l Counselor Jen Hartenburg, and social 
studies teacher Glen Rexer  were invited up to explain  
more about th eir program. They shared a brief 
PowerPoint presentation with the Board, which included 
testimonials from several Smith students.
