Background: 
As part of the 2004 Bond issue, the district planned to replace the district laptops in two 
refresh cycles. The first refresh deployed 1500 laptops in 2007.  
The useful life of a laptop is 4 years d ue to the increasing technical specifications of 
new applications and operating systems. In addition , the cost to maintain these older 
laptops is continually increasing and the parts are more difficult to obtain.  
The district began the review and pilot o f new technologies for the second rollout during 
the 2010-11 school year. The technology staff in coordination wit h district staff piloted 
the iPad challenge and tested an internal cloud computing option.  
The iPad Challenge was well received and teachers  reported a higher level of 
engagement and learning when the devices were integrated into the lessons. The iPads 
were presented at the April Board meeting  
The Troy Cloud will be utilized to elongate the life of laptops from the 2007 deployment 
at the Middle and High Schools. This will enable the district to provide an updated 
experience on the older laptops. The district will retain the laptops being replace d at the 
Elementary for parts.
