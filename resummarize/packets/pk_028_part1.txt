1. ELECTION OF ACTING CHAIRPERSON  
The first order of business is to el ect an acting chairperson who will 
conduct the meeting until such time as a president has been elected.  
Past practice has been that the previous year’s president  conducts 
this part of the meeting. 
2. APPOINTMENT OF TEMPORARY SECRETARY 
 The second order of business is for the acting chairperson to 
 appoint Judy Holmes to act as temp orary secretary until such time as the 
 secretary to the Board has been elected. 
3. ELECTION OF PRESIDENT  
The acting chairperson declares nomi nations in order for the office of 
president of the board of education (please note, nominations need not 
be seconded; and a motion and support are made to close the 
nomination.)  Please understand that four votes are required for election. 
4. ELECTION OF VICE PRESIDENT  
The newly elected president  declares nominations in order for the 
office of vice president, following the same procedures as outlined for the 
election of president. 
5. ELECTION OF A SECRETARY  
The president declares nominations in  order for the office of secretary, 
following the same procedures as outlined for the election of president. 
6. APPOINTMENT OF TREASURER  
According to Board Policy, the trea surer is appointed annually by the 
Board as a nonvoting officer.  The assistant superintendent of business 
services has served as treasurer of the Board.  It is my recommendation 
that this procedure continue.

work of a very important group of individuals: The Board of

Troy is truly fortunate to be governed by Board members who are 
tireless, professional, and passionate about improving the 
opportunities of our students. The successes that we receive in the 
Troy School District stem in large part from the support and guidance 
we receive from our Board member s. The Troy Board of Education 
works very hard to e nsure that the entire district is committed to 
promoting student achievement . Tonight, we would like to recognize 
Board of Education  members with a gift both meaningful and 
heartfelt. One of our esteemed art teachers, Heather Kelly, who was 
honored at Art Prize this year, has created seven fabulous canvas 
pieces that are meant to symbolize our appreciation for your service
