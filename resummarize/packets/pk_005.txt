2. RECOGNITION 
 A.  2009-2010  Outstanding Support Staff Award  – Pam Byrd 
In an effort to recognize the valuable contribution of all staff members, the Troy School 
District, in conjunction with the Troy Educational Secretaries Association (TESA) and the 
Troy Educational Support Personnel Association (TESPA), invited all employees to nominate 
candidates for the District's Outstanding Support Staff Award.  A committee comprised of 
TESA, TESPA, and non-represented personnel selected Pam Byrd, Secretary, at Hamilton 
Elementary School, as the overall winner from nine nominations.  
This evening, it is with great pleasure to recognize Pam Byrd for her exemplary service to 
the Troy School District. 
RECOMMENDATION:  Adopt the following resolution. 
MOVED BY ____________________________ 
SECONDED BY __________________________ 
RESOLUTION 
Pamela Byrd 
Secretary 
Hamilton Elementary School 
2009-2010 Outstanding Support Staff Award 
Whereas, Pam Byrd started as a Teacher’s Assistant for the Troy School District in September 
of 1995, and has served as a Secretary since May of 1999; and  
Whereas, Pam Byrd has been selected the recipient of the Troy School District's 2009 -2010 
Outstanding Support Staff Award; and  
Whereas, Pam's commitment, initiative, strong work ethic, enthusiasm, willingness to assist 
others, and winning attitude makes her an asset to the District and earned her this award; and  
Whereas, Pam continuously exhibits these personal qualities which have endeared her to the 
students and staff of Hamilton Elementary School and her coworkers of the Troy School District. 
Now Therefore Be It Resolved that the Troy School District Board of Education commends 
Pamela Byrd for her selection as the recipient of the 2009 -2010 Outstanding Support Staff 
Award, and for her exceptional service to the students and staff of the Troy School District.  
Signed this fourth day of May, 2010.

6.  PERSONNEL 
 A.  CONSIDERATION:  Administrative Appointment – Assistant 
   Superintendent, Elementary Instruction 
I am pleased to recommend the appointment of Janet Keeling  to the 
position of Assistant Superintendent, Elementary Instruction.  Mrs. 
Keeling was hired as a teacher in the district in November 1977.   She 
became a building principal at Morse Elementary in  1988 and then 
Bemis Elementary in 1999.  With your approval, Mrs. Janet Keeling  will 
begin her duties as Assistant Superintendent, Elementary Instruction 
effective July 1, 2010.

BE IT THEREFORE RESOLVED  that the Board of Education issues a two -year 
probationary administrative contract without tenure in the administrative 
position to Mrs. Janet Keeling , who will begin her duties as Assistant 
Superintendent, Elementary Instruction effective July 1, 2010.
