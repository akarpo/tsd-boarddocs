       A.  2015–2016 National Merit Scholarship Semifinalists  
This year, more than one million students, nationwide, from some 22,000 high schools, 
entered the National Merit Scholarship Program; however, only 16,000 of these seniors 
are selected to be semifinalists.  This evening, we will be passing a resolution to 
commend and congratulate our sixty-eight National Merit Scholarship Semifinalists. This 
is a remarkable number of semifinalists and a tribute to the staff and parents of the 
Troy School District.  Copies of this resolution were presented to the students at a 
reception on October 14th.

B. TSD Mission Moment – Troy High Student/Build On 
Tonight, as part of our Troy School District Mission moment, we recognize 
Nithya (Nith-ee-ya) Ramadurai for her efforts to make the world a better 
place here in Troy, across the Metro Detroit region and as far away as Haiti. 
As a 17-year-old senior, Nithya is president of the Build On chapter at Troy 
High School. Build On is an organization that empowers yo uth to transform 
their neighborhoods and the world through intensive community service. 
Nithya volunteers monthly here in Metro Detroit and is a state 
representative for the entire organization. But what really transformed her 
was the opportunity to travel  to Haiti with Build On, on a mission to build 
schools for students there. There are only enough schools to serve a 
quarter of the school age population in Haiti, and Nithya is helping to 
change that. 
That experience was her first exposure to the plight o f underprivileged in 
third-world countries and solidified her desire to spend her life giving back . 
Nithya plans a future caree r with Doctors without Borders where she can 
use her skills and talents to help those struggling with health issues across 
the globe. 
Her accomplishments at the age of 17 are too numerous to mention, but 
tonight we honor Nithya for her hard work and dedication for those less 
fortunate.
