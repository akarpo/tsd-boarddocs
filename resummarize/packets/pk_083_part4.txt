The following factors were considered in making the recommendations for the Schools of Choice Program for  
2016-2017 school year: 
 Current enrollment 
 Enrollment projections, patterns and trends 
 Numbers and percentages of Schools of Choice students currently in the district 
 Projected number of Schools of Choice siblings who would be age-eligible to enter the district in 2016-2017 
if the decision is made to admit a “Limited” number of kindergarten, first and sixth through eighth grade  
students (Baker Middle School) under Schools of Choice 
 Available elementary classrooms 
 Recommendations from Facilities Study Team 
After analyzing the data and factors referenced above, we recommend that the district: 
 Use Fred Ignatovich’s 1.5 enrollment projection numbers, with his recommended formula (most likely + 
low/2) as the foundation for determining the number of Schools of Choice (SOC) students to admit for the 
2016-2017 school year.  Please note when reviewing “Document 1,” that our “actual” enrollment as of the 
February Count for both kindergarten and first grade students was above both Mr. Ignatovich’s “Most 
Likely” and “Low” enrollment 2015-16 projections. 
 Accept SOC applications for 100 Kindergarten and 30 First Grade students.   Based upon the number of 
applications to kindergarten received for the 2015-2016 school year, we anticipate attracting more than 100 
applicants.  This would allow us to admit 100 kindergartners initially, with the option of admitting additional 
students, if space allows, in the third week of August.  While this is quite close to the opening of school, we 
have found in the past that some families still welcome the opportunity to enroll their child in the Troy 
School District.    
 Limit Schools of Choice kindergarten applicants to the full-day kindergarten program.   
 Accept SOC applications for 15 (total) 6th, 7th and 8th grade students to attend Baker Middle School.  
This will accommodate “Sibling Preference” students for current attending SOC students.  Our estimate for 
enrollment next year approximates to 727.  Accepting 15 applications during the initial application process 
will bring us extremely close to an ideal total population at Baker, while allowing an option to admit 
additional students in August if space permits.

the foundation for determining the number of Schools of Choice (SOC) students to admit for the 2016-2017 school 
year.  Please note when reviewing “Document 1,” that our “actual” enrollment as of the February Count for both 
kindergarten and first grade students was above both Mr. Ignatovich’s “Most Likely” and “Low” enrollment 2015-
16 projections.
 Accept SOC applications for 100 Kindergarten and 30 First Grade students.   Based upon the number of 
applications to kindergarten received for the 2015-2016 school year, we anticipate attracting more than 100 
applicants.  This would allow us to admit 100 kindergartners initially, with the option of admitting additional 
students, if space allows, in the third week of August.  While this is quite close to the opening of school, we have 
found in the past that some families still welcome the opportunity to enroll their child in the Troy School District.   
 Limit Schools of Choice kindergarten applicants to the full-day kindergarten program.  
 Accept “Sibling Preference” applications only for siblings of current SOC students (to attend Baker Middle School 
only).  Our current estimates for enrollment next year approximate to 728.  Accepting 15 applications during the 
initial application process will bring us extremely close to an ideal total student population at Baker, while allowing 
an option to admit more students in August if space permits.
