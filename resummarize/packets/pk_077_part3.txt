C.   REPORT:  Post-Secondary Attainment Update 
Students earning postsecondary credentials prior to graduation from 
high school enjoy significant advantages over their peers without 
those credentials.  They are more likely to be successful in academic 
studies beyond high school and more likely to persis t and obtain a 
postsecondary degree.  Because of this, the Post -Secondary credit 
attainment level for school districts is a key performance indicator of 
the success of the students in a district.   
Consequently, the number and percentage of students parti cipating 
in the traditional high school advanced placement programs are 
reviewed annually. These advanced placement classes can qualify 
students for college waivers and credit upon their matriculation to 
the college or university of their choice.  Additionally, we track the 
annual Equity and Access scores as these statistics further clarify the 
number of graduates successfully taking advantage of advanced 
placement classes. 
Post-secondary opportunities also exist at our alternative high 
schools.  At Niles , students are encourage d to take college classes 
at Oakland Community College prior to graduation through their 
dual enrollment program.  At the International Academy, students 
enroll in challenging courses intended to make them eligible for a n 
IB Diploma, which qualifies them for college waivers and credit at 
colleges and universities similar to advanced placement classes. 
This evening’s report will provide information regarding the Troy 
School District’s progress toward the goal of expanding early 
postsecondary opportunities for as many students as possible.

Subject:  Trane Compressor R’newal™ for  CenTraVac® 
Trane’s CenTraVac® Compressor R’newal service is a major inspection and maintenance program that brings the 
chiller compressor back to like-new condition.  The CenTraVac Compressor R'newal program is backed with a 
Trane warranty covering compressor motor, bearing and lubrication system failures. This unique warranty reflects 
Trane’s confidence in our compressors as well as our factory-authorized service technicians who work on them. 
This CenTraVac Compressor R'newal program addresses the following major components:  
Gaskets and seals – Both internal and external elastomer seals give up elasticity and harden with time. The rate 
of deterioration depends on length of service, temperature, and operating conditions. Gasket and seal wear 
ultimately results in chiller efficiency loss, refrigerant loss and loss of lubrication capability.   
Bearings – Individual unit operating conditions and certain site conditions can influence bearing permanence.  
These factors can result in bearing damage and compressor failure. 
Compressor Motors – Over time compressor motors can develop flaws in the rotor and stator assemblies. These 
flaws, if left uncorrected, can result in compressor motor failure.  
Trane Building Services uses the CenTraVac Compressor Evaluator to predict when a compressor should be 
considered for R’newal.  The CenTraVac Compressor Evaluator takes into account run hours, starts, and seasons 
of operation to derive the service threshold indicator used in this evaluation process.  
We have assessed your CenTraVac compressor with the Evaluator and have determined a service threshold 
indicator of >100%. Trane recommends that any compressor above 85% be considered for the R'newal program 
and compressors above 100% be immediately scheduled for the R’newal program.  
Per the attached proposal, it is our recommendation that your chiller be scheduled for a CenTraVac Compressor
