Re: Elementary free field microphone replacement  
As discussed in July, the District is required to replace many pieces of wireless microphone equipment due 
to FCC action prohibiting the use of certain radio frequencies that have been in public use for many years.  
Based on conversations with teachers, principals, and administration, this project will replace free field voice 
amplification systems in all lower elementary classrooms (K -2) and provide portable speaker/microphone 
systems to be available via check out for upper elementary.  We do not have sufficient funds to replace every 
system, and usage patterns from the buildings indicate that this hybrid solution is an appropriate 
compromise.  We have access to extra systems as needed where medically indicated.  
The District released bid #9687 on July 16, 2010, with bids opened on August 3.  We received four valid 
responses and one alternate bid for technology that was tested but not approved for th e project.   
The compliant low bid response from VSC, Inc is complete and the vendor has delivered many previous 
projects for the District as requested, and I recommend award to this vendor.  We secured unit pricing on 
various microphones and other compon ents that we will not purchase at this time, so the award amount is 
slightly less than the bid tabulation.
