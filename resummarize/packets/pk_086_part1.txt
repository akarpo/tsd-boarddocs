A.   TSD Mission Moment—Baker Middle School Shark Tank Winners 
Tonight we honor a special group of students and teachers at Baker Middle School 
who have created innovative project ideas and marketing plans that they pitched 
to potential “would-be investors” in a Shark Tank format. 
These creative and well -thought out projects solved real world problems and 
provided solutions that could make it in the marketplace. Under the guidance of 
teachers Anne Costello, Lisa Gaglio and Colin Quinn, 8th grade students developed 
their ideas, researched feasibility and production and came up with a plan to sell 
their products to the masses. Experts from the business community as well as 
administrators and members of the Board of Education served as “Sharks,” asking 
challenging questions to the would -be inventors and requiring them to provide 
proof of concept. 
Baker Shark Tank is truly an opportunity for learning to come to life for our 
students and for them to put their skills to the test in a real world situation. 
At this time, we would like to call up teachers, Anne Costello and Lisa Gaglio to tell 
us more about this innovative project.

 D.  National Teacher Appreciation Week 
Teacher Appreciation Week is observed nationally each year in May. This year, 
Teacher Appreciation Week was celebrated May 2nd – May 6th.  At tonight’s 
meeting, the Troy School District and Board of Education would like to 
recognize all of our outstanding teachers by adopting the following resolution: 
WHEREAS, throughout Troy and across the country, teachers inspire, 
nurture and open children’s minds to new ideas,  
WHEREAS, teachers promote and strengthen the principles of democratic 
equality, develop leaders and encourage critical thinking; and 
WHEREAS, Troy teachers set the bar for excellence in education and 
continually exceed the highest standards in the profession; and 
WHEREAS, the goal of Teacher Appreciation Week is to raise national and 
local awareness of teaching excellence in building a better society; now,  
THEREFORE BE IT  RESOLVED, that the Troy School District supports 
National Teacher Appreciation Week, May 2-6, 201 6, and encourages our 
schools and community to appropriately recognize the vital and changing role 
of teachers in imagining and creating a better future for society.

E. Troy Foundation for Educational Excellence Mini-Grant Recipients 
This evening we are pleased to recognize the Troy Foundation for 
Educational Excellence and the work they do to support our schools, our 
students and our staff. The Foundation awards grants to help fund projects 
that support the curriculum in every school in the Troy S chool District. This 
year, the Foundation was able to fund  79 grants for a diverse group of 
projects to help enhance learning for students in every school in the District. 
That kind of support is invaluable. 
The Foundation is made up of volunteers who give of their time and talents 
to raise money and support innovative projects that enrich student learning. 
Again, these projects take place in every school in our District and many 
would not be possible without the resources the Foundation provides.
