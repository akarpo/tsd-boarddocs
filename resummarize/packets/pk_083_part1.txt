A.   TSD Mission Moment—Boulan Park Middle School Effective Teens 
Tonight we honor a special group of students and teachers who have worked to 
hone leadership skills while volunteering their time to help younger students find 
success. The Boulan Park Middle School Effective Teens is made up of 7th and 8th 
grade students who practice the 7 Habits of Highly Effective Teens, participate in 
service learning experiences, and create student-driven Genius Hour projects.  
Students volunteer weekly at Bemis Elementary and serve as mentors to   
elementary students with iPad activities, math, art, writing, science, and reading. 
Effective Teens volunteers learn how to better navigate academic situations and 
how to plan and modify lessons for the youngsters they have in their care. They 
also practice flexibili ty and di fferentiation skills. They analyze and reflect upon 
their experience in a student-led problem-solving debriefing session afterward.  
Genius Hour is a student -driven, project -based learning opportunity. Students 
will spend one to two class periods each wee k researching and working on a 
project that interests them. Genius Hour culminates in student presentations to 
the other Effective Teens classes in the Troy School District at the Genius Hour 
Sharing Event field trip in May. 
At this time, we would like to  call up teachers , Mary Typinski and Michelle Slaviero, 
who teach these classes and lead this impressive group as well as the students 
involved to be recognized.

B. Sodexo Healthy High School Challenge 
At this time, we would like to invite Gayle Moran, Director of Food Services 
for Sodexo to recognize two of our high schools for their success with the 
Healthy High School Challenge. 
The Healthy High School Challenge is a national event aimed at promoting 
healthy lunch and snack options in the cafeteria.  High school nutrition 
programs managed by Sodexo around the county competed in the 
challenge, earnin g points for meals and healthy a la carte snacks and 
beverages purchased by students.  The more healthy meals and healthy 
snacks and beverages a school sold during the challen ge, the more points 
they earned, while competing for grant money as prizes.    
Gayle, would you please come forward to explain how our high schools 
fared and honor those who will be receiving grant money tonight?

budget.
