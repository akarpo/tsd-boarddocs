B.  12 Years of Service to the Troy Board of Education -  
Todd Miletti 
This evening, we would like to take a moment to recognize one of our own 
Board members, Todd Miletti, who is retiring from the Troy Board of Education 
and this evening is his final meeting. Todd is leaving us after selflessly giving 
12 years of service to our students, staff and families.  
Aside from being an elected position, m ost of you know that serving on the 
Board of Education is also a volunteer position, and it requires a great deal of 
time, effort and commitment. Mr. Miletti has been an  exemplary trustee and 
has done a remarkable service to past and future generations of Troy 
students. We know this is not goodbye, and he will no doubt be active in the 
greater Troy community…. but as for this Board, will miss him. 
Recommended Action:  Present certificate.
