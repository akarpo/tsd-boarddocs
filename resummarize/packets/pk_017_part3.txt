Re: SMART Interactive Whiteboards project purchase  
At the March 2, 2010 Board of Education meeting, the Board authorized the purchase of 100 Smart Boards 
to Smart Ed Services as the low bid response from a February 2010 bid  process; that equipment was installed 
during the summer of 2010.  The bid documents required the vendor to honor that volume discount pricing 
for one calendar year from the date of award.   I have negotiated a two week extension of the pricing to 
accommodate our Board meeting schedule.  
During the Smart Board project, many buildings have secured building internal account funds or PTO 
donations to purchase additional boards .  I therefore sent a “last chance” survey to buildings asking if they 
had plans to purchase additional boards prior to the expiration of the current bid pricing.   Bemis, Hill, 
Martell, Schroeder, Troy Union, and Wass elementaries, along with Athens, Troy High, and IA East, have all 
indicated that they have sufficient funds to acquire a total of 41 more Smart Board systems, for a total cost of 
$162,523.78.  These boards will be installed and teachers trained prior to the end of the school year.  
NOTE:  The Qomo document camera for secondary rooms  is less expensive from City Animation Inc. o n the 
statewide REMC contract than from Smart Ed and was not included in the original award.  Elementary 
schools do not require this piece of equipment because they already have document cameras and carts in 
their classrooms from a previous bond project.   Please see the attached spreadsheet for parts lists.   
All of these purchases are from either building funds or PTO sources ; none of them will be using bond 
funds.  Instead of a district-wide project, this is simply a collection of individual building purchases 
coordinated centrally for economy of scale, volume pricing, and consistency of installation.   
In summary, the requests for consideration are:  
 Approve the purchase of 23 Qomo document cameras from City Animation from the REMC contract 
in the amount of $22,747.00. 
 Approve the purchase of 41 Smart Board systems, including installation, from Smart Ed Services as 
an extension of the March 2, 2010 Board of Education award in the amount of $139,776.78.   
The total project cost will be $162,573.78 from building funding sources.  
This item requires Board action due to the dollar amount s of the purchases; please place this on the March 1, 
2011 agenda.  Thank you for your attention to this matter.

resolution each school year if they intend to participate in a Schools of 
Choice program.  It is anticipated that seats will be available for the 
2011-2012 school year for grades 6 - 8 at Baker Middle School.  It would 
be appropriate at this time to cons ider adopting a Schools of Choice 
Section 105 and Section 105c resolution for nonresident 6 th – 8 th grade 
students who attend another school district within Oakland County 
(Section 105) or if the student attends a district in another county, if that 
intermediate school district is cont iguous with the Oakland Intermediate 
School District boundaries (Section 105c).
