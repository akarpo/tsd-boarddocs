Re: Lusk Albertson Policy Service - Updated 
Dear Mr. West: 
 We are pleased the Troy School District is considering retaining Lusk Albertson School 
Policy Services (LA) to help prepare its Board bylaws, policies, and administrative regulations .  
It was my pleasure t o meet with the Board of Education on February 2, 2016 to discuss our 
service.  If the District wishes to move forward, to confirm the terms of LA’s engagement for 
this project, please counter-sign where indicated below and forward a copy to my attention. 
 LA Bylaws, Policies, and Administrative Regulations.  LA will provide the District with a 
standard set of bylaws, policies , and administrative regulations.  As discussed, LA’s standard 
bylaws, policies , and administrative regulations  are much more concise than the District’s 
current version.  Among other things, LA’s documents do not attempt to re -state the law, as the 
District is obligated to follow the law regardless of its policies and administrative regulations. 
 We can deliver the standard bylaws and policies upon engagement.  We are still in the 
process of completing our standard administrative regulations, which we anticipate will be 
finished by the end of this month .  LA will also obtain and incorporate input from the Board of 
Education and Administration as necessary to finalize the District’s documents. 
 Copyrighting and Branding.  The District understands and acknowledges the efforts 
undertaken by LA to create model bylaws, policies, and administrative regulations , as well as 
LA’s intellectual property rights in those products .  In recognition of this fact, the District agrees 
that all bylaws, policies and administrative regulations prepared by LA  will be copyrighted and 
will be and remain the property of LA , even if such model policies are  modified consistent with 
District input. The District understands that it will have LA’s license to use them .  It is expressly 
understood and agreed that t he District will continue to have license to use bylaws, policies, and 
administrative regulations cr eated as a result of this agreement even if the District decides, at 
some point in the future, to discontinue its relationship with LA’s School Policy Service.  To this 
end, it is understood and agreed that if the District invokes the opt-out provision provided herein,

$1,500.00 annually.  The cost of updates to administrative regulations is $2,500.00 annually.  
The District will be billed for annual updates on or about December 1st of every year.  The 
District will receive advance notice of any price increases. 
 Opt-Out.  The District may elect to opt -out of the LA Policy Service by providing LA
