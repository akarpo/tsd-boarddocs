The  American  Mathematics  Competitions  (AMC)  is 
sponsored  by  the  American  Mathematics  Society,  which 
is  dedicated  to  strengthening  the  mathematical 
capabilities  of  our  nation’s  youth,  the  AMC  program 
identifies,  recognizes,  and  rewards  excellence  in 
mathematics  through  a  series  of  national  contests.  The 
AMC  12  is  a  25‐question,  75‐minute,  multiple  choice 
examination  in  high  school  mathematics  designed  to 
promote the development and enhancement of problem‐
solving  skills.  It  covers  the  entire  high  school  curriculum 
including trigonometry, advanced algebra, and advanced 
geometry,  excluding  calculus.  This  competition  is 
administered  throughout  the  country  to  more  than 
350,000  students  and  provides  an  opportunity  for  high 
school  students  to  develop  positive  attitudes  towards 
analytical  thinking  and  mathematics  that  can  assist  in 
future careers. This is the first in a series of competitions 
that  eventually  lead  all  the  way  to  the  International 
Mathematical Olympiad. This Boulan student took 1st in

allow the Troy School District to levy a building

be used to make improvements and repairs to the

the expenditure of the building and site sinking fund 
tax proceeds must be audited, and the proceeds 
cannot be used for teacher, administrator or 
employee salaries, maintenance or other operating 
expenses.

$1,000 of taxable valuation), for a period of ten (10) years, from July 1, 
2018 through June 30, 2028, to create a building and site sinking fund to be 
used for the purpose of the purchase of real estate for sites for, and the 
construction or repair of, school buildings, for school security 
improvements, or for the acquisition or upgrading of technology, and for 
other purposes, to the extent permitted by law?  This millage would provide 
estimated revenues to the Troy School Distri ct of approximately Three

both Athens and Troy High School gymnasiums and outdoor stadiums, it became apparent that 
the cost of this equipment was above the Michigan Department of Education’s Competitive 
Bid Threshold; hence, a formal bid was processed.

required sound system equipment; t hree responses were received. After analyzing the 
information provided in the proposals with Mike Jolly, Athletic Director, the lowest qualified 
bidder was Hardy & Son’s Sign Service.   
 
 It is therefore recommended that Hardy & Son’s Sign Service be awarded BID 9843 –Sound
