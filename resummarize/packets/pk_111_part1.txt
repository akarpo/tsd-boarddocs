2. RECOGNITION 
A. TSD Mission Moment—Outreach to Parkland Students 
Tonight, in our Mission Moment, we honor students and staff at Troy High School, 
Athens High School, Boulan Park Middle School and Larson Middle School, who 
took it upon themselves to reach out in a show of support to students at Marjory 
Stoneman Douglas High School in Parkland, Florida, the scene of a deadly school 
shooting last month. 
At Troy High and Boulan Park, our students, staff and teachers wrote hundreds of 
letters and cards to their counterparts in Florida, sending messages of love, prayers 
and positive thoughts. At Athens, students and art teachers sent student artwork 
and uplifting messages. And at Larson, the Craft Club made a banner in a show of 
support that could be hung at the school to let Stoneman Douglas students know 
that people as far away as Troy were thinking of them.  
Though there is no way to take away the pain that students in Florida are feeling, 
we are proud that our students and staff took the initiative to be part of the healing 
process and decided to reach out and offer some comfort. 
Students and staff, will you please come up when your name is called? 
Recommended Action:  Board presents certificates to students and staff

B. State School of Character—Larson Middle School  
We also honor Larson Middle School this evening for being named a 2018 
State School of Character.  
A select few schools across the country are chosen each year as a State School 
of Character through the implementation of 11 core principles, which include 
promoting good character, culture and ethics ; fostering student self -
motivation, giving students the opportunity for moral action and engaging 
families and community member s as partners in character -building efforts. 
Larson Middle School was praised for the high quality of work in all 11 principle 
areas and the school’s application has now been forwarded on for 
consideration as a National School of Character, which will be announced later 
this Spring. 
We would also like to commend Wass Elementary School, which was named 
a 2018 Honorable Mention School of Character. Wass was strongly encouraged 
to re-apply for the state designation next Fall.  
At this time, we would like to invite Larson Middle School Principal Joe Duda 
and Teacher Charlie Tull to come up and receive a certificate from the Board 
of Education.
