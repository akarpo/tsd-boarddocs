SUBJECT:   Schools of Choice Recommendations for the
2018-2019 School Year
The following factors were considered in making a recommendation for a Schools of Choice Program for 2018-2019 
school year:
• Current enrollment across the Troy School District
• Enrollment projections, patterns and trends
• Numbers and percentages of Schools of Choice students currently in the district
• Projected number of Schools of Choice siblings who would be age-eligible to enter the district in 2018-2019 if the 
decision is made to admit a “Limited” number of kindergarten, first and sixth through eighth grade students under 
Schools of Choice
• Available elementary classrooms
After analyzing the data and factors referenced above, we recommend that the district:
• Use Fred Ignatovich’s 1.5 enrollment projection numbers, with his recommended formula (most likely + low/2) as 
the foundation for determining the number of Schools of Choice (SOC) students to admit for the 2018-2019 school 
year. 
• Accept SOC applications for 40 Kindergarten, 3 First Grade and 14 Middle School students. Based upon the 
number of applications to kindergarten received for the 2017-2018 school year, we anticipate attracting many more 
than 50 applicants.  This would allow the District to admit 40 kindergartners initially, with the option of admitting 
additional students, if space allows, in the third week of August.  
• Middle school students would be placed in a building according to available space.

Beth, 
We received, opened and publicly read bids and proposals for maintenance of the District’s fiber cabling 
at 2:30 P.M. on January 8, 2018. The following is a summary of the evaluation and our recommendation. 
Three proposals were received: Amcomm, Fiber Link and Turnkey Network Solutions.  Several voluntary 
alternates were provided by Fiber Link.  The initial results were tabulated as follows: 
Proposal Responses 
Bidder  Base Bid  
Amcomm  $ 75,791.00  
Fiber Link, Inc.  $ 45,865.00 
Turnkey Network Solutions  $ 29,651.62  
All proposals were evaluated for compliance with the RFP requirements, pricing, technical solution and 
acceptance of terms and conditions of the RFP. All Bidders provided compliant proposals and any 
exceptions or clarifications by the bidders were deemed acceptable.  While all bids were compliant, 
there were variations in interpretations of the bid items.  As a result, we requested clarification from 
several of the vendors to ensure an accurate comparison of unit items and hourly rates. 
At first glance, Turnkey appears to be the lowest bid.  Hiowever, we found the following to be true: 
 Turnkey under estimated the amount of work involved 
 Turnkey’s hourly rates are higher 
 Based on a time and material contract, their higher rates would use up the allocated amounts 
quicker than Fiber Link’s rates 
 Fiber Link’s lower hourly rates will allow the District to accomplish more and to mazimize the 
refunds available to the Ditrct under E-rate 
Based on our evaluation, we recommend the award of the Fiber Maintenance Contract Bid #9851 to 
Fiber Link, Inc.  We are also recommending the District accept Voluntary Alternate 1 and 2 as proposed
