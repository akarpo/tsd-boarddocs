A.   TSD Mission Moment— Smith Middle School “U-Knighted Hour” 
This past summer, a group of interested teachers from Smith Middle School 
met to develop a “homeroom” for all Smith students.  As a building, Smith 
recognized the need to develop a stronger bond between staff and students 
and students between grade levels.  A plan was made to implement a 7 -
period day once a month and have “U-Knighted” time during the 7 th hour.  
Students were arranged by grade level and then divided amongst 41 
teachers resulting in  approximately 17 students in each group.  The 
committee meets before each U -Knighted Hour and develops a lesson plan 
for the groups.  The focus has been on character deve lopment, diversity, 
motivation, community, goal setting, and FUN!!!   Th is program has been 
well-received by both students and staff alike.   
Recommended Action:  Invite Amy Buchanan, Assistant Principal, Smith 
Middle School, Counselor Jen Hartenburg, and social studies teacher Glen Rexer,  
up to share a bit more about this program.
