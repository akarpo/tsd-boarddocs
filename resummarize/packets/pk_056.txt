Re: Bid Award Recommendation for Voice, Video, Security and Data Network 
As part of the Technology Bond Project for 2013/14, the district has planned to replace major components of 
the Telephone, Network Security and Wired/Wireless Network.  Using the MHEC, Midwest Higher Education 
Consortium (MHEC) and Michigan Intercollegiate Telecommunications Consortium (MiCTA), both state 
approved bid tools, TSD evaluated two vendors, Extreme Networks and Cisco Systems (our current network 
provider).  After an in-depth analysis comparing features for each product, the bid from Hi-Tech, Inc. of Casco, 
Michigan, is being recommended for the following;   
1. The acquisition and installation of networking equipment from Extreme Networks for 
wired/wireless data systems; and 
2. The acquisition and installation of telecommunications equipment from Shoretel. 
The total recommended amount of the award to Hi-Tech, Inc. will be in the amount of $1,912,095.  Recent 
updates include a cost reduction of $304,248, which is $409,453 less than Cisco’s most recent updated bid as 
of 3/5/14.  Attached please find a copy of the bid summary with related pricing and also a copy of support 
materials from both Cisco and Extreme outlining features that each company believes differentiates them 
against the other. 
Both items use the approved state bid tools and the bond funds being allocated are within budget for the 
project.  Should the Board of Education choose to award the recommended bids, installation will be 
completed by June 31, 2014.  
The Extreme Networks bid presented a solid, reliable solution providing for great network access for any 
device connecting in our environment.  Some of the benefits of the solution are as follows: 
 The installation of an enterprise-grade, fully redundant network core allowing for faster network traffic 
management;  
 Full network management from a centralized management console; 
 The ability to manage network traffic at the access point preventing unnecessary traffic from reaching the core 
of the network;  
 Policy for optimized control of the users and network resources; 
 Wireless device data analytics allowing for better network control and management; 
 Network integration to our district content filter, iBoss; 
 Full district-wide wireless access point coverage;   
 Data Center Manager to help manage and support the district’s VMWare environment; 
 Integrated Digital Citizenship enforcement; 
 BYOD solutions with the ability to differentiate guest and non-district issued devices; and 
 All network related costs are covered for the next five years with a full warranty included.

Dear Mark,  
Thank you for considering Extreme Networks for the Troy Schools Network refresh. We are pleased to 
present the enclosed proposal to Troy Schools.  Extreme Networks delivers the most educationally 
focused networking solution in the industry.  Extreme Networks offers a complete wireless networking 
solution that will help augment and optimize the diverse services offered by Troy Schools all while 
reducing operational costs. 
Technology plays a vital role in enabling educators to provide their students with the skills they need in 
order to succeed in the global economy. Along with traditional school subjects, students must also 
become competent with the latest technology and be able to think critically and communicate 
effectively with a wide audience as they address new and increasingly difficult problems. In order to 
engage today’s students and reflect their techn ology-based lifestyles, the classroom needs to provide a 
media-rich, interactive environment that supports a variety of digital networking devices such as 
laptops, netbooks and smartphones. 
In particular, wired and wireless LANs are the critical “glue” bet ween the K-12 classroom and the vast 
array of network-based resources that educators utilize to enhance the classroom learning environment. 
With student safety as a top priority, the integrated wired/wireless network must enable access to 
leading education applications as well as research-oriented web sites while maintaining appropriate 
access and control over network services.  
The K-12 environment presents numerous challenges from a networking perspective, including:  
• A diverse set of educational web sites and education applications residing on both internal and 
external network locations, databases, and servers  
• A large and dynamic student population with increasing and diverse expectations for network 
and information access  
• A teacher and administrator population with varying skills that are expected to manage their 
own day to day technology-based needs  
As the number and type of connected devices, applications and technologies increase, so too does the 
complexity of the networking environment. In recognition of the need to manage this complexity 
effectively and also tune the K-12 network to education priorities, Extreme Networks provides 
capabilities to:  
• Prioritize access for school operations and multimedia classroom applications 
• Enforce authorized access criteria to protect student confidentiality and preserve data integrity  
• Offer wireless network access to administrative staff, teachers, and students

Today’s K-12 environment with diverse needs must not only be facilitated and secured by the network,

Facilitating Individual Education Plans 
The services offered to Troy Schools students, faculty and staff should not be a “one -size-fits-all” or even 
a “one -VLAN-fits-most” environment. The diverse and complex needs handled by your district  require a 
robust wireless system with a focus on delivering the best end user experience and be able to deliver 
individualized instruction to allow students to reach their full academic potential. 
Extreme Networks solutions allow an optional, “differentiated” access to resources. In a BYOD 
environment, all users need not be treated alike; a faculty member with a BYOD should be able to access 
different resources, and have different bandwidth, than a student. With our solution, users are based on 
their identity. Identity is based on WHO you are, WHERE you are, and WHAT DEVICE you have, and even 
on What time of day it may be. Since user profiles are accessed from Active Directory, Troy Schools does 
not have to reinvent the wheel and can empower services utilizing existing resources.  
The Extreme Networks solution provides complete visibility over the district’s network as well as its 
airspace. Utilizing tunable parameters relating to signal strength and location, a MyWi-enabled device 
operating on agency or affiliate properties can be automatically detected and identified as an 
unauthorized access point so it cannot be used, ensuring CIPA compliance even on non-district owned 
devices.

 MiCTA Overview: 
MiCTA represents thousands of governmental and charitable entities, higher 
education, K-12, healthcare, and libraries.  Nationally, MiCTA seeks to resolve common 
voice, video and data issues, provide helpful information, participate in legislative and 
regulatory telecommunications advocacy, and negotiate discounted purchase 
contracts for telecommunications and technology services for its members. For more 
information, please visit the MiCTA Web site, http://www.mictatech.org.
