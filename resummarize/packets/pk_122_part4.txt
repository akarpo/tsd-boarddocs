                                        2019-2020 
 
Administration has reviewed the enrollment for elementary, middle, and high 
school attendance areas.  Due to the elementary and middle school  buildings’ 
total student enrollment and physical space constraints, we recommend that 
Barnard, B emis, Schroeder and Boulan Park  Schools be closed to open 
enrollment.  Our goal  for Athens and Troy High  has been to maximize each 
building’s enrollment which has resulted in  both schools being closed to open 
enrollment for the past several years. Based on current enrollment projections 
for the 2019-2020 school year, it is recommended that Athens and Troy High be 
closed to open enrollment with the exception of not more than (25) twenty-five 
incoming ninth grade students who reside in the Troy High School attendance 
area but wish to attend Athens High School.  Applications for the (25) twenty-
five spots at Athens High School will be accepted during a two-week window at 
the beginning of March.

Board of Directors’ Elections—Candidate Information  
and Email Voting Procedures
This year, seven seats on the MASB Board of Directors are up for election. 
The MASB Board of Directors is comprised of 19 members—16 directors elected from the eight regions (two 
elected from each); and one director each from Groups V, VI and VII. Directors are elected by active member 
school boards and, each year, several seats are up for election. As approved by the 2018 Delegate Assembly, 
beginning in 2019 and in each subsequent year, the MASB President will nominate a member to serve as an at-
large director, subject to Board approval. Beginning in 2021, the Board of Directors will consist of 22 members, 
including three at-large directors. 
Board Limitations:
Article VIII, Section 6 of the MASB Bylaws permits only one director to be elected or appointed from any one 
local or intermediate school district board. 
According to MASB Bylaws Article VIII, Section 8, if any nomination for the Board of Directors is unopposed, 
the Board of Directors shall declare the unopposed candidate elected without conducting an election for the 
uncontested group or region.
This year, the following seats on the MASB Board are up for election (one seat each, three-year terms unless 
otherwise noted): Regions 3, 4, 6, (two-year term) 7 and 8, and Groups V and VI; if you’re unsure of your 
district’s region or group, please see the list on page 2.
• Region 4 will continue to be represented by Ruth Coppens, Saginaw ISD (unopposed, no ballots will    
be sent)
• Group VI will continue to be represented by Shawna Walker, Wayne-Westland Community Schools 
(unopposed, no ballots will be sent)
Voting in Regions 3, 6, 7 and 8 and Group V will take place via an online ballot. No local district/ISD funds 
can be used to campaign for anyone running for a seat. To help your board make an informed decision, the 
following pages contain background information and statements from each of the candidates.
Each district received ONE ballot, which was emailed to the superintendent secretary on record with MASB on 
Jan. 24, 2019. These ballots CANNOT be forwarded to anyone else. Once your board makes a decision on 
which candidate it wishes to vote for, please convey this information to your superintendent secretary.
Completed ballots must be submitted and confirmed by the superintendent secretary by 1 p.m. on Wednesday, 
March 6, 2019.
Electronic voting is a two-step process:
 Step 1 = Submit ballot.
 Step 2 = Confirm ballot has been submitted.
Unofficial election results will be available on the voicemail recording at 517.327.5915 as soon as possible, but 
generally after 2 p.m. on the deadline date. Official results will be approved at the March 22, 2019 Board of 
Directors’ meeting, which newly elected directors should plan to attend.
To summarize:
 • Ballot deadline is 1 p.m. on Wednesday, March 6, 2019.
 • No local district/ISD funds can be used to campaign for anyone running for a seat.
 • Have questions? Contact Cheryl Huffman at 517.327.5915 or chuffman@masb.org.
