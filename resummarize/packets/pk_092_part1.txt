schools, entered the National Merit Scholarship Program; however, only 16,000

passing a resolution to commend and congratulate our sixty-two National Merit 
Scholarship Semifinalists. This  is a remarkable number of semifinalists , and a 
tribute to the staff and parents of the Troy School District .  Copies of this 
resolution will be presented to the students at a reception on November 2nd.

B.   TSD Mission Moment — Morse Summer Nights 
Tonight we recognize the people who came together to put on a special community 
outreach program at Morse Elementary. Called “Morse Summer Nights,” this serie s of 
events brings together students, staff, parents and the community with the goal to 
reduce summer learning loss by providing free, regular access to great books for 
students in preschool through fifth grade.  
Morse Summer Nights also provides food to families who might not have the resources 
to feed their families without the school year breakfast and lunch programs. Finally, it 
keeps a sense of community engagement throughout the summer months. A large 
group of sponsors helps to make these events possible, but this evening, we honor the 
Morse staff, who spearheaded this effort and put countless hours into making Morse 
Summer Nights a huge success. 
At this time, I would like to invite Stephanie Zendler, principal at Morse Elementary, up 
to tell us a little more about the program.

C.  2016 Gregory Gwisdalla Special Education Administrator of the Year –  
   Kathy Jagels 
Tonight we recognize Kathy Jagels, Director of Special Education,  who has been 
named the 2016 Gregory Gwisdalla Special Education Administrator of the Year for 
Oakland County . This award is give n annually to by the Special Education 
Administrators of Oakland County (SEAOC) in honor of Greg  Gwisdalla, who once 
served as the Oakland Schools Director of Special Education. The recipient embodies 
the spirit of servant leadership that was the hallmark of Greg’s leadership.   
Mrs. Jagels was presented with the award at Oakland Schools on September 9th. Dr. 
Machesky, Board of Education Trustee Gary Hauff, members of the Superintendent’s 
Cabinet and the TSD Special Education team were on hand at the celebration, along 
with Mrs. Jagels’ family, to congratulate her. 
The Gwisdalla award is a very prestigious  recognition and the SEAOC team at 
Oakland Schools said Mrs. Jagels is extremely deserving of this  award because she 
embodies what servant leadership truly means. 
At this time, I would like to invite Kathy Jagels up to say a few words.
