The National School Lunch Program (NSLP) requires school food authorities (SFAs) participating in 
the National Lunch Program to ensure sufficient funds are provided to the nonprofit schoo l food 
service account for lunches served to students not certified eligible for free or reduced price meals.  In 
other words, the NSLP is ensuring that the free or reduced price meal reimbursements are not being 
used to subsidize the SFA’s paid lunches. 
 
The paid lunch equity tool provided by the United States Department of Agriculture Food and 
Nutrition Service was created to help SFAs calculate their paid lunch price increase requirement to 
meet the requirements in Section 205 of the Healthy, Hunger-Free Kids Act of 2010. 
 
The calculation uses the number of paid meals served in October of the previous fiscal year, 
multiplied by the price per meal to determine the weighted average price of paid meals served.  That 
calculation must meet or exceed the differ ence between the NSLP reimbursement price of free meals 
and the NSLP reimbursement price of paid meals.  If the weighted average price does not exceed the 
difference in the NSLP reimbursement prices, an adjustment of the SFA’s paid meals is required. 
 
The result of the calculations provided by the tool requires Troy School District to increase the paid 
lunch prices to comply with Section 205 of the Healthy, Hunger -Free Kids Act of 2010.  The 
calculation yields a  weighted average price of $2.55  while the wei ghted average requirement for 
2015-16 is $2.70.  In order to meet the $2.70  weighted average requirement for the 2015-2016 fiscal 
year, it is recommended that a $0.1 5 cent increase per paid lunch is established. The follow ing paid 
lunch prices reflecting a  $0.15 cent increase from the previous year are recommended for 2015 -16 
and yield a weighted average price of $2.70.

  Revise Troy Board of Education Meeting Schedule for the 
remainder of the 2015 Calendar Year 
   
  In order to most efficiently conduct business related to bond-related needs, 
it has been suggested that dates for workshops and regular Board 
meetings be interchanged so that items discussed during a workshop can 
be presented to public at the next consecutive meeting.  The District is 
recommending that workshops be conducted on the first Tuesday of each 
month followed by regular Board meeting s on the third Tuesday of the 
month.  Attached is a copy of the proposed changes to the 2015 Board 
Meeting Schedule beginning in September 2015.
