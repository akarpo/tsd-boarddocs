consideration.

Moran be accepted and a contract be awarded to  have them  provide annual 
professional audit services to  the District for the next five years .  We found their  
presentation and proposal to be complete, firm and audit team experienced , audit 
approach aligned with our expectations, and a superior customer service model that 
meets our needs .  Plante Moran will perform an annual independent audit of our 
financial statements while providing a fresh perspective, collaborative partnership,  
and highly competent audit team.

savings of $250 from our cost of $57,750 for the June 30, 2019 annual audit.

Subject: Paging Upgrade RFP #9899 Recommendation Letter 
Beth, 
On January 27, 2020, Troy School District issued a Request for Proposal (RFP) soliciting bids for district-wide paging 
upgrades.  The RFP was posted to the District’s website.  Convergent also spoke with several vendors regarding the 
solicitation and pointed them to the website to download the RFP and supporting documentation.  On February 
18, 2020, sealed bids were accepted and opened by the District. Two proposals were received.  The initial results 
were tabulated as follows: 
Contractor Base Bid 
The DataCom Group $778,105.00 
Moss $1,114,132.00 
All proposals were evaluated for compliance with the RFP requirements, pricing, technical solution and acceptance 
of terms and conditions of the RFP. Both were compliant.   
A technical evaluation of the system was conducted with the manufacturer’s fie ld engineer and it was found the 
proposed system meets all RFP requirements. 
On February 25th the District IT staff and Convergent Technology Partners conducted a interview with the low 
bidder, The DataCom Group, where we discussed scope of work, TSD expectations and scheduling. All questions 
and topics discussed were answered satisfactory. The DataCom Group has previously worked for TSD installing 
paging systems in the ECC and IAE and are familiar with the system and the district IT staff.  
Based on these factors, we recommend the award of the Paging Upgrade (RFP #9899) project to the low qualified 
bidder, The DataCom Group, in the amount of $778,105.00, with an additional ~10% contingency of $77,810.00 
for a total of $855,915.00.  Please contact me with any questions at 810-223-0531.
