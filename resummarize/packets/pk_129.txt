A pilot one to one program was conducted during the first semester of the 2018 -19 school 
year with 10 th and 11th grade students and 12 teachers at Athens and Troy High Schools.  
Informational surveys were conducted to gather input from stakeholders including pilot 
teachers, non-pilot teachers, students, and parents.  Student work was reviewed for evidence 
supporting the blue pillar district goals.  All collected data was reviewed and numerous 
discussions were held to determine the best course of action.   
It is recommended that a one to one iPad program be phased in with 9th grade students and 
9th grade teachers at the start of the 2019-20 school year.  
Phasing in the 1:1 digital learning environment starting with 9th graders addresses the 
following issues: 
 Allows time to evaluate a fully implemented 1:1 environment for the full school year.   
 Allows for focused professional learning with a smaller group of teachers.   
 Allows students to continue learning with an iPad from 8th grade – with the addition 
of a keyboard. 
Recommended Implementation Plan: 
2019-20:  iPads provided for all 9th graders   
Spring 2020:  Program evaluation  
• If positive, the district will continue to phase in iPads with each incoming 9th grade class 
• If negative, the iPads purchased will be used to refresh 3rd grade iPads the following year 
as planned 
The purchase from Apple Computers with pricing from the REMC bid for 1050 iPads with

special pricing.  After June 21, 2019, the cost for 1050 devices will increase to $412,650.

Rationale 
“Leveraging digital is not about devices but about learning that can be amplified, accelerated, and facilitated by 
interaction with the digital world.”  (Fullan & Quinn) 
“Effective use of digital facilitates deep learning partnerships with students, families, community members, and experts 
regardless of geographical location and supports student capacity to take control of their own learning both within and 
outside the classroom walls.”  (Fullan, Quinn, & McEachen) 
The Troy School District has an established 1:1 iPad program for students in grades 3 – 8.  A 1:1 digital learning 
environment in the high school would allow students to continue deepening their learning by leveraging digital 
resources in a meaningful, equitable way.  We are excited to consider a 1:1 digital learning environment that: 
• allows students to deepen learning, create knowledge, and apply digital in innovative ways, 
• enables students and teachers to connect and collaborate locally and globally, 
• enables timely and accurate feedback for learning,  
• and lightens the load of backpacks since most of our textbooks also provide students with electronic access to 
content.   
It is essential to keep these ideals in mind as we make decisions about moving to a 1:1 environment, which device to 
provide, and what kind of teacher professional learning is needed. 
Bond money has been set aside to support this initiative. 
Process 
The process used to explore a 1:1 high school digital learning environment is outlined in Table 1:  Process for Moving 
Toward a HS 1:1 Digital Learning Environment (page 2).  All high school teachers were invited to participate.   
Results 
The results from different activities are summarized in Table 2:  Results (pages 3-5).   
Recommendation 
The Teaching and Learning Cabinet reviewed the results outlined in Table 2.  While many stakeholders are split between 
providing an iPad or a laptop for each high school student, the teachers who piloted the devices in the classroom report 
that iPads offer more opportunities to amplify deep learning in the classroom (see Table 3:  iPad Apps Used in High 
School Classes, pages 6-8).  As this reason is aligned with our Core Value of Deepening Learning, the Teaching and 
Learning Cabinet recommends that iPads are provided for all high school students.  In 2019-20, iPads will be supplied to 
all 9th graders.  If an evaluation of the program in the spring of 2020 is positive, we will then continue to phase-in iPads 
at the high school with each new 9th grade class.  The cost for the 2019-20 school year will be approximately $350,000 
($350 for iPad/case x 1000 9th grade students). 
Implementation Plan 
iPads will be purchased during the summer of 2019.  Professional learning for the 2019-20 school year will be focused on 
supporting 9th grade teachers.  This professional learning will be planned with the teachers who piloted the devices, the

• Allows time to evaluate a fully implemented 1:1 environment for the full school year.  If we find that this is not

• Allows for focused professional learning with a smaller group of teachers.

• Most parents have not already purchased a device for incoming 9th graders.
