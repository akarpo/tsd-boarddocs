2.   RECOGNITION  
 
A.   TSD Mission Moment—Smith Connectors 
 
 
The Smith Connectors is a mentoring program that consists of 8 th grade students who 
serve as leaders at Smith Middle School as well as in the community. The Smith 
Connectors are held to a high expectation of leadership, respect, and responsibi lity.  
Their actions and choices both in and out of school  are expected to uphold the 
standards of Smith.  The Connectors are involved in many leadership activities 
throughout the school year such as assisting at Adventure Island, helping 6 th graders 
adjust to middle school, teambuilding and anti-bullying activities.  They do a wonderful 
job of helping to create a positive and safe culture at Smith.  These 8 th grade students 
are role models for the other students at Smith.  The staff at Smith is so proud of each 
one of the Connectors. We would like to invite advisors Jennifer Hartenburg and Ray 
Torres along with the 45 Smith students who serve as Connectors up to receive their 
certificates.

B.   National Blue Ribbon Award 
 
We are pleased to recognize Wattles Elementary Sch ool and Bemis Elementary 
School tonight for their designation as National Blue Ribbon Schools . This award 
honors them as one of the very best schools in the Unites States.  
 
Secretary Arne Duncan recognized Wattles and Bemis in a ceremony in Washington, D. C. 
last month. Just 337 schools from around the country were chosen for this honor this year. 
This award affirms the hard work of students, staff, parents, and t he Bemis and Wattles 
communities.  As Secretary Duncan stated, “National Blue Ribbon Schools are models of 
consistent excellence and a resource for other schools and districts.  We celebrate them for 
their tireless effort and boundless creativity in reaching and teaching every student.”  
 
Congratulations to both schools for this newest (and impressive) designation!

TSD Mission Moment – 
Leonard Elementary School 
“Peer to Peer Mentoring” 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
NUMATS Award – Tracey 
Kinney 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Last year, Lisa Carruthers, teacher at Leonard 
Elementary School, worked tirelessly to implement the 
“Peer to Peer Mediation” program into her third grade 
classroom.  Peer to Peer Mediation is an evidence-
based practice that positively impacts students with 
Autism Spectrum Disorders as well as other students 
with disabilities. In essence, the Peer to Peer program 
develops peer relationships.  Students learn about 
autism and how it affects other students in their 
classroom.  Mrs. Carruthers attended seminars during 
the summer months and met with other teachers that 
had implemented the program.  Mrs. Carruthers is 
currently busy sharing her knowledge with other 
teachers at Leonard and around the Troy School 
District. Principal, Jerry Cottone, introduced Mrs. 
Carruthers and her student then shared a few 
comments the student and his parents said about Mrs. 
Carruthers. The Board congratulated Mrs. Carruthers 
and presented her with a certificate of recognition.
