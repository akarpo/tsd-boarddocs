stakeholders responding positively to the quality of their learning environment and  
ensuring School District resources are directed to the maintenance of high quality

warehouse services of the School District’s facilities through contracting for the 
School District’s custodial, grounds and warehouse operations with D.M. Burr.

the RFP.

(a) negotiate and finalize the terms and conditions of the contract

District’s legal counsel.

We issued Request for Proposals (RFP) #9881 for Custodial, Grounds & Warehouse Services on March 
21, 2019.  Proposals were due April 4, 2019 in which we received five (5) competitive proposals.  Rob 
Carson, Larry Franckowiak, and I reviewed and analyzed the submitted proposals.  We selected ABM, 
D.M. Burr, and GDI to interview.   
On April 18, 2019, a committee conducted interviews of the three selected companies.  The committee 
consisted of Nicole Wilson, Bob Dowd, Joe Duda, Sarah Glasser, Larry Franckowiak, Rob Carson, Jim 
Crowley, and me.  The companies were each provided approximately 1-hour interview in which they 
presented their proposals and engaged in a Q&A session with the committee. Each committee member 
brought a unique perspective and provided valuable input to assist in making a recommendation.   
Although the committee was not a decision-making body, a general level of discomfort was conveyed 
with the proposal and presentation submitted by GDI.  The committee did not feel GDI demonstrated 
the relevant K-12 experience needed to service our District; therefore, eliminated them from further  
consideration.  The committee felt that both ABM and D.M. Burr were qualified to service our District.  
We discussed the cost associated with both proposals and each member experience with transitioning 
service providers.  It was recommended that Rob and I further review the costs of each proposal and 
explore the possibility of negotiating a successor agreement with DM Burr.   
In further reviewing the cost associated with ABM and DM Burr proposals, we asked both companies 
to provide us the number of assumed overtime hours in their proposal to adequately cover all events 
requested by the District and a new proposed number if we were to bill overtime as a direct pass 
through.  DM Burr responded they had assumed 3,000 hours of billable overtime and reduced their 
proposed price by $124,000 if we were to bill as a direct pass through .  ABM responded they did not 
include any billable overtime hours; therefore, their price was firm .  Rob Carson and I believe it is in 
our best interest to have the successful contractor begin to bill overtime hours as a direct pass through 
as opposed to including it in the contract price.   Additionally, our RFP asked for each company to cost 
out their proposal at a $12, $13, and $14 beginning wage for custodians.  It is important to note that 
each of the companies stated that the current labor market in Troy would require a $13 minimum wage 
to recruit and retain custodians.
