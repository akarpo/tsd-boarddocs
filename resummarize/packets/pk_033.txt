As the technology department was determining the best solution to recommend for 
replacement of teacher workstations and secondary labs, the added benefits of a 
virtualized desktop and the ongoing costs were important to consider. Some features 
that are gained when implementing a cloud based virtual desktop are: 
 Remote access to same desktop from any location and device (iPad, laptop, 
computer, Smartphone) on the network or through an external portal. 
 Ease of software deployment and compatibility 
 Flexible allocation of system technical specification as end user demand change. 
 Reduce electrical costs through lower power consumption on Zero Client. 
 Support for the End device becomes a swap out of the device keeping the 
technical support at a lower level and the high level trouble shooting more 
centrally supported increasing service level. 
On-going Costs for a typical desktop after the initial Useful Life include monies to extend 
the warranty or purchase parts as they fail. This can become costly as the age of the 
device increases and parts are unavailable. The on-going cost of a virtualized 
environment include the maintenance cost for the VMware  licenses which is $22 per 
client per year and the cost to maintain the Storage and Server infrastructure. According 
to ROI studies of virtual desktop implementations, districts have elongated the life of 
existing hardware by two to three years and have experienced a 40-50% decrease in 
on-going maintenance cost. Other districts currently deploying virtualized desktops 
include: Chippewa Valley, Clawson, Rockford, and Central Michigan University.

  Complete cost analysis of current data processing

The Data Processing department supports a number of different elements to the daily 
operations of our school district. The major categories of which they provide support to 
our staff, students, parents and state/federal reporting are comprised of the following: 
• Student Information System – eSchoolPlus 
• Financial/Human Resources Information System – FinancePlus 
• 3rd Party Applications 
• Databases 
• Operating Systems 
• Data Requests 
• State and Federal Reporting 
• Internally Developed Web Applications 
• General District Support 
The department is comprised of three (3) staff members. Two (2) are technical 
programmers, with one being the department manager. There is also one (1) support staff 
person. The attached chart (Attachment A) lists the three staff members’ breakdown of 
approximate annual time devoted to each product/element area.  
This detailed work level was an essential first step in developing a cost analysis of the 
department’s operations so we could have comparable data when seeking other solutions 
to provide the same or similar level of support along each of the product line areas. 
The need to continuously evaluate our operations has been pushed not only by legislative 
consolidation efforts in recent years, but also possible funding incentives for collaboration 
amongst districts. We have had other districts indicate a willingness to collaborate with 
the use of our software, but to date, no collaboration has materialized.  
We have approached our cost analysis from three main prongs. One, current 
configuration of self-operation using the same software and looking at whether we could 
reduce costs in either product alignment (migrate to web version) or staffing reductions. 
Secondly, we are looking at changing from self-operation to one of seeking our same
