F. CONSIDERATION:  DISTRICT-OWNED PROPERTY 
 The District has been conducting pre -sale investigations of district -owned 
vacant property and offers this resolution for Board of Education 
consideration. 
 RECOMMENDED ACTION:  Approve the resolution below. 
SUGGESTED RESOLUTION 
Moved By: ______________________________ 
Supported By: ______________________________ 
Whereas, the Troy School District Board of Education’s mission is to ensure learning for 
all members of the school community; and 
Whereas, the Troy School District Board of Education appreciates and values the real 
property owned by the District; and  
Whereas, the District desires to develop an Early Childhood Center to service our 
preschool programs; and  
Whereas, the Troy School District Board of Education desires to explore and consider 
the sale of certain surplus, vacant real property in order to offset long term debt for the 
development of an Early Childhood Center, and 
Whereas, the Troy School District Board of Education desires to authorize the School 
District’s Administration, its legal counsel, Clark Hill PLC, and its real estate consultant, 
Great Northern Consulting Group, to explore/market/analyze the sale of certain surplus, 
vacant real property , as outlined below , with the understanding that any 
Offer/Agreements etc. will be brought before the Board for approval,

Now, Therefore, be it Resolved That:  
1. The School District  authorizes administration to further investigate the 
preservation of Section 1 property , with the exception of  the frontage on 
Square Lake which will be split into its own parcel and marketed for sale.  
The remainder of the land may be preserved in perpetuity  either by the School 
District or through a conservation easement or other similar form of agreement. 
2. The School District authorizes the marketing of Section 11 property for purposes 
of sale. 
3. The School District desires to develop an Early Childhood Center on approximately 
8 acres of Section 16 and preserve the balance of the subject property. 
4. The School District appoints TMP Architecture as the Architect of record and 
authorizes TMP Architecture to commence the design process  for an Early 
Childhood Center in accordance with their submitted  proposal for services dated 
March 10, 2017 .  The cost of services is  not to exceed $933,716  to complete 
phases 1 through 4 of the proposal  and is approved as an expenditure of the 
Capital Maintenance Fund.     
5. The cost associated with the selling of property is an approved expenditure of the 
Capital Maintenance Fund Professional Services Budget. 
6. Revenue generated from these property sales shall be restricted in the Capital 
Maintenance Fund for the purposes of using toward the development of the Early 
Childhood Center.
