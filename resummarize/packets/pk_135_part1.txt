  RE: Policy Update Considerations for Policy Committee  
   DATE: Thursday, November 7, 2019 
On Wednesday, October 23, 2019, the Policy Committee of the Board of Education reviewed the 
policy updates from Lusk and Albertson and recommend the following policy additions/changes be 
adopted by the Board of Education: 
1. To clear any confusion regarding which Board members are subject to the “Conflict 
of Interest” policy, typically found under Section 1001/1000.01, we recommend 
the following underlined change: 
Conflict of Interest. If a member of the immediate family (father, mother, 
husband, wife, son, daughter, sister, brother, father-in-law, mother-in-law, son-
in-law, daughter-in-law, sister-in-law, brother-in-law, or step-family members) 
of a newly elected Board member is  already an employee of the School 
District, such Board member shall abstain from voting on any matter affecting 
the employment status of the employee. 
2. We recommend the following underlined change to the first and last sentences 
of “Curriculum Development,” typically found under Section 3001/3000.01, to 
comply with MCL 380.1282(2): 
Board directs the Superintendent is responsible for the to development, 
implementation, and provide ongoing evaluation of the School District’s 
curriculum. The curriculum will: 
 Be consistent with the Board’s policy on Student Learning and 
Achievement; 
 Meet or exceed all requirements of the State of Michigan for 
instructional programs; and, 
 Be standards- based and founded upon legally compliant, research- 
based grade level learning and achievement standards that lead to 
the awarding of a School District diploma.

6. Governor Whitmer signed into law House Bill 4242 which amends certain sections  
of the State School Aid Act. One of those sections is MCL 388.1766, which, 
generally, prohibits school districts from referring students for abortions or assisting 
students in obtaining abortions. The statute also: required school district personnel 
to provide sex education in compliance with the Revised School Code and assessed 
a financial penalty against any school district official, employee, or Board member 
who referred or assisted a student in obtaining an abortion or violated MCL 380.1507. 
The recent amendments remove references to the Revised School Code and to 
any financial penalties assessed against school district personnel. In addition, school 
districts will no  longer forfeit $100,000 in state aid funding for failure to adopt a 
policy consistent with MCL  388.1766; instead, school districts may be subject to a 
forfeiture of 1% of state aid funding if corrective action is not taken under MCL 
3800.1766a. The amendments also added an exception to the general prohibition 
of referral or assistance language, by excluding employees, Board members, and 
officials who are parents of students. 
20
In light of these amendments, we recommend the following, underlined changes to the 
“Prohibition of Referral or Assistance” policy, typically found under Section 
4005/4000.05: 
Prohibition of Referral or Assistance. All District employees, Board members, 
and school officials must provide instruction in sex education in strict compliance 
with MCL 380.1507 of the Revised School Code. In addition, All District 
employees, Board members, and school officials are prohibited from referring a 
student for an abortion or assisting a student in obtaining an abortion. This policy 
does not apply to employees, Board members,  and school officials who are

Pursuant to state law, District personnel found to be in violation of this policy

policy.
