A.  TSD Mission Moment - Facilities Study Team 
This year, the Troy School District formed a Facilities Study Team to 
conduct a comprehensive review of all buildings in the District. This team 
was made up entirely of members of the community, who volunteered 
and gave countless hours of their time and expertise  to this important 
project. Tonight, we are pleased to recognize the members of the 
Facilities Study Team for their exemplary service . We thank them for 
their thoughtful and skilled analysis and the recommendations they gave 
that will help guide us as the District moves forward.   
We would also like to recognize and thank Gary Wood who was the 
Community Facilitator for the Facilities Study Team.  The District could 
not have undertaken a comprehensive study like this without his 
leadership and direction.    
When I read your name, please come forward and receive a small plaque 
commemorating your service to the Facilities Study Team.

B. Michigan Reading Educator Award – Natalie Haezebrouck  
Taking the step from a classroom to a reading specialist role takes a master 
teacher and one who has the respect of the administration and staff. This year’s 
co-recipient of the Teacher K -6 Educator Award for the Michigan Reading 
Association, Natalie Haezebrouck, from Leonard Elementary School, took on this 
role with high expectations and vast knowledge. One parent shared that she 
could w atch her child’s confidence and enthusiasm build after developing 
strategies with Ms. Haezebrouck over the course of the year. Natalie developed 
a Guided Reading Academy in her school. This academy brought teachers 
together to explore strategies that would be used in their classroom. Teachers 
viewed videos , and through coaching and shared experiences, helped the 
students at Leonard reach new heights. Her unique strategy was adopted by 
the whole district. Natalie’s actions are a true reflection of the core v alues in 
OneTroy. Congratulations to Natalie Haezebrouck. 
RECOMMENDED ACTION:   Invite Natalie up to receive her certificate.

Mission Moment 
Boulan Park Middle School 
group of students and teachers who worked to enhance 
their leadership skills while volunteering their time to help 
younger students find success. The Boulan Park Middle 
students who practice the 7 Habits of Highly Effective Teens.  
They participate in service learning experiences and create 
student-driven Genius Hour projects.   Students volunteer 
weekly at Bemis Elementary and serve as mentors to 
elementary students with iPad activities, math, art, writing, 
science, and reading. Effective Teens volunteers learn how 
to better navigate academic situations and how to plan and 
modify lessons for the youngsters they have in their care. 
They also practice flexibility and differentiation skills. They 
analyze and reflect upon their experience in a student-led 
problem-solving debriefing session afterward.   Genius Hour 
is a student-driven, project-based learning opportunity 
where students spend one to two class periods each week 
researching and working on a project that interests them. 
Genius Hour culminates in student presentations to the 
other Effective Teens classes in the Troy School District at 
the Genius Hour Sharing Event field trip in May.  Teachers 
Mary Typinski and Michelle Slaviero and their students were

Sodexo Healthy High School 
Sodexo, to come up and recognize two of our high schools 
for their success with the Healthy High School Challenge.  
The Healthy High School Challenge is a national event aimed 
at promoting healthy lunch and snack options in the 
cafeteria.  High school nutrition programs managed by 
Sodexo around the county competed in this challenge, 
earning points for meals and healthy a-la-carte snacks and 
beverages purchased by students.  The schools who sold the 
healthiest meals, healthy snacks, and beverages during the 
challenge earned points.  The points earned were put 
towards grant money as the prize.   Both Athens and IA-East 
won the competition, earning over 100 points each.  Ms. 
Moran presented the building principals, Dr. Dixon and Mr. 
Brinks, a check for $1,000 to be split between the two 
schools.   Dr. Philippart thanked Sodexo for this award and 
for being partners with the Troy School District.
