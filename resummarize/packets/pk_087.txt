A.  Oakland County School Business Official of the Year – Rick West 
 
Tonight we recognize Assistant Superintendent Rick West, who has been named the 
2015-16 Oakland County School Business Administrator of the Year. This award is 
given annually to a school business official  to recognize outstanding 
accomplishments and contributions to their profession. Rick West was nominated by 
Superintendent Dr. Rich Machesky, with support from the district’s Board of  
Education and the entire central office staff. Trustee Gary Hauff was on hand at the 
ceremony to speak on behalf of the Board. 
 
The judges for the award praised Mr. West’s financial acumen, his leadership and 
his efforts to build a culture of trust and collaboration throughout the district. Mr. 
West is a visible and responsive member of the oneTROY community and has done 
an excellent job of steering the business office through challenging times. He is also 
an active member of the Michigan School Business Officials (MSBO), a MSBO 
Leadership Institute participant, and past president of OCSBO. 
 
At this time, we would like to invite OCSBO President Tina Kostiuk to the podium to 
speak and present their award. 
 
             
 
After Tina presents the OCSBO award, ask Rick West to come forward to 
receive his award from the Troy Board of Education and invite him to say 
a few words.

Over the last  couple of months, I have engaged in an evaluation of our property, casualty and auto 
insurance coverage.  I reviewed our current coverage and process through the Metropolitan Association 
for Improved School Legislation (MAISL)  Pool and solicited a quote from SET SEG ’s MASB-SEG 
Property Casualty Pool.  Both pool’s provide coverage for property, liability, and fleet insurance to 
adequately cover the district’s risk exposure.  Below are the quoted prices from each pool: 
 
Agent Company SET SEG MAISL
Total Premium 276,715.00$  309,557.00$   
 
Given the quotes and programs of each, it is my recommendation to switch from MAISL to SET SEG.   
SET SEG’s program offers us adequate property and casualty insurance coverage at a lower price.  
Additionally, the program includes at no additional cost,  access to  SafeSchools online training, 
premises medical payments for students, and a property appraisal service which combined is valued at 
$45,000.  Changing insurance providers will save Troy School District $32,842.  
 
Conversely, the MAISL Pool continues to decrease in size.  The pool currently consists of 33 members 
in which four (including us) of those members have opted to  solicit bids this year.  Of the other three 
districts soliciting bids, I have confirmed with two of them that they will be recommending leaving the 
pool to go to SET SEG’s MASB-SEG Property Casualty Pool.  As the MAISL Pool continues to shrink, 
it will become more difficult to receive the level of coverag e and service necessary to appropriately 
service our district.  
 
SET SEG currently provides insurance coverage to over 500 public schools in the state.  I am confident 
in their ability to provide us adequate coverage and exceptional service.  I have attached the premium, 
coverage limits and deductible comparison summary for your review.
