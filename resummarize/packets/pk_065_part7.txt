ANNUAL SUMMER TAX RESOLUTION 
 WHEREAS, this Board of Ed ucation by resolution of November 11, 2014, 
determined to impose a summer property tax levy to collect one -half of school property 
taxes, including debt services, upon property located within the Troy School District, 
beginning with 20 15 and continuing from year to year until specifically revoked by this 
Board of Education. 
NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT: 
 1.  This Board of Education, pursuant to 1976 P.A. 451, as amended, hereby 
invokes a summer tax levy of one -half of school property taxes, including d ebt service, 
continuing with 2015, and continuing from year to year until specifically revoked by this 
Board of Education, and requests each city in which this district is located and in which a 
local school district or city is concurrently imposing a summer tax levy to collect those 
summer taxes. 
 2.  The Superintendent or his/her designee is authorized and directed to 
forward to the governing body of each city in which this district is located and in which a 
local school district or city is concurrently imposing a summer tax levy, a copy of this 
Board’s resolution requesting that each such city agree to coll ect the summer tax levy 
for 2015 in the amount spe cified in this resolution.  Such forwarding of the resolution 
and the request to collect the summer tax levy shall be sent so that they are received by 
the appropriate governing bodies before January 1, 2015. 
 3.  The Superintendent or his/her designee is  authorized and directed to 
negotiate on behalf of this District with the governing body of each city in which the 
District is located for the reasonable expenses for collection of the District’s summer tax 
levy that the city may bill under MCLA 380.1612.  Any such proposed agreement shall be 
brought before this Board for its approval or disapproval. 
 4.  All resolutions and parts of resolutions insofar as they conflict with the 
provisions of this resolution be and the same are hereby rescinded.
