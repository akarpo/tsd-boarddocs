A. TSD Mission Moment—oneTROY Autism Programming 
Tonight, as our Mission Moment, we would like to highlight some of the 
wonderful programs in our district that focus on autism – especially since  April 
is designated as Autism Awareness Month.  
Autism is a condition that is characterized by difficulty in communicating and 
forming relationships. Students with autism are challenged in their ability to 
recognize and understand thoughts, beliefs, desires and intentions of other 
people in order to make sense of their behavior and predict what they are going 
to do.  
To celebrate the work that we are doing in Troy Schools, specifically around 
autism, we wanted to give you the opportunity to take a peek inside our 
programs.  At this time, I would like to welcome principal of Barnard Elementary 
School Mrs. Melanie Morey to share.
