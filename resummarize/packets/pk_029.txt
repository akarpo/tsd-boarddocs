B. CONSIDERATION:   Schools Cl osed to Open Enrollment for  
       2012/2013 
Administration has reviewed the enro llment for elementary, middle school, 
and high school attendance areas.  Our goal has been to maximize the 
enrollments at both Athens and Troy Hi gh School resulting in both schools 
being closed to open enrollment for th e past three years. For the 2012/2013 
school year it was recommended that  30 spots be available for students 
scheduled to go to Troy High School but wishing to attend Athens High 
School.  It is projected Troy High Scho ol will continue to maintain optimal 
enrollment and, due to some continued demographic shifts, it is appropriate 
to consider a limited numbe r of open enrollments be made available for the 
2012/2013 school year at Athens High School.  It is therefore recommended 
that Athens High School and Troy High School be closed to open enrollment, 
with the exception that 30 openings be  made available for incoming ninth 
graders who live in the Troy High School  attendance area but wish to attend 
Athens High School for the 2012/2013 school year.  Due to limited space, the 
district will be accepting applications from February 8 – 24, 2012. 
RECOMMENDED ACTION: Adopt the following resolution.

 Moved by: __________________________

close Athens High School and Troy High Sc hool to open enrollment for the 2012/2013 
school year, with the exception of not more  than 30 ninth grade students who live in 
the Troy High School attendance area but wish to attend Athens High School.  Students 
with siblings attending Athens High School and currently enrolled in  grades 9 – 11 will 
be allowed to open enroll to Athens for the 2012/2013 school year.

B‐1401  MINUTES 
The minutes of Board meetings, except closed sessions, are 
recorded and printed for the secretary by the administrative staff 
and forwarded to members with the agenda for the next meeting 
for approval.  Corrected minutes shall show both the original entry 
and the correction. 
  a.  The time, date, and place of the meeting. 
  b.  The members present and members absent. 
  c.  A statement of the topics discussed and any decisions 
made at the meeting. 
  d.  The purpose or purposes for which a closed session is held. 
  e.  All roll call votes taken at the meeting. All resolutions,  
names of those who propose and second all resolutions, 
results of all votes taken including the number of those 
voting yes and no.  In the event of either a required roll call 
vote or a non‐unanimous vote, a roll call vote will be taken 
and the names of those voting yes and no will be recorded.  
The disposition of the vote will be noted.   
2.  Proposed minutes must be available for public inspection at 
the address stated on the posted notice of the meeting within 
eight business days after a meeting.  Approved minutes must 
be available at the posted address within five business days 
after the meeting at which they are approved. 
3.  All motions are recorded.  The names of those who make 
motions, those who second motions, and those who vote ayes 
and nays are recorded. 
43.  All records of the Board are made available to the public in 
compliance with the Michigan Freedom of Information Act, 
Act No. 442, PA 1976.
