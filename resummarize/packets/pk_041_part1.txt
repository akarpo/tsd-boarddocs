1. ELECTION OF ACTING CHAIRPERSON 
The first order of business is to ele ct an acting chairperson who will 
conduct the meeting until such time as a president has been elected.  Past 
practice has been that the previous year’s president conducts this part 
of the meeting. 
2. APPOINTMENT OF TEMPORARY SECRETARY 
 The second order of  business is for the acting chairperson  to 
 appoint Judy Holmes to act as temporary secretary until such time as the 
 secretary to the Board has been elected. 
3. ELECTION OF PRESIDENT 
The acting chairperson declares nominations in order for the office of 
president of the board of education (please note, nominations need not be 
seconded; and a motion and support are made to close the nomination.)  
Please understand that four votes are required for election. 
4. ELECTION OF VICE PRESIDENT 
The newly elected p resident declares nominations in order for the 
office of vice president, following the same procedures as outlined for the 
election of president. 
5. ELECTION OF A SECRETARY 
The president declares nominations in order for the office of secretary, 
following the same procedures as outlined for the election of president. 
6. APPOINTMENT OF TREASURER 
According to Board Policy, the treasurer is appointed annually by the 
Board as a nonvoting officer.  The assistant superintendent of business 
services has served a s treasurer of the Board.  It is my recommendation 
that this procedure continue.

2.  RECOGNITION       
C.    Board of Education Recognition 
Each January the Troy School District recognizes and celebrates the 
work of a very important group of individuals: The Board of 
Education.  
Troy is truly fortunate to be governed by Board members who are 
tireless, professional, and passionate about improving the  
opportunities of our students. Tonight, we would like to recognize 
our Board of Education  members with a gift  that illustrates the 
impact our trustees have on the lives of students. We are pleased to 
present you with a work of art done by Madel eine Chong, a student 
at International Academy -East. Madeleine’s ink  drawing, entitled 
“Transformation” depicts seaweed morphing into a beautiful fish. We 
chose this piece because it symbolize s the way this Board works to 
transform students’ lives with each and every decision made. 
Madeleine’s art teacher, Sandy LewAllen, helped to matte and frame 
this beautiful work for each of you, as a token of our appreciation for 
the hard work and commitment you show to the Troy School District. 
We als o have created a laminated year -long calendar, with photos 
showing Board members  with the students you so graciously serve . 
As you look at  this throughout the year, we hope that it provides  a 
reminder that our students and staff are grateful every day to have 
such a wonderful Board behind us.  
Thank you for all you do. 
Present artwork and calendar.
