Award Recipients 
 
 
This evening, we are please d to acknowledge Mark Martin , mathematics 
teacher at Boulan Park Middle School; Melissa Rehbine, mathematics teacher at 
Smith Middle School; and Jennifer Slade , mathematics teacher at Larson Middle 
School, who have all been selected winners of the prestigious 2010  Edyth May 
Sliffe Award for Distinguished Middle School Mathematics Teaching.  This  
award, sponsored by the Mathematical Association of America, recognizes the 
excellence of those teachers who have contributed substantially to the success 
of the highest scoring teams on the American Junior High School Mathematics 
Exam.  It is an honor to  recognize outstanding teachers who are dedicated to 
developing their students’ mathematical talent.

MEMORANDUM 
 
To: Frank Lams, Purchasing Supervisor  
From: Scott Bryan, Director of Technology  
Date: August 27, 2010 
Re: iPad purchase 
 
Pursuant to my discussions with Cabinet regarding a pilot project to evaluate the viability of Apple iPads as 
part of next year’s laptop replacement project, I have received  a quote from Apple from the REMC Contract 
for 44 of these devices along with miscellaneous accessories to support the project.  
 
I am proposing to purchase enough devices to permit teachers to evaluate their use directly for a month or 
two, then will solicit proposals on how iPads would be used in a classroom in place of laptops for this school 
year.  Proposals will be evaluated by a selection committee and awarded to various buildings on a rotating 
basis, and will include a presentation plan for teachers a nd students to report back their experiences.  This 
will allow our instructional experts to evaluate this new technology directly and to recommend if and how 
iPads should be considered as a component of next year’s equipment refresh.  This will be funded a s an 
advance purchase from the bond allocation for laptop replacement, per my discussions with Mr. Rajter and 
Dr. Fowler. 
 
44 total devices will be purchased:  30 for the rotating classroom set, one each for the Directors of 
Technology and Instruction, one for technical evaluation, two for administrator evaluation purposes and 9 
floating checkout devices.  Apple offers a $20 per unit discount when purchasing ten -packs at one time from 
the REMC Contract – REMC Contract number 155575.  Per the attached quote,  the total price for 44 iPads, 
extended warranty on four of the devices (more expensive model with additional functionality), 44 cases and 
miscellaneous adapters is $24,158.47.   
 
This item requires Board action due to the dollar amount of the purchase; pl ease place this on the September 
7, 2010 agenda.  Thank you for your attention to this matter.  
Barbara A. Fowler, Ph.D., Superintendent of Schools
