Dear Community Member, 
I have been a resident of Troy for 35 years and had five children attend Troy Schools.  Now, I 
have six grandchildren who are (or will be) educated by Troy Schools.  I moved to Troy, just as 
many others have, for the outstanding schools.  I believe we all share the pride of the excellent 
reputation of our schools. 
However, as with everything else, our schools are aging and need upgrades that the general 
operating budget is unable to provide.  Our newest elementary school in Troy is 30 years old.  
Some schools have leaking roofs, broken sidewalks, and windows that need replacement.  Most 
of the schools need to upgrade technology resources to keep pace with today’s educational 
requirements.  We, as proud residents of Troy, need to do our part to continue to have Troy 
Schools remain the best. 
A bond proposal for the schools will be on the November 5th ballot.  I urge every resident to 
become informed about this bond proposal.  It is not an increase to our current tax millage 
rate.  It cannot be used for salaries, pensions, administration, or operating costs.  It is specifically 
used for building upgrades, technology and bus purchases.  It allows the school district to borrow 
money to do these things, just as you may need to borrow money to replace the roof on your own 
home.  Your property taxes will not go up from what you are currently paying. 
I am a conservative and normally oppose these kinds of proposals.  However, even conservatives 
realize that necessary spending is required and that aging buildings need maintenance, and 
technology resources need upgrading, to maintain the excellence we all enjoy.   
Whether you have children or grandchildren currently in school or not, let us all share the pride of 
creating a wonderful community for generations to come and help our school district maintain its 
excellence. 
If you need further details about the bond, please visit the District’s website at 
www.troy.k12.mi.us.  
Cordially, 
Daniel Fair
