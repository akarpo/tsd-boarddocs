eSchools is the system Troy currently uses for its student management system, and it was recently 
acquired by PowerSchool.  PowerSchool has an SMS package that is widely used across the State of 
Michigan representing over 735,000 students/455 districts.  All districts in Macomb County utilize 
PowerSchool, as well as four districts in Oakland County (Birmingham, Lake Orion, Oxford, and West 
Bloomfield).  In comparison, eSchools currently represents 42,000 students/11 districts in Michigan.  We 
are planning to migrate to PowerSchool for several reasons including: 
1. Staff/Parent concerns with eSchools 
2. Ability to collaborate with neighboring districts on reports, training, integrations, etc. 
3. On-Line registration and forms 
4. Single sign-on for parents to access student information items such as Schoology, payments, 
attendance, report cards, etc. 
5. Improved Parent/Teacher Portals (HAC/TAC) 
Demonstrations have been held for administrative staff and the staff responsible for secondary 
scheduling.  The feedback from those that attended the demonstrations has been positive.   
Timeline 
It is planned to have the initial set-up complete in early January, so that tasks that occur second 
semester in preparation for the upcoming school year can be built in the new system (i.e. kindergarten 
round-up, secondary scheduling, elementary scheduling, etc.).   
Teacher gradebook training will take place for tech chairs in advance so a train the trainer model can be 
used to get all teachers training as they return for the new year.  
Administrative training will be scheduled throughout the second semester of the 2017/18 school year 
and during the summer with a plan to deliver training on specific functions as the need to complete a 
specific function occurs.  This will ensure that staff are able to implement and practice the new 
tasks/processes learned.  
For the start of the 2018/19 school year, Troy will be completely live on PowerSchool. Communication 
will be made with parents in a timely manner so that they can set up accounts in the parent portal and 
teachers will have training available as they return for the start of the school year.
