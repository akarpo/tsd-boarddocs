2.   RECOGNITION  
A. PAULA FLEMING – BOARD SERVICE 
Tonight, we have an opportunity to honor the commitment and dedication of a 
woman who has given so much of herself to the Troy School District.  Paula 
Fleming finished her 12 th year of service on the Troy Board of Education in June  
2012, and we would like to show our appreciation for this accomplishment. 
Throughout Paula’s 12 years  on the Board , she has been a tireless cheerleader 
for the Troy School District community.  She has brought passion, expertise and 
high expectations to every decision that has affected this community and its 
children.  Paula has worked hard to bring the entire Troy com munity together to 
build a district that is recognized for excellence at both the state and national 
levels.  
In Paula’s past year as trustee on the Board, the district has celebrated 
significant accomplishments in the classroom and  in academic, artistic and 
athletic competitions , nationwide. Our students have seen their experiments 
conducted at the International Space station. Our bands and orchestras have 
performed for thousands of people at venues across our country.  And, under the 
leadership of Paula and the rest of the Board , the entire district has  increased 
the number of NUMATS Midwest Talent Search winners, National Merit Semi -
finalists, and the number of students taking and pass ing A dvanced Placement 
courses.     
It is true that the strength of o ur district is the quality of the staff, students, 
parents and community that make up the Troy School District team.  Tonight, 
however, we want to make sure that at least one of our key players is 
recognized for her tireless efforts to enhance the opportunities for the children of 
our community.  Paula, we are proud to present you with this specially engraved 
chair, a token of appreciation for your years of service.
