A.  TSD Mission Moment - Facilities Study Attendance Area  
                                        Review Team 
This year, the Troy School District formed a Facilities Study Team 
Attendance Area Review Committee to conduct a comprehensive review 
of current Troy School District attendance area patterns according to 
specific criteria . They were asked to look into resolving double split 
learning pathways, enrollment across buildings to address overcrowding 
and imbalances within the classroom, and to minimize changes to school 
attendance areas across Troy.  This team was made up of members of 
the community and our Pupil Accounting Coordinator.  The individuals 
volunteered and gave countless hours of their time and expertise to this 
important project. Tonight, we are pleased to recognize the members of 
the Facilities Study Attendance Area Review Team for their exemplary 
service. We thank them for their thoughtful and skilled analysis and the 
recommendations they shared that will help guide us as the District 
moves forward.   
We would also like to recognize and thank Deputy Superintendent for 
Teaching & Learning, Mark Dziatczak, and Assistant Superintendent, 
Elementary Instruction, Kris Griffor who were the administrative liaisons 
for this team.  The District could not have undertaken a comprehensive 
study like this without their leadership and direction.

 D.  National Teacher Appreciation Week 
Teacher Appreciation Week is observed nationally each year in May. This year, 
Teacher Appreciation Week was celebrated May 7th – May 11th.  At tonight’s 
meeting, the Troy School District and Board of Education would like to 
recognize all of our outstanding teachers by adopting the following resolution: 
WHEREAS, throughout Troy and across the country, teachers inspire, 
nurture and open children’s minds to new ideas,  
WHEREAS, teachers promote and strengthen the principles of democratic 
equality, develop leaders and encourage critical thinking; and 
WHEREAS, Troy teachers set the bar for excellence in education and 
continually exceed the highest standards in the profession; and 
WHEREAS, the goal of Teacher Appreciation Week is to raise national and 
local awareness of teaching excellence in building a better society; now,  
THEREFORE BE IT  RESOLVED, that the Troy School District  supports 
National Teacher Appreciation Week, May 7-11, 2018, and encourages our 
schools and community to appropriately recognize the vital and changing role 
of teachers in imagining and creating a better future for society.
