imposing a summer tax levy to collect those summer taxes.

in which a local school district or city is concurrently imposing a summer tax levy, 
a copy of this Board’s resolution requesting that each such city agree to coll ect 
the summer tax levy for 20 14 in the amount specified in this resolution.  Such 
forwarding of the resolution and the request to collect the summer tax levy shall 
be sent so that they are received by the appropriate governi ng bodies before 
January 1, 2014.

which the District is located for the reasonable expenses for collection of the 
District’s summer tax levy that the city may bill und er MCLA 380.1612.  Any such 
proposed agreement shall be brought before this Board for its approval or 
disapproval.
