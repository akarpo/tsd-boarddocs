Subject:  Video Surveillance System Software Upgrade 
As you are aware, the District collaborates with the City of Troy for its video surveillance system.  The 
City needs to update its system to Pelco’s latest VideoXpert software platform, and as a result the 
District also needs to update its software.  The current Pelco software that is utilized by the City and 
District has been discontinued by Pelco, and an end of support announcement is expected in the future.  
The City wants to ensure that it remains on a supported platform and desires several of the features 
available in the new software.  
At the time of the City upgrade, the District‘s system will be converted to a standalone Pelco VideoXpert 
Enterprise system.  The City will repurpose some of its existing hardware to provide the District the 
hardware necessary for this configuration and software update.  After the update, the District will 
manage its own system on the updated platform, with the City retaining access to view District cameras.  
There are several benefits to the District as part of this update which improve access to footage at the 
building level and provide better search capabilities.  With the current configuration, footage can only 
be accessed from a few specific computers in the District, and access to footage is slow due to the 
connection speed between City and District.  The updated software and configuration allows the Pelco 
client to be installed on computers anywhere on the District network, which provides greater access to 
Safe-Ed and building administration. 
The cost for the District portion of this project is $44,682, for the software, licensing, and associated 
installation services.  There are no additional annual license fees, and all current hardware is fully 
supported with the new software.  The joint proposal for this update is attached for your review.
