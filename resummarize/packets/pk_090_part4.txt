A. CONSIDERATION:   Schools of Choice - Section 105c 
     9th Grade for 2017 – 2018  
In accordance with Michigan state law, school districts shall pass a resolution 
each school year if they intend to participate in a Schools of Choice program.  It 
is anticipated that seats will be available for the 2017-2018 school year for grade 
9 at the International Academy East.  It would be appropriate at this time to 
consider adopting a Schools of Choice section 105c resolution for 90 non-resident 
9th grade students who attend a district in another county, if that intermediate 
school district is contiguous with the Oakland Intermediate School District 
boundaries.
