 1. Section 624 of the Revised Sch ool Code, as amended, requires the 
intermediate school board to submit its proposed budget no later than 
May 1 of each year to the board of each constituent district for review; 
and

district shall review the proposed in termediate school district budget, 
shall adopt a board resolution expressing its support for or disapproval 
of the proposed intermediate school district budget, and shall submit 
to the intermediate school board an y specific objections and proposed 
changes the constituent district board has to the budget.

intermediate school district budget  in accordance with Section 624 of

responsible for its own operations, in cluding adoption of its budget.

District’s budget is for the sole pu rpose of complying with Section 624 
of the Revised School Code.

 CONSIDERATION:  TEA CONTRACT RATIFICATION 
A tentative agreement between representatives of the Troy Board of 
Education and the Troy Education Association/MEA/NEA (TEA) was reached 
on May 13, 2011.  The TEA membership  voted to ratify the agreement on 
May 17, 2011.  The tentative agreemen t is a two-year contract for the 
period of August 15, 2011, throug h June 30, 2013.  The tentative 
agreement provides no salary sche dule increase for the 2011/2012 and 
2012/2013 school years; a salary schedule step freeze for the 2011/2012 
and 2012/2013 school years; and cost -sharing between TSD and the TEA 
of the total compensation for the fu ll-time release TEA President.  The 
teachers’ insurance plan, MESSA Choice s, will be changed to include the 
addition of a $300/$600 in-network  deductible, $10/$40 prescription 
drug card, and $10 office visit co-pays effective no later than July 1, 2011.  
In addition, the maximum annual amount the District will pay toward a 
teacher’s total MESSA PAK (medical, pres cription drug, vision, dental, life, 
and long-term disability) insurance premium for both the 2011/2012 and 
2012/2013 school years will be as follows: $6,500 (single coverage), 
$14,600 (two-person coverage), and $16, 250 (full family coverage).  Any 
remaining premium cost for the MESSA PAK coverage will be paid by the 
teachers electing such coverage on  a pre-tax basis through payroll 
deduction.
