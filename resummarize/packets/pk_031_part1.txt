C.  Oakland County Outstanding Teacher of the Year Awards  
The Troy School District has many outstanding teachers on its staff. This evening we 
are proud to recognize the accomplishments of six individuals who were nominated 
for the Troy School District Outstanding Teacher Award Program.  
The six nominees represent exemplary educators at every level and were nominated 
by their peers, by parents and by former and current students, all of whom talked 
about the impact these teachers have on the lives of those around them. Their 
nomination lett ers described how the professionals we honor here tonight help 
students find their voices, their passion and their love for learning.  
Would you please come forward as your name is called? (Distribute 
certificates/plants.) 
Two of our district nominees ar e sent to Oakland Schools to represent Troy in the 
county-wide outstanding teacher award program. Due to the criteria set by Oakland 
Schools, Mark Cavataio , Physical Education teacher at Wass Elementary School 
was nominated at the elementary level; Susan P ickering, Media Specialist from 
Smith Middle School was nominated at the middle school level.  Mrs. Pickering was 
also selected as the District’s overall Teacher of the Year and awarded a free one -
year lease of a car, donated through the generosity of the Suburban Collection.  The 
two candidates were chosen by a TEA selection committee to represent the District.

words.

D. Troy Foundation for Educational Excellence  
This evening we are pleased to recognize the Troy Foundation for 
Educational Excellence and the work they do to support our schools, 
our students and our staff. The Foundation awards $500 grants to help 
fund projects that support the curriculum in every school in the T roy 
School District. This year, the Foundation was able to fund two rounds 
of grants, one in the fall and one in the spring, for a total of 110 
separate grants. That kind of support is invaluable. 
The Foundation is made up of volunteers who give of their time and 
talents to raise money and support innovative projects that enrich 
student learning. Again, these projects take place in every school in 
our District and many would not be possible without the resources the 
Foundation provides. 
At this time, I would like to introduce  Mike Cianciolo, President of the 
Troy Foundation for Educational Excellence, who will briefly  recognize 
the most recent group of grant recipients and the many diverse ways 
they are enhancing the education of our Troy School District students.  
RECOMMENDED ACTION: Karen Rockwell will roll names of recipients/projects 
on screen as Mr. Cianciolo speaks.
