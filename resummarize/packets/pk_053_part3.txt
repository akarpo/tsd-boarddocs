B.   CONSIDERATION:  MASB Superintendent Search Contract Approval  
At the Tuesday, January 7, 2014,  Special Board Workshop,  the Board met with 
representatives from Michigan Association o f School Boards (MASB) to discuss the search 
for a new superintendent. MASB offers a service to assist local boards of education  in 
conducting custom-designed searches for a new superintendent.  Each search is 
individualized to meet the needs of the Board at a cost below other market rates.  The 
Troy Board would like to enter into a service contract with MASB to support the district as 
follows: 
 Supply Troy School District with trained, experienced and professional consultant to 
assist and guide Board throu gh all stages of the search and selection process 
professionally and efficiently; 
 Develop individualized process of search to best match needs of the Board by 
handling all details and advising Board of its options; 
 Enlist staff, parents and community members for selection criteria as desired; 
 Coordinate the entire process  - collecting information on candidates, keeping 
records and maintaining contact with educational agencies nationwide, university 
placement centers, educational journals and webpages; 
 Advise Board in every facet of selection process, including reaching consensus on 
decisions that must be made during search. 
RECOMMENDED ACTION:  Adopt the following resolution.

   Moved by  
   Supported by
