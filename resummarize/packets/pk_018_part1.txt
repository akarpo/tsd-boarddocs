2. RECOGNITION 
A.  Oakland County Outstanding Teacher of the Year Awards  
The Troy School District has many outstanding teachers on its staff. This 
evening we are proud to recognize t he accomplishments of sixteen  individuals 
who were nominated for the Troy School District  Outstanding Teacher Award 
Program. 
The sixteen nominees represent exemplary educators at every level  and were 
nominated by their peers, by parents and by former and current students , all 
of whom talked about the impact these teachers have on the lives of those 
around them. Their nomination letters described how the professionals we 
honor here tonight help students find their voices, their passion and their love 
for learning. Perhaps the best tribu te came from a former student of one of 
the nominees who said “I will carry with me the lessons I learned from this 
teacher everywhere I go.” 
Would you please come forward as your name is called? (Distribute 
certificates/plants.) 
Three of our district no minees are sent to Oakland Schools to represent Troy 
in the county -wide Outstanding Teacher Award Program. Due to the criteria 
set by Oakland Schools, Caryn Leonard, fourth-grade teacher at Barnard 
Elementary School was nominated at the elementary level; Ewa Ostrowski , 
ESL teacher from Smith Middle School and Trevor Smith, Physics teacher at 
Troy High School was nominated at the high school level.  Mrs. Leonard was 
also selected as the district’ s overall Teacher of the Year and awarded a free 
one-year lease of a car, donated through the generosity of the Suburban 
Collection.  The three candidates were chosen by a TEA selection committee 
to represent the District.   
At this time, I would like to invite Mrs. Leonard, Mrs. Ostrowski and Mr. Smith 
to say a few words.
