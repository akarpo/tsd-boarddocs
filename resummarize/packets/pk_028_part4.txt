B. T.R.O.Y. Elementary Fitness Program  
The Elementary Physical Education Department is committed to instilling 
in all students the skills, fitness and positive character traits that lead to a 
healthy, active lifestyle -for life.  They are proud to share with you a new 
fitness program they have d eveloped called T.R.O.Y. (Teachers 
Recognizing Outstanding Youth) Fitness Program.  This program focuses 
on students challenging themselves to continually increase their personal 
fitness level rather than comparing themselves to others.  This new 
program w ill replace the Presidential Physical Fitness Award in which 
fourth and fifth grade students have traditionally participated.  The 
physical education teachers have found that the Presidential Physical 
Fitness Award only motivated a small percentage of thei r students and 
feel the T.R.O.Y Fitness Program will motivate all students.

Choice, Section 105 resolution for nonresident students who attend a school 
district within Oakland County .  We would advertise for 100 kindergarten
