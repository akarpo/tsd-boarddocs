DRAFT TSD COMMUNICATION PLAN 
2014/2015   
GOAL: The goal of this plan is to enhance our communication methods, vehicles and avenues to 
reach the maximum number of stakeholders within the Troy School District as well as the greater 
Troy community and beyond in a thoughtful, clear and efficient manner. Doing so will help 
position TSD going forward as a lighthouse district both nationally and internationally as well as 
boost internal and external communication. 
BACKGROUND: The TSD has countless great stories to tell, from student achievement to 
outstanding staff and programming initiatives. We are the “success story” of exemplary U.S. 
public education and our students, employees and citizens would all see tangible benefits in 
further promoting that success. It is also important that the District share critical information 
regarding budgeting, legislative impact and strategic planning to better engage our community in 
the process. This plan will allow us to anticipate upcoming issues and prepare people with 
necessary background on key areas before crises arise.  
The plan would also “introduce” the new Superintendent, Dr. Richard Machesky, and provide 
the opportunity for fresh methods of communication (both one-way and two-way), which would 
enhance the relationship between the superintendent’s office and various stakeholders, including 
staff, parents and students.  
EXISTING COMMUNICATION TOOLS: 
 School Messenger: This tool is an extremely effective way to reach parents, students and 
staff for both immediate/critical communications as well as e-blasts, newsletters, etc. The 
downside is that it ONLY reaches our TSD community. We pay per student and cannot 
add additional community members who wish to keep up to date on the district, so we 
post all newsletters on the TSD website for public consumption. 
 Social Media: The number of fans on the TSD Facebook page has increased more than 
100% and the number of Twitter followers has more than tripled in the past 12 months. 
The users who visit, comment and retweet are more engaged and active than any time in 
the last five years. Continuing to build and develop these audiences is important to 
reaching a key demographic that may not regularly read email or are inundates from 
messages using more “traditional” means.  
 TSD Website: The TSD website and individual school websites provide both static 
information (school start/end times, registration information, building contact 
information) and “news items” (updated consistently during the school year, less 
151
frequently during the summer months.) As measured by page hits, this is the go-to 
vehicle for items such as the lunch menus, email addresses and stationary information, 
but is utilized less so for news and events. Proposed change: Replace “News” section 
with Key Information/Links Section and embed a twitter feed along the right side of the 
front page (where the “links” currently reside) where people can see the most up-to-date 
information, without adding to the work load by duplicating efforts. Photo gallery on the 
front page would remain because it provides visual interest and feedback tells us 
parents/staff like to see student photos prominently displayed. A new content 
management system and website overhaul is being considered for the 2015-16 school 
year.   
 TSD E-blasts: This is a bi-weekly e-newsletter that lists events, activities and 
achievements by students and staff. Although helpful in disseminating information 
(especially in regards to student fundraisers), this vehicle takes a tremendous amount of 
time for limited readership (the “list” format does not lend itself to a visually friendly 
read.) Proposed change: Sending out link to online version of School Life (see below) 
and encouraging TSD families to connect with us electronically on Twitter and 
Facebook.    
 Troy Times Staff Newsletter: This vehicle is well read and consistently used by 
employees to get the word out regarding items for sale, births and staff-only discounts 
and offers.  
 Vimeo: This is our video-hosting site for videos produced by TS-TV. It allows us to put 
hours of sporting events, fine arts performances and classroom videos online for a very 
low costs. We send links out to parents and have a direct link on the TSD website. 
 Superintendent Welcome: This has traditionally been a one-time video message sent out 
to welcome staff back in August. Proposed change: Regular, monthly podcasts produced 
by TS-TV on subjects of importance to TSD staff and community (student testing, budget 
issues, legislative issues, school safety—as well as current events.) See below. 
 Earned Media: The TSD maintains an excellent relationship with local newspaper, radio, 
TV and online media. Continuing to cultivate that strong connection and rapport is 
critical to credibility and the opportunity to help shape external messaging. 
 Paid Media: The TSD buys a limited amount of ad space in weekly, daily and monthly 
publications to advertise for Schools of Choice (K-2, Baker Middle School and IA East.) 
Radio spots are occasionally purchased for IA East as well. The strategic placement of ad 
dollars has been extremely successful in bringing in applications for SOC. 
 Personal visits/meetings: Arguably one of the most important communication vehicles. 
The Superintendent would maintain a calendar of visits to PTO/PTA meetings, 
community/civic organizations, staff meetings, athletic and fine arts events, etc. 
NEW COMMUNICATION TOOLS/INITIATIVES: 
152
 School Life newspaper: This joint project with School Life publications will provide our 
parents, students, staff and the greater Troy community with a professionally done, hard 
copy monthly newspaper filled with great stories about the Troy School District, as well 
as a compiled district calendar, menus and photo pages. Every student in the TSD would 
be given a copy to take home and newsstands would be placed around Troy for 
community members to pick up a free copy. This is produces at no cost to the TSD (it is 
advertiser supported) though the communications department will be busy editing copy 
and proofing stories before they hit print. 
 Superintendent’s Column: This is a monthly “Message from the Superintendent” that 
would appear in each issue of School Life. The topic/content could directly coincide with 
the monthly podcast or occasionally be completely unique. Some columns would reflect 
directly the content of that month’s School Life (i.e. Dr. Machesky could comment on 
achievements covered in the newspaper.) 
 Superintendent’s Podcasts: These would be monthly video messages produced by TS-
TV on subjects of importance to TSD staff and community (student testing, budget 
issues, legislative issues, school safety—as well as current events.) The format would be 
Q & A with students from various broadcasting classes serving as the interviewer for 
each podcast. Topics would follow a calendar (listed below) that allows for substitution 
and/or additional podcasts if current events dictate a need. 
 OneTroy Doing More Videos (working title): These videos would highlight a 
contribution an individual staff member (or group of staff) is doing that inspires, teaches, 
reaches and nurtures students in an extraordinary way. These videos would also show the 
power of our collective work and the impact it has on our students. They would be 
loosely modeled after this videos, produced by the Ottawa Area Schools: 
http://doingmoretogether.org/stories/  
 Weekly Staff Emails from Superintendent: These are simple, straightforward, casual and 
conversational—just a message each week from Dr. Machesky on something that struck 
him in a building visit, a word of thanks, a congratulations and reminder that we all work 
in a phenomenal visit because of the people who make TSD great. Scheduled to go out 
either every Friday or Monday.
