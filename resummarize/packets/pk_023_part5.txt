                      Contract Extension                      
On June 15, 2004, the Board of Education approved a contract for diesel fuel for the 
2004/05 school year.  In July 2011, we contacted RKA Petroleum Company to extend the 
contract through June 30, 2012 with the same terms.  RKA Petroleum has agreed to 
again extend the terms of this contract.  
Note that this contract is based on the lowest profit margin per gallon (fixed for contract 
period).  Please note that our price lock continues to be lower than that obtained by the 
Macomb/Oakland Fuel Consortium.  Our cost per gallon per delivery/profit i s $0.0045 for 
regular diesel vs. $0.0165 for the Macomb/Oakland Consortium.  Estimated annual usage 
of 160,000 gallons.  Actual contract will change based on market conditions and fuel 
usage. 
In follow-up with RKA Petroleum, I was told that our pricing is  grandfathered under the 
old contract.  Considering that we are being offered a lower price than the Consortium , 
it appears to be in our best interest to continue this contract.  
Accordingly, I recommend continuation of this bid as follows:  
 RKA Petroleum Company Initial Estimate $385,000
