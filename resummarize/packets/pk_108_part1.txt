A. TSD Mission Moment – Wass Elementary School Armed Forces  
                          Student Lesson 
Captain Sollars is a Wass Parent and currently serves our country as a Flight 
Nurse in the United States Military.  Her service requires her to take tours out of 
the country for long periods of time.   Recently, she shared a presentation 
informing third graders at Wass of  her role in the United States Military a nd 
teaching the Wass students about the importance and purpose of Veterans Day.   
Principal Matt Jansen will give a brief introduction and invite Captain Sollars and 
her daughter Abigail up to the podium to share more about this valuable lesson.  
RECOMMENDED ACTION: Thank Captain Sollars for her service to our country 
and for reaching out to our students at Wass Elementary.  Present certificates to 
the Captain and her daughter Abigail .
