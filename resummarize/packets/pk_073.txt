 CONSIDERATION:  TESPA Contract Ratification 
A tentative agreement between representatives of the Troy Board of Education and 
the Troy Educational Support Personnel  Association/MEA/NEA (TES PA) was 
reached on June 5th , 201 5, and the  TESPA membership , comprised of  
paraprofessionals and skilled -trades employees, voted to ratify the agreement  on 
June 10th, 2015.  The new one (1) year contract is for the period commencing July 
1, 2015 and ending June 30, 2016.  The agreement includes a 1% increase in the 
existing salary schedule, step movement for all eligible members, 4% off -schedule 
payment to members on the tier 1 schedule who are currently on step 5 or higher.   
The agreement includes additional language changes which do  not have significant 
monetary impact.

 RE: TESPA Tentative Agreement 
         DATE:    Friday, June 12, 2015  
We are pleased to announce that a Tentative Agreement has been reached with TESPA.   The TESPA 
agreement is recommended for Board action at the June 16th, 2015 meeting since member ratification 
has occurred.   
The agreement allows for a net increase (new dollars over 2014/2015) of less the 1.5%.  This is due to 
changes in the pay structure which is explained below and outlined in the attached signed Tentative 
Agreement.  
The agreement provides modest improvements to the salary structure, while retaining portions of the 
increase as off-schedule (non-compounding) payments.  A clear understanding is also expressed in 
the agreement that such payments are for 2015/16 only and not to be considered as a basis or starting 
point for a successor contract.   This will help the district retain some flexibility if the district’s 
financial condition requires modifications next year.   
The total dollar net increase over 2014/15 is approximately $30,000.  This includes the following 
economics: 
 1% across-the-Board increase to the existing salary schedule, including longevity steps. 
 Step movement for all eligible members. 
 4% off-schedule payment for employees on the tier 1 schedule (those hired prior to February 
14, 2012) who are currently on step 5 or higher. 
 Premium costs for additional $20,000 in life insurance coverage for the 5 skilled trade 
employees. 
 $200 reimbursement for work boots for the 5 skilled trade employees. 
Please contact either Larry Lobert or Rick West if you have questions, comments, or concerns 
regarding this agreement.
