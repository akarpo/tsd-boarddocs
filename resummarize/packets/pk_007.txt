Re: RF microphone replacement 
The District is required to replace many pieces of wireless microphone equipment due to FCC action 
prohibiting the use of certain radio frequencies that have been i n public use for many years.  Troy School 
District has many pieces of equipment affected by this ban.  We have contacted our federal legislators to 
request waivers and/or financial assistance in funding this unexpected and unplanned project, to no avail – 
schools across the country are in the same situation and the FCC has confirmed that there are no exceptions, 
extensions, or financial support.  Therefore, the District released bid # 9684 on June 21, 2010, with bids 
opened on July 6.  We received 21 valid responses and one fax response that was rejected per District policy.   
I have inspected the low bid response from Videotape Products, Inc. of Burbank CA, and have spoken with 
the vendor.  As an equipment-only bid, I have no reason to believe that the ven dor will have difficulty 
completing delivery of the equipment, and replacements will be completed in -house.  I will work with you 
after the project is complete to review options for disposing of the existing equipment and possibly 
recovering some funds, although since it is no longer legal to operate it, our choices may be limited.  
I am recommending award of Bid#9684 to Videotape Productions, Inc. in the amount of $24,623.05 to be 
paid with capital project funds.  This item requires Board action due to the  dollar amount of the purchase; 
please place this on the July 13, 2010 agenda.  Thank you for your attention to this matter.

of one-half of school property taxes, including d ebt service, beginning with 20 11, and 
continuing from year to year until specifically revoked by this Board of Education, and 
requests each city in which this district is located and in which a loca l school district or 
city is concurrently imposing a summer tax levy to collect those summer taxes. 
 2.  The Superintendent or his/her designee is authorized and directed to 
forward to the governing body of each city in which this district is located and in which a 
local school district or city is concurrently imposing a summer tax levy, a copy of this 
Board’s resolution requesting that each such city agree to coll ect the summer tax levy 
for 2011 in the amount specified in this resolution.  Such forwarding  of the resolution 
and the request to collect the summer tax levy shall be sent so that they are received by 
the appropriate governing bodies before January 1, 2011. 
 3.  The Superintendent or his/her designee is authorized and directed to 
negotiate on be half of this District with the governing body of each city in which the 
District is located for the reasonable expenses for collection of the District’s summer tax 
levy that the city may bill under MCLA 380.1612.  Any such proposed agreement shall be 
brought before this Board for its approval or disapproval. 
 4.  All resolutions and parts of resolutions insofar as they conflict with the 
provisions of this resolution be and the same are hereby rescinded.
