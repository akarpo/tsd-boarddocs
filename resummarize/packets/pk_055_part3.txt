Memorandum 
TO:  Dr. Fowler, Superintendent 
FROM: Mark Washington, Director of Technology 
RE: E-Rate/USF Bid Recommendations 
DATE: February 27, 2014 
Each year, the district submits a request through the Universal Services Fund Program allowing 
companies to submit competitive bids on telecommunications and internet services. Our E-
Rate/USF consultant Convergent Technology of Flint, Michigan, analyzes the submission and 
presents the district with a summary of those companies that chose to respond.  
Recommendation: 
For Primary Rate Interface (PRI-Phone Lines) a recommendation of continuing with Windstream 
is being submitted for an annual cost of $4361.40.  While they were not the low bidder, the cost 
to move the lines to another provider would be at least $1500 in fees and labor.  Windstream has 
provided the district solid performance and the savings is not enough reason to change 
companies.  
For Web Hosting, a recommendation to continue with Blackboard (formerly known as Edline) at 
an annual cost of $30853.16.  Blackboard currently provides an interface to the district’s grading 
program as well as the primary provider of our current website format.  While they are the 
second highest bidder, the cost of changing our grading system and all of the district public 
websites would be prohibitive.   
For Long Distance Service, a recommendation to continue with AT&T is being made as they 
were the only company to respond.  Their cost for service is $.0245/minute. 
For Cell Phone provider, Sprint is being recommended to continue the contract from the state bid 
for Smartphones at $49.99/month.  While Verizon has a lower monthly cost by $1.92/line per 
month, in order for the district to switch, we would have to break the current contract.  The early 
termination fees being billed by Sprint would exceed the value of the savings from the monthly 
charges.    
Attached please find detailed summary of the bid responses as received by Convergent 
Technology.
