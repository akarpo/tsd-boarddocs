2.   RECOGNITION  
A. Years of Dedicated Service to the Troy Board of Education -  
Ida Edmunds and Wendy Underwood 
This evening, in the Recognition portion of our meeting, we would like to take a moment 
to recognize two of our own Board members. Wendy Underwood and Ida Edmunds are 
retiring from the Troy Board of Education and this evening  is their final meeting. It’s 
hard to describe the impact these two trustees have had on our community, but it has 
been tremendous. Wendy is leavi ng us after selflessly giving more than 11 years of 
service to our students, staff and families. Ida is retiring after dedicating more than 31 
years of her time, talents and passion into learning for all.  
Aside from being an elected position, m ost of you know that serving on the Board of 
Education is also a volunteer position, and it requires a great deal of time, effort and 
commitment. These two exemplary trustees have done a remarkable service to past 
generations and future generations of Troy students. We know this is not goodbye, and 
they will no doubt be active in the greater Troy community …. but on this Board, will 
miss them. 
Tonight, we have several declarations to read in recognition of these two trustees.
