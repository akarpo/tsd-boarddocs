shared a presentation with the Board on Common Core 
Standards in the Troy Sch ool District.  She explained that 
governors and state superintendents began the 
development of common core standards for grades K – 12 
in ELA and mathematics  due to the disparate standards 
across states,  student mobility, global competition, and 
the fact that today’s jobs require different skills .  She 
explained the importance of these standards in providing 
educators, parents, and students with clea r, focused 
guideposts and to prepare students with knowledge and 
skills needed for success in college and wo rk.  All states 
except Alaska and Texas supported the initial development 
of these standards and 35 states, including Michigan, have 
officially adopted the standards as of September 1, 2010.   
She discussed some of the common core standards for 
English Lan guage Arts and mathematics along with the 
next steps we are taking as a District to work  towards 
being compliant with the State’s initiatives.  She indicated 
that steps have been taken to begin to set benchmarks in 
elementary grades so as to identify students earlier to see 
if they are on track  for their grade level.  She reported 
that the NCA committee met to discuss these 
requirements and are working to enhance our Title I 
staffing support  in order to target those students who 
need supplemental instruction.
