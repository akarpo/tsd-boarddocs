1. ELECTION OF ACTING CHAIRPERSON 
The first order of business is to e lect an acting chairperson who will 
conduct the meeting until such time as a president has been elected.  
Past practice has been that the previous year’s president conducts this 
part of the meeting. 
2. APPOINTMENT OF TEMPORARY SECRETARY 
 The second order of business is for the acting chairperson  to 
 appoint Judy Holmes to act as temporary secretary until such time as the 
 secretary to the Board has been elected. 
3. ELECTION OF PRESIDENT 
The acting chairperson declares nominations in order for the office o f 
president of the board of education (please note, nominations need not 
be seconded; and a motion and support are made to close the 
nomination.)  Please understand that four votes are required for election. 
4. ELECTION OF VICE PRESIDENT 
The newly elected  president declares nominations in order for the 
office of vice president, following the same procedures as outlined for the 
election of president. 
5. ELECTION OF A SECRETARY 
The president declares nominations in order for the office of secretary, 
following the same procedures as outlined for the election of president. 
6. APPOINTMENT OF TREASURER 
According to Board Policy, the treasurer is appointed annually by the 
Board as a nonvoting officer.  The assistant superintendent of business 
services has served  as treasurer of the Board.  It is my recommendation 
that this procedure continue.

5.  RECOGNITION       
A.    Board of Education Recognition 
Each January, the Troy School District recognizes and celebrates the 
work of a very important group of individuals: The Board of 
Education.  
Troy is truly fortunate to be governed by Board members who  
dedicate an incredible amount of time and energy toward ensuring 
that we provide the best possible educational opportunities for all 
students. You are tireless, professional, and passionate  and this 
community owes you a debt of gratitude for all that you do and all 
that you give. T onight, we would like to recognize our Board of 
Education members with a gift that represents our students and staff 
- the very people you work so hard to serve. 
One of the first initiatives we developed when you selected me to 
lead this district was to develop the idea of ONE TROY —that we are 
not merely a district of schools, but ONE school district, with a single 
mission and a single focus on student achievement for ALL. 
This evening, we are happy to present you each with a ONE Troy 
ensemble, a basket filled with a token of appreciation and pride in 
each of our schools. You will find t -shirts and hats, water bottles and 
mugs, pens and pencils—there are even some baked goods in there, 
each serving as a symbol of one of our outstanding schools. We hope 
that this will serve as a reminder of the impact you have on the l ives 
of our students and staff. 
From our more senior members to our newest trustees, we are 
grateful to have each and every one of you. 
Thank you for all you do.   
Recommended Action:  Present gift to Board members.
