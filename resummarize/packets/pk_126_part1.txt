B.   Troy School District’s Athletics CAP Pact 
The Coach-Athlete-Parent (CAP) Pact is an expectations model that was 
finalized by the Troy Athletic Advisory Committee (TAAC).  TAAC is 
comprised of four high school athletes, four parents of high school athletes, 
four high school coaches, and athletic directors.  Over the course of winter 
and spring meetings, this committee’s collaborative work and efforts 
molded a generic athletics expectations model originally created by 
Growing Great Relationships into our district’s model.  This expectations 
model will now serve to guide how coaches, athletes, and parents interact 
and operate.  The TSD CAP Pact will be implemented in the upcoming fall 
sports season.  Thank you to our TAAC members for their investment in 
the process and development of the CAP Pact.

 E.  National Teacher Appreciation Week 
Teacher Appreciation Week is observed nationally each year in May. This year, 
Teacher Appreciation Week was celebrated May 6th – May 10th.  At tonight’s 
meeting, the Troy School District and Board of Education would like to 
recognize all of our outstanding teachers by adopting the following resolution: 
WHEREAS, throughout Troy and across the country, teachers inspire, 
nurture and open children’s minds to new ideas,  
WHEREAS, teachers promote and strengthen the principles of democratic 
equality, develop leaders and encourage critical thinking; and 
WHEREAS, Troy teachers set the bar for excellence in education and 
continually exceed the highest standards in the profession; and 
WHEREAS, the goal of Teacher Appreciation Week is to raise national and 
local awareness of teaching excellence in building a better society; now,  
THEREFORE BE IT  RESOLVED, that the Troy School District supports 
National Teacher Appreciation Week, May 6-10, 2019, and encourages our 
schools and community to appropriately recognize the vital and changing role 
of teachers in imagining and creating a better future for society.
