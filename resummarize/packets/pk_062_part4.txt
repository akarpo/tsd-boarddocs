Re:  Bond Smart Board Purchases  
After meeting with the Elementary Technology Design Team, a recommendation was made that all 
elementary classrooms serving an average size of 25 students or more should be equipped with a multiple 
touch SmartBoard.  Over the past several years via bond projects, district purchases and PTO 
contributions, SmartBoards have been installed in a majority of the elementary classrooms.   
This recommendation is for $185,012.50 to VSC, Inc for the purchase, installation and configuration of 40 
SmartBoards and 5 Sharp LED Touch TVs for the elementary schools.  This will complete the installation 
of SmartBoards at the elementary schools and match the existing boards to the district standard in all of the 
other elementary classrooms.   
The additional 5 LED TVs will be located on the elementary school stages that are used for instruction.  In 
those places, SmartBoards cannot be mounted on the walls and require a portable unit.  Although Smart 
makes a portable unit with a projector, it requires constant recalibration if the unit is moved or bumped.  
When this frequently happens, it takes away valuable instructional time while recalibrating.  Installation will 
occur this summer. 
These prices are from the REMC Statewide Purchasing Cooperative program.  Attached please find a copy of 
the VSC quote as well as an email from Nancy Corner, REMC Project Director, confirming that pricing is from 
statewide contract and therefore meets individual school district bidding requirements. 
All items recommended for purchase are being expensed from the 2014 Bond Fund.
